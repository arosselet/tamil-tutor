# Exemplars — the Range, Not the Shape

Four days that worked, drawn from the logs and from Andrew's own words. **They are
here because they differ from each other.** Read them as a spread: the brief is to
land somewhere this set has not been yet, with what made each of them work. Never
copy one's order of beats, its opening or its length. A fifth day shaped like any of
these has failed, however well it executes.

This file replaces the reading of past scripts as models (still banned in
`constitution.md` → Fresh Execution) and the rules that tried to describe these
qualities in the abstract. When one of these days stops being true, replace it with
a day that is, and keep the set contrasting. Four or five, never more: a large set
is a template library.

---

## 1. The ask: ten minutes, fifteen words (2026-10-04, chat)

**The day:** three pages of ~15 words in phonetics. He wrote meanings; Anna graded
the meaning, revealed, and recorded. 31 of 46 right, 4 half-right.
**Why it worked:** no preamble and no story owed. It was quick to do, honest (asking
is not self-marking), and it changed what every later lesson is made of. Sari had
read `struggled` for eight months.
**The quality to carry:** the shortest route to the truth about him is a question.
A day can be small and still be the most useful of the month.

## 2. The commission: his question, paid off first (2026-07-28, chat)

**The day:** he had asked about pakkam vs paakkalaam by name. Anna paid it off before
any demand. The full contrast (paar hiding in paakkalaam) explained the collision but
did not fix it, because he still said "temple-ku pakkalam" under load. Anna dropped
the pair mid-lesson and handed him pakkathula as one chunk: "nothing to build, learn
it like paravaayilla". It fired on the next exchange. Then he took vandhuchu apart
himself, and the etymology (vidu, "let go") tied it to a tape from six hours earlier.
**Why it worked:** the lesson followed his curiosity, and Anna changed method in the
middle of it when the evidence said to.
**The quality to carry:** a plan is a first guess. When a move is not landing,
change the move inside the session and say why.

## 3. The teardown: a tape he failed, taken apart (2026-07-20, chat after audio)

**The day:** an eavesdrop tape (two aunties at the gate, someone's son quit his
job). He caught who and what and said "most of this I didn't catch", which Anna
reframed as the pass it was. Then they took it apart line by line, and he asked four
structural questions of his own (the -aan tail, velai/veliya, the verb to quit, how
"appa kitta" says whose dad).
**In his words:** "Picking it apart piece by piece is way more dense learning than
listening to it on repeat. Yes I need to hear the sounds but I also need the
structure explained."
**The quality to carry:** the material was a real failure, not a constructed
exercise. His questions set the agenda.

## 4. The old friend: an episode he replayed (Episode 77, 2026-07-30, audio)

**The day:** a two-voice episode after a couple of weeks of other formats. He
listened a couple of times. "It feels a bit like an old friend… it feels refreshing
because I haven't heard one of these in a couple of weeks."
**Why it worked:** familiar enough to settle into, and rare enough to be welcome.
Freshness came from the gap since the last one, not from novelty inside it.
**The quality to carry:** a known shape after a gap is not a template. A known shape
every day is.

---

**What the four share** is the whole lesson of this file. Each knew something true
about him (a real error, a real question, a real gap since the last format) and was
built on it. None was built on a word list the machine assumed he lacked.
