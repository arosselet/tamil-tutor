# Captions — Ep 68 · The Late Call

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: The dull, rhythmic whir of a ceiling fan. Outside, the steady drone of crickets. A digital clock on the nightstand flips to 3:14 AM. Suddenly, the harsh, jarring ringtone of a cheap smartphone shatters the silence.]*

> **HOST:** *Three fourteen in the morning.*

> **HOST:** *You are completely, heavily asleep.*

> **HOST:** *The phone is vibrating aggressively against the wooden nightstand.*

> **HOST:** *A phone call at this hour is never a casual chat.*

> **HOST:** *It's never just someone checking in.*

> **HOST:** *It's either a wrong number, or a disaster.*

> **HOST:** *You squint at the blinding white screen.*

> **HOST:** *The caller ID says Maama. Your uncle.*

*[SFX: Phone picked up, sheets rustling heavily.]*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *(Groggy, voice thick with sleep) Hello?*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Vanakkam maama.*<br>Hello, uncle.

*[Pause]*

> **MAAMA:** *(Hushed, breathless, as if hiding from something) Seekkiram!*<br>Quickly!

> **MAAMA:** *Enakku phone pannu-nu sonnaen-la?*<br>I told you to call me, didn't I?

> **HOST:** *He skips the pleasantries entirely.*

> **HOST:** *He's whispering, but it's an intense, terrifying whisper.*

> **HOST:** *He uses 'seekkiram' right out of the gate. Quickly.*

> **HOST:** *And he's immediately annoyed that you didn't call him first.*

> **HOST:** *'Enakku phone pannu-nu sonnaen-la?'*

> **HOST:** *I told you to call me, didn't I?*

> **HOST:** *Listen to how he slips that '-nu' in there to quote his own previous instruction.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *(Confused, trying to force his brain to wake up) Call you? I was asleep.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *I don't know what's going on.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Bayam irukku maama...*<br>There is fear, uncle...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Enna prachanai?*<br>What's the problem?

> **HOST:** *You are stating a plain, objective fact about your current state.*

> **HOST:** *'Bayam irukku.' There is fear.*

> **HOST:** *Because uncles don't whisper into the phone at three a.m. for no reason.*

> **HOST:** *You ask the only question that matters. 'Enna prachanai?' What's the problem?*

> **MAAMA:** *We do not have time to discuss the details over the phone.*

> **MAAMA:** *Avasaram irukku.*<br>There is urgency.

> **HOST:** *He says 'Avasaram irukku'... he is in a serious hurry.*

> **HOST:** *'Avasaram' is urgency. A high-stakes emergency.*

> **HOST:** *It exists right now. 'Irukku.'*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *(Rubbing his eyes, pleading) Maama...*<br>Uncle...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Naalaikku varalaama?*<br>May I come tomorrow?

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Thoonga mudiyala.*<br>I can't sleep.

> **HOST:** *You are desperately trying to negotiate your way out of this.*

> **HOST:** *You want to know if you can just handle this tomorrow instead.*

> **HOST:** *'Naalaikku varalaama?' May I come tomorrow?*

> **HOST:** *You use that polite '-laama' ending to ask for permission.*

> **HOST:** *And you give him a very human, biological reason.*

> **HOST:** *'Thoonga mudiyala.'*<br>I can't sleep.

> **HOST:** *I can't sleep. The ability to sleep has been completely disrupted.*

> **MAAMA:** *(Harshly, cutting you off) Mudiyathu.*<br>Cannot.

> **MAAMA:** *Innaikku varanum.*<br>You must come today.

> **MAAMA:** *Petti edunga.*<br>Take the suitcase.

> **HOST:** *He completely rejects your negotiation.*

> **HOST:** *'Mudiyathu.' Cannot. It is not possible.*

> **HOST:** *He brings down the hammer with that '-num' sound of pure obligation.*

> **HOST:** *'Innaikku varanum.' You must come today.*

> **HOST:** *And then he gives you an instruction that makes absolutely no sense.*

> **HOST:** *'Petti edunga.'*<br>Take the suitcase.

> **HOST:** *Take the suitcase. He uses the polite '-nga' ending, but make no mistake, it's a direct order.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *A suitcase? Why do I need a suitcase?*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Are we leaving town?*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Enga poganum?*<br>Where must we go?

> **HOST:** *You throw the obligation right back at him.*

> **HOST:** *Where must we go? 'Enga poganum?'*

> **MAAMA:** *We are leaving right now.*

> **MAAMA:** *Madurai-kku poganum.*<br>We must go to Madurai.

> **HOST:** *He's pulling the trigger: 'Madurai-kku poganum'.*

> **HOST:** *He needs to go to Madurai right now.*

> **HOST:** *That '-kku' sound pins the destination on the map.*

> **HOST:** *To Madurai.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *(Sits bolt upright in bed, heart rate spiking) Madurai? At this hour?*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Sari maama...*<br>Okay uncle...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Appurama pesalaama?*<br>Shall we talk later?

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Naan kilambanum.*<br>I must leave.

> **HOST:** *You accept the terrifying mission. 'Sari.'*

> **HOST:** *But you need to get off the phone so you can actually pack this suitcase.*

> **HOST:** *'Appurama pesalaama?' Shall we talk later?*

> **HOST:** *'Naan kilambanum.' I must leave. More obligation.*

> **MAAMA:** *No. Listen to me very carefully.*

> **MAAMA:** *Time sollu.*<br>Tell me the time.

> **HOST:** *He ignores your request to hang up.*

> **HOST:** *'Time sollu.' Tell me the time. He wants an exact ETA for when you will be ready.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *(Looking wildly at the clock) Give me ten minutes to pack.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Pathu nimisham.*<br>Ten minutes.

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Naan appurama kooppiduraen.*<br>I will call later.

> **HOST:** *You promise him ten minutes. 'Pathu nimisham.'*

> **HOST:** *And you assure him you'll call him back later.*

> **HOST:** *'Naan appurama kooppiduraen.' I will call later.*

> **MAAMA:** *I am waiting outside in the dark.*

> **MAAMA:** *Eppa free-yaa iruppa?*<br>When will you be free?

> **MAAMA:** *Seekkiram kooppidu.*<br>Call quickly.

*[SFX: A sharp, metallic click. The line goes completely dead.]*

> **HOST:** *Wait, he's outside right now?*

> **HOST:** *He just asked when you will be free, 'Eppa free-yaa iruppa?'*

> **HOST:** *And ordered you to call him back quickly. 'Seekkiram kooppidu.'*

> **HOST:** *Then he hung up on you.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *(Staring at the dead screen of the phone, terrified) I thought I was going back to sleep...*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Appurama povaen-nu ninaichaen...*<br>I thought I would go later...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Aana...*<br>But...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Ippo poraen.*<br>Now I am going.

> **HOST:** *You are processing the grim reality of the situation.*

> **HOST:** *You had a future plan. 'Appurama povaen...' I will go later.*

> **HOST:** *'Aaana'... but.*

> **HOST:** *The future is canceled. The dread is happening right now in the present.*

> **HOST:** *'Ippo poraen.' Now, I am going.*

*[SFX: The heavy, slow, metallic zip of a large canvas suitcase being dragged open in the dark.]*

*[SFX: The harsh, immediate roar of an old car engine roaring to life. Tires spin on loose gravel, throwing rocks into the wheel wells, and then settle into the steady, unnerving hum of the highway in the dead of night.]*

> **HOST:** *You are in the passenger seat.*

> **HOST:** *It is pitch black outside.*

> **HOST:** *The trees are flying past the window at a terrifying speed.*

> **HOST:** *Maama is hunched over the steering wheel.*

> **HOST:** *His knuckles are white. His eyes are locked on the road ahead.*

> **HOST:** *The needle on the dashboard is climbing steadily higher.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Maama...*<br>Uncle...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Medhuva ponga.*<br>Go slowly.

*[Pause: 2s]*

> **HOST:** *You are begging him.*

> **HOST:** *'Medhuva ponga.'*<br>Go slowly.

> **HOST:** *Go slow-ly.*

> **HOST:** *You know the word medhu for slow.*

> **HOST:** *But right now, you need it to be an action. A description of how he is driving.*

> **HOST:** *You turn it into an adverb by slapping that -aa sound onto the end of it.*

> **HOST:** *'Medhuva.' Slowly.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Maama. Medhuva ponga.*<br>Uncle. Go slowly.

> **MAAMA:** *Neram aachu.*<br>Time is up.

> **MAAMA:** *Nera poraen.*<br>I am going straight.

> **HOST:** *Time is up. I'm going straight.*

> **HOST:** *He doesn't even look at you. He doesn't blink.*

> **HOST:** *You look out the window, trying to see where you are.*

> **HOST:** *It is too dark. Just endless highway and deep shadows.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Pakkathula...*<br>Nearby...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Enna irukku?*<br>What is there?

*[Pause: 2s]*

> **HOST:** *You're checking your surroundings.*

> **HOST:** *'Pakkathula enna irukku?'*<br>What is nearby?

> **HOST:** *What is nearby?*

> **HOST:** *Pakkathula... in the vicinity. Around us.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Pakkathula enna irukku maama?*<br>What is nearby, uncle?

> **MAAMA:** *(Silence. Just the tense, rigid posture of a man on a desperate mission.)*

> **HOST:** *Your throat is completely dry. Your heart is hammering in your chest.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Konjam thanni kudunga.*<br>Give me some water.

> **HOST:** *Give me a little water.*

> **HOST:** *Notice how you naturally used the polite -nga sound at the end of the action.*

> **HOST:** *'Kudunga.' Give.*

> **HOST:** *Even in absolute terror, you instinctively respect the elders.*

> **MAAMA:** *(Muttering, speaking entirely to himself, completely ignoring you) Namma anna irukkanla...*<br>Our brother is there, right...

> **MAAMA:** *Ennamo prachanai...*<br>Some problem...

> **HOST:** *He's just talking to the dashboard now. The speed of his voice is jarring.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Enna maama?*<br>What, uncle?

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Enakku puriyala.*<br>I don't understand.

> **HOST:** *'Enakku puriyala.'*<br>I do not understand.

> **HOST:** *I do not understand.*

> **HOST:** *A cold, hard fact. You have no idea what is happening or where you are going.*

> **MAAMA:** *(Sighs heavily, grips the wheel tighter) Onnum illa.*<br>Nothing.

> **MAAMA:** *Nethu kalyaanathukku ponom.*<br>Yesterday we went to the wedding.

> **HOST:** *Nothing, he says.*

> **HOST:** *Yesterday, we went to the wedding. 'Ponom.' We went.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Appadiya?!*<br>Is that so?!

> **HOST:** *'Appadiya?!'*

> **HOST:** *Oh really?!*

> **HOST:** *It slips out of you with pure, unfiltered disbelief. You were literally just at the wedding together.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Aana...*<br>But...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Ippo enga porom?*<br>Now where are we going?

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Maama...*<br>Uncle...

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Maama kitta petti irukku...*<br>Uncle has the suitcase...

> **HOST:** *The heavy canvas suitcase.*

> **HOST:** *It is sitting right behind his seat, practically radiating dread.*

> **HOST:** *'Maama kitta petti irukku.'*

> **HOST:** *Uncle has the box.*

> **HOST:** *He ignores your question completely. His mind is somewhere else entirely.*

> **MAAMA:** *Madurai-kku ponom...*<br>We went to Madurai...

> **MAAMA:** *Romba nalla irundhuchu.*<br>It was really good.

*[Pause: 3s]*

> **HOST:** *He slips into the standard day-recap out of absolutely nowhere.*

> **HOST:** *It is completely unsettling.*

> **HOST:** *'Madurai-kku ponom... romba nalla irundhuchu.'*

> **HOST:** *We went to Madurai, it was really good.*

> **HOST:** *Why is he talking about how nice the trip was while driving like a maniac into the darkness?*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Maama... enna prachanai? Sollunga...*<br>Uncle... what is the problem? Tell me...

> **MAAMA:** *Anga ponaa...*<br>If we go there...

> **MAAMA:** *Theriyum.*<br>You will know.

> **HOST:** *'Anga ponaa... theriyum.'*

> **HOST:** *If we go there... you will know.*

> **HOST:** *Notice that '-ndhaa' or '-naa' ending clipped onto the action. The 'if'.*

> **HOST:** *If we go.*

> **HOST:** *He goes back to muttering to the windshield. Fast, breathless, disconnected.*

> **MAAMA:** *Sattai anga irukkaam...*<br>The shirt is there, it seems...

> **MAAMA:** *Avare paathukkaraaraam...*<br>He is looking after it himself, it seems...

> **MAAMA:** *Alaichal.*<br>Exhausting running around.

> **HOST:** *You catch fragments of the gossip frame.*

> **HOST:** *Something about a shirt being there. Someone looking after it himself. The heavy, exhausting word for running-around... alaichal.*

> **HOST:** *None of it makes any sense.*

> **HOST:** *And then, without warning, he slams on the brakes.*

*[SFX: The violent crunch of tires tearing off the pavement and skidding onto a dirt driveway. The headlights cut through the darkness, washing over the towering, jagged facade of a massive, silent old house in the middle of nowhere. The engine dies abruptly.]*

> **HOST:** *The silence after the engine cuts out is deafening.*

> **HOST:** *Maama kills the headlights.*

> **HOST:** *We are sitting in absolute darkness.*

> **HOST:** *Outside the window, just the imposing, shadowy outline of a massive structure.*

> **HOST:** *You step out into the cold air.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Idhu pazhaya veedu.*<br>This is an old house.

> **HOST:** *This is an old house.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Iruttaa irukku.*<br>It is dark.

> **HOST:** *It is incredibly dark.*

> **HOST:** *You squint toward the heavy wooden door.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Paarka mudiyala.*<br>I cannot see.

> **HOST:** *I can't see.*

> **HOST:** *That mudiyala. The inability to do something.*

> **HOST:** *Maama is already marching up the stone steps, gripping the suitcase like a shield.*

> **HOST:** *You hesitate at the threshold.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Ulla varalaama?*<br>May I come inside?

> **HOST:** *You ask permission.*

> **HOST:** *Ulla varalaama?*

> **HOST:** *May I come inside?*

> **HOST:** *That -laama ending gently floating the question.*

> **HOST:** *The heavy wooden door creaks open before Maama even knocks.*

> **HOST:** *Standing in the dim glow of a single yellow bulb is an older woman. Atthai.*

> **ATTHAI:** *Ulla vaanga!*<br>Come inside!

> **HOST:** *Come inside.*

> **HOST:** *Notice the respect marker. The nga.*

> **ATTHAI:** *Ukkaarunga!*<br>Sit!

> **HOST:** *Sit. Ukkaarunga.*

> **HOST:** *You step inside.*

> **HOST:** *The tension is unbearable.*

> **HOST:** *Maama places the heavy suitcase on the ancient wooden table.*

> **MAAMA:** *Petti kondu vandhom.*<br>We brought the suitcase.

> **HOST:** *We brought the box.*

> **HOST:** *The om sound. We did this. We brought it.*

> **HOST:** *Vandhom.*

> **HOST:** *Atthai slowly places her hand on the brass latches.*

> **ATTHAI:** *Paarthaa...*<br>If seen...

> **HOST:** *If seen...*

> **HOST:** *That -ndhaa ending again. The conditional 'if'.*

> **ATTHAI:** *Semmaiyaa irukkum!*<br>It will be excellent!

> **HOST:** *It will be excellent!*

> **HOST:** *You brace yourself and look inside.*

> **HOST:** *Folded meticulously inside the sinister suitcase is a blindingly bright, violently pink silk shirt.*

> **HOST:** *Your brain is misfiring.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Idhu... kalyaana sattai.*<br>This... is a wedding shirt.

> **HOST:** *This... is a wedding shirt.*

> **PROTAGONIST:** *Romba azhagaana sattai!*<br>A very beautiful shirt!

> **HOST:** *You pull that familiar word out of the absurdity.*

> **HOST:** *Azhagaana. Beautiful.*

> **ATTHAI:** *Aama!*<br>Yes!

> **HOST:** *Yes!*

> **ATTHAI:** *Adhaan prachanai-nu sonnaanga.*<br>They said that's the problem.

> **HOST:** *The gossip frames blur past you at full speed.*

> **HOST:** *Adhaan. That's the thing.*

> **MAAMA:** *Seekkiram podu!*<br>Put it on quickly!

> **HOST:** *Seekkiram.*

> **ATTHAI:** *Innum konjam saappidunga...*<br>Eat a little more...
