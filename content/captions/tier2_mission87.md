# Captions — Ep 87 · The Repair Shop

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

[SFX: Sound of a busy, narrow Coimbatore side-street. Motorbike horns, the distant rattle of a shutter, and the thick humidity of a crowded lane.]

> **Host B (M):** You are standing in front of a blue metal door. It is locked. This is supposed to be the repair shop, but there is no sign. Just a dusty lock and a feeling that you’ve been walking in circles.

> **Host A (F):** Then, he appears. A man in a faded shirt, leaning against a pillar, watching you. He smiles, but his eyes stay sharp. Suspicious.

> **Host B (M):** **Thambi, yaarai paakkareenga?**<br>Brother, who are you looking for?

> **Host A (F):** You tell him you’re looking for the shop owner. He nods slowly, like he knows a secret.

> **Host B (M):** **Oh, andha kadaiya? Avaru ippo inga illa.**<br>Oh, that shop? He is not here right now.

> **Host A (F):** You ask when he’ll be back. The man shrugs.

> **Host B (M):** **Theriyala.**<br>I don't know / It's not known.

[Pause: 1 sec]

> **Host A (F):** You try to call the number on your phone. It rings and rings, then cuts to a busy tone. The man watches your screen.

> **Host B (M):** **Summa solraanga. Signal illa thambi.**<br>They are just saying that. There is no signal, brother.

> **Host A (F):** You ask if there's another way to reach him. The man starts talking fast, pointing down a dark alley, mentioning a cousin and a second shop. It’s too much.

> **Host B (M):** **Puriyala.**<br>I don't understand.

[Pause: 1 sec]

> **Host A (F):** As soon as you say you don't understand, the man turns to walk away. The lead is disappearing. You have to keep him talking.

> **Host B (M):** **Enna sonneenga?**<br>What did you say?

> **Host A (F):** He stops. He turns back, leaning in close. The street noise surges—a truck bellows nearby.

> **Host B (M):** **Anga ponaa number kidaikkum!**<br>If you go there, you will get the number!

> **Host A (F):** You shake your head, pointing at your ear.

> **Host B (M):** **Ketkala.**<br>I couldn't hear / It wasn't audible.

[Pause: 1 sec]

> **Host A (F):** He sighs, looking at your phone again. You show him the "Message Sent" icon that’s been spinning for five minutes.

> **Host B (M):** **Adhu varala.**<br>It didn't come (through).

> **Host A (F):** You ask how much it will cost to just get a new part elsewhere. 

> **Host B (M):** **Evlo aagum? Theriyala. Aana avaru onnum pannala.**<br>How much will it be? I don't know. But he didn't do anything.

> **Host A (F):** You realize you've been standing here for twenty minutes. The sun is hot, the story is changing, and you're no closer to your repair.

> **Host B (M):** **Pannala.**<br>He didn't do (it).

[Pause: 1 sec]

> **Host B (M):** **Mudiyala.**<br>I can't / It's not possible.

> **Host A (F):** You look at the locked door, then at the man who is now checking his own teeth in the reflection of your phone screen. 

> **Host B (M):** **Romba alaichal, illa?**<br>A lot of hassle, isn't it?

[Pause: 2 sec]

[SFX: The loud sound of a shutter finally rattling open, but two buildings down. The man disappears instantly toward it.]

---

[Pause: 2 sec]

> **Analyst Maya (F):** Raj, that man in the street was the perfect Coimbatore "helpful stranger." The kind who gives you directions to a place that closed in 1994 just to keep the conversation going.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** Exactly! He wasn't trying to help; he was enjoying the *alaichal*—the hassle. The drama of the "nothing works" day. 

> **Analyst Maya (F):** But did you notice how the "Nothing Works" machine sounds? Everything ended in that specific *-la* sound. *Theriyala, varala, pannala.*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** It’s so satisfying to say. It’s the Negative Machine. You take the verb, and you just... blunt the end of it. It’s not just "no," it’s "it didn't happen."

> **Analyst Maya (F):** Right, like when the message wouldn't send. He said *Varala*. 

> **Analyst Raj (M):** [Pause: 1 sec]
**Adhu varala.** It’s not coming. The message is stuck in the air somewhere. 

> **Analyst Maya (F):** And the social repair tool! When Andrew said *Puriyala*, the guy almost walked away. In a suspicious transaction, "I don't understand" sounds like "I'm done."

> **Analyst Raj (M):** **Enna sonneenga?**—"What did you say?"—that’s the hook. It forces the other person to stay and repeat themselves. It keeps the game alive.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** I love that contrast. *Puriyala* is a wall. *Enna sonneenga?* is a bridge. Even if the bridge leads to a guy who knows absolutely nothing.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** Which brings us to the most honest thing he said the whole time: **Theriyala.** I don't know. 

> **Analyst Maya (F):** He said it with such confidence though! Like not knowing was his full-time job.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** In Coimbatore, sometimes "I don't know" is just the start of a very long story.

[Pause: 1 sec]

> **Analyst Maya (F):** We should probably check if that shop ever actually opened.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** **Theriyala, Maya. Theriyala.**<br>I don't know, Maya. I don't know.

[SFX: Brief street ambience fades out.]
