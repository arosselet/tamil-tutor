> **Analyst Maya (F)** · "கடைசில பாக்கலாம்" — அந்த line மட்டும் கேட்டாலே, அந்த call-ல என்ன நடந்துச்சுன்னு புரியும். · *"Kadaisila paakalam" — anda line mattum kettaale, anda call-la enna nadanduchunu puriyum.* · "We'll see at the end" — hear just that one line and you know exactly what happened in that call.

> **Analyst Raj (M)** · ஆமா. The verdict came from the table — அவரு கிட்டேர்ந்து இல்ல. · *Aama. The verdict came from the table — avaru kitterndhu illa.* · Yes. It came from the table — not from him.

> **Analyst Maya (F)** · And notice the structure. "கடைசில" — at the end. "பாக்கலாம்" — we'll see. Combined, it isn't "maybe." It's a closed door with a polite smile on it.

> **Analyst Raj (M)** · Coimbatore-ல family decision இப்படித்தான். யாரும் நேரடியா "no"-னு சொல்ல மாட்டாங்க. "கடைசில பாக்கலாம்"-னு சொன்னா — முடிஞ்சுது. · *Coimbatore-la family decision ippadittaan. Yaarum nerdiyaa "no"-nu solla maattaanga. "Kadaisila paakalam"-nu sonnaa — mudinjudu.* · In Coimbatore, family decisions work like this. Nobody says "no" directly. Once they say "கடைசில பாக்கலாம்" — it's done.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Analyst Maya (F)** · And every conditional before it — சீட் இருந்தா, price OK-னா, அத்தை வந்தா. ஒவ்வொரு "if"-உம் ஒரு door. · *Seet irundaa, price OK-na, attai vandaa. Ovvoru "if"-um oru door.* · Seats available, price OK, aunt comes — each "if" is a door.

> **Analyst Raj (M)** · One by one, closing.

It's the optimistic "if" with a "no" hiding inside. · *"Mudinjaa" — mudiyum means "it's possible." Mudinjaa — "if it's possible." Aanaa in practice?* · The optimistic "if" — with a "no" hiding inside.
