# Captions — Ep 59 · Kadaisila Paakalam — The Family Verdict

*Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — blind is the win.*
**Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.

---

> *[SFX: home kitchen — chai cups clinking, low family chatter, pressure cooker whistle — then a mobile phone rings]*

> **Host A (F):** *Aama, ticket book pannaNNum. Innaike. Final.*<br>
> Yes, we need to book the ticket. Today. Final.

> **Host B (M):** *Aama aama. Naan paakuren.*<br>
> Yes yes. I'll look into it.

> **Host A (F):** *"Paakuren" vendam. Ippave sollu.*<br>
> No more "I'll look into it." Tell me right now.

> **Host B (M):** *Saari saari. Seet irukka?*<br>
> OK OK. Are there seats?

> **Host A (F):** *Seet irukku. Seet irundaa book pannalaam daane?*<br>
> There are seats. If there are seats, we can book — right?

> **Host B (M):** *Seet irukku. Aanaa price OK-na pannalaam.*<br>
> Seats exist. But if the price is OK, we can do it.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A (F):** *Price nallaa irukkunu niiye sonne! Netre.*<br>
> You yourself said the price is good! Yesterday.

> **Host B (M):** *Sonneen. Aanaa — amma kitta kekkaNum.*<br>
> I said it. But — I need to check with Mum.

> **Host A (F):** *Ippo enna solraanga?*<br>
> What are they saying right now?

> **Host B (M):** *Amma, ticket book pannalamaannu kekkuraanga.*<br>
> Mum, they're asking if we should book the ticket.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host B (M):** *Amma solraanga — mudinjaa vaarомnu.*<br>
> Mum says — if possible, we'll come.

> **Host A (F):** *"Mudinjaa vaarom"? Adenna solre?*<br>
> "If possible, we'll come"? What does that even mean?

> **Host B (M):** *Adaavadu — neram irundaa vaarom, seet kidaicchaa paakalam, ellaame OK-na polaam.*<br>
> Meaning — if there's time we'll come, if seats are there we'll see, if everything's OK we'll go.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A (F):** *Aama, ellaame ready-yaa irukke! Ippave confirm pannaNNum.*<br>
> Yes, everything IS ready! We need to confirm right now.

> **Host B (M):** *Aanaa — attai vandaa pesaNNum. Avanga sonnaa polaam.*<br>
> But — if aunt comes, we need to talk. If she says yes, we can go.

> **Host A (F):** *Attai eppo varuvaanga?*<br>
> When is aunt coming?

> **Host B (M):** *Naalaikku varuvaanganu sonnaanga.*<br>
> They said she'll come tomorrow.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A (F):** *Saari. date-ai sollu — enda date OK-na polaaMnu?*<br>
> OK. Tell me the date — which date works for us to go?

> **Host B (M):** *Date-aiyum kadaisila paakalam.*<br>
> The date too — we'll see about that at the end.

> **Host A (F):** *Date-aiyum!?*<br>
> The date TOO!?

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host B (M):** *Illa — pattaam date OK-na vaarom. Illainnaa ver date paakalam.*<br>
> No — if the 10th works, we'll come. If not, we'll look at another date.

> **Host A (F):** *Illainnaa — inda price naalaikke poidum. Puriyudaa?*<br>
> Otherwise — this price disappears tomorrow. You understand?

> **Host B (M):** *Puriyudu. Aanaa — mudinjaa — kadaisila paakalam.*<br>
> I understand. But — if possible — we'll see at the end.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A (F):** *"Kadaisila paakalam"? Moonuu naalaa idaiye solre!*<br>
> "We'll see at the end"? You've been saying this for three days!

> **Host B (M):** *Enna solrenga? — Saari. Avanga solraanga — kadaisila paakalamnu.*<br>
> What do you all say? — OK. They say — we'll see at the end.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A (F):** *Saari. Seekiram decide pannunga. Price ponaa — naan sollalanu sollaadeenga.*<br>
> Fine. Decide quickly. If the price is gone — don't say I didn't warn you.

> **Host B (M):** *Saari. Mudinjaa phone panren.*<br>
> OK. If possible, I'll call.

> *[Pause: 1.5 sec]*

---

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Kadaisila paakalam" — anda line mattum kettaale, anda call-la enna nadanduchunu puriyum.*<br>
> "We'll see at the end" — hear just that one line and you know exactly what happened in that call.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Aama. The verdict came from the table — avaru kitterndhu illa.*<br>
> Yes. It came from the table — not from him.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *And notice the structure. "kadaisi-la" — at the end. "paakalam" — we'll see. Combined, it isn't "maybe." It's a closed door with a polite smile on it.*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Coimbatore-la family decision ippadittaan. Yaarum nerdiyaa "no"-nu solla maattaanga. "Kadaisila paakalam"-nu sonnaa — mudinjudu.*<br>
> In Coimbatore, family decisions work like this. Nobody says "no" directly. Once they say "kadaisila paakalam" — it's done.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Seet irundaa, price OK-na, attai vandaa. Ovvoru "if"-um oru door.*<br>
> Seats available, price OK, aunt comes — each "if" is a door.

> **Analyst Raj (M)** · One by one, closing.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Mudinjaa vaarom" — ammaave solliTTaanga.*<br>
> "If possible, we'll come" — Mum herself said it. After that, it's just theatre.

> *[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *"Mudinjaa" — mudiyum means "it's possible." Mudinjaa — "if it's possible." Aanaa in practice?*<br>
> The optimistic "if" — with a "no" hiding inside.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Tea table-la "kadaisila paakalam"-nu sonnaa —*<br>
> Once the tea table says it, the call was over before it ended.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Adudaan. Anda table sonnadu — final.*<br>
> That's it. What the table said — final.
