# Captions — Ep 95 · Trichy Road: Paati's Secret Present

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: Trichy Road traffic hum, a shop bell, a ceiling fan clattering]*

> **Karthi:** *Inga vaanga, inga vaanga. Indha shop nalla irukkum.*<br>
> Come here, come here. This shop will be good.

> **Deepa:** *Karthi, paatikku enna vaanga porom?*<br>
> Karthi, what are we going to buy for Paati?

> **Karthi:** *Onnu nice-aa venum, Birthday-ku.*<br>
> We need something nice, for the birthday.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

*[SFX: bangles clinking, boxes shuffling on a counter]*

> **Shopkeeper:** *Vanakkam! Enna venum ungalukku?*<br>
> Hello! What do you need?

> **Deepa:** *Anga irukke andha toy. Adhu evlo aagum?*<br>
> That toy over there. How much will that cost?

> **Shopkeeper:** *Adhu? Ettu hundred rupees.*<br>
> That? Eight hundred rupees.

> **Deepa:** *Ettu hundred-aa? Sari sari.*<br>
> Eight hundred? Okay, okay.

> **Ravi:** *Enakku idhu venum!!*<br>
> I want this!!

> **Deepa:** *Nee illa, Ravi. Idhu paatikku.*<br>
> Not you, Ravi. This is for Paati.

> **Ravi:** *Aana enakkum venum!*<br>
> But I want it too!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Karthi:** *Sari sari, appuram paappom. Mudhalla paatikku paakkalaam.*<br>
> Okay okay, we'll look later. First let's look for Paati.

> **Deepa:** *Idhu maama kudutha money illaiyaa?*<br>
> Isn't this the money Uncle gave?

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Karthi:** *Illa illa, idhu onnum illa! Romba money irukku enkitta!*<br>
> No, no, it's nothing! I have lots of money!

> **Deepa:** *Appadiyaa?!*<br>
> Is that so?!

> **Shopkeeper:** *Indha bangles paarunga. Aaru bangles oru set-la.*<br>
> Look at these bangles. Six bangles in one set.

> **Deepa:** *Aaru-aa? Idhu evlo aagum?*<br>
> Six? How much will this cost?

> **Shopkeeper:** *Idhu konjam adhigam thaan. Six hundred rupees.*<br>
> This is a bit much. Six hundred rupees.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Karthi:** *Six hundred-aa?!*

> **Karthi:** *Paravaayilla, paravaayilla! Idhu onnum illa!*<br>
> It's okay, it's okay! It's nothing!

> **Deepa:** *Namma kitta five hundred rupees thaan irukke?*<br>
> We only have five hundred rupees, don't we?

> **Karthi:** *Konjam thaan...*<br>
> Just a little...

> **Karthi:** *(catches himself) Illa illa, podhum podhum. Vera onnu paakkalaam.*<br>
> No, no, enough, enough. Let's look at something else.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Ravi:** *Karthi, idhu maamaavoda money thaane?*<br>
> Karthi, this is Uncle's money, right?

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Karthi:** *Yaaru sonna?! Idhu onnum illa!!*<br>
> Who said that?! It's nothing!!

> **Karthi:** *Naan ippo romba nalla irukken!*<br>
> I'm doing really well right now!

> **Shopkeeper:** *Neenga nalla irukkeengalaa?*<br>
> Are you doing okay?

> **Karthi:** *Nalla irukken, nalla irukken.*<br>
> I'm fine, I'm fine.

> **Karthi:** *(to Deepa, quieter) Andha bangles vendaam. Vera sinna onnu paaru.*<br>
> Not those bangles. Look at something else small.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Deepa:** *Sari. (pointing) Idhu evlo aagum?*<br>
> Okay. How much is this?

> **Shopkeeper:** *Andha sweet box-aa? Naalu hundred rupees.*<br>
> That sweet box? Four hundred rupees.

> **Ravi:** *Sweets!! Enakku idhu venum!!*<br>
> Sweets!! I want this!!

> **Deepa:** *Paatikku thaan, Ravi.*<br>
> It's for Paati, Ravi.

> **Ravi:** *Paatikkum enakkum!*<br>
> For Paati and for me!

*[SFX: light laughter]*

> **Deepa:** *Paati eppavume "onnum vendaam"-nu solluvaanga aamaa?*<br>
> Paati always says "I don't want anything," doesn't she?

> **Karthi:** *Aamaa solluvaanga. Aana avangalukku enna pidikkum-nu namakku theriyum.*<br>
> Yes, she says that. But we know what she likes.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Deepa:** *Appo indha sweet box thaan. Sinnadhaa irukku, nallaavum irukku.*<br>
> Then it's this sweet box. It's small, and it's nice too.

> **Shopkeeper:** *Ezhu sweets irukku ulla.*<br>
> There are seven sweets inside.

> **Ravi:** *Ezhu sweets!!*<br>
> Seven sweets!!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Karthi:** *Sari, idhe vaangalaam. Sinna present, aana paravaayilla.*<br>
> Okay, let's buy this one. A small present, but that's fine.

> **Shopkeeper:** *Naalu hundred thaan. Paravaayilla.*<br>
> Just four hundred. It's fine.

> **Karthi:** *Aamaa aamaa, idhu onnum illa!*<br>
> Yes yes, it's nothing!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

*[SFX: coins, a small bag crinkling]*

> **Deepa:** *Paatikku pidikkumaa idhu?*<br>
> Will Paati like this?

> **Karthi:** *Pidikkum. Avanga vendaam-nu solluvaanga, aana pidikkum.*<br>
> She'll like it. She'll say she doesn't want it, but she'll like it.

> **Karthi:** *Adhu thaan paati.*<br>
> That's just how Paati is.

> **Ravi:** *Namakku two birthdays irukkaa?*<br>
> Do we have two birthdays?

> **Deepa:** *Illa, Ravi. Onnu thaan.*<br>
> No, Ravi. Just one.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Karthi:** *Polaamaa? Lunch-ku neram aagudhu.*<br>
> Shall we go? It's getting time for lunch.

*[SFX: shop bell, footsteps fading into traffic]*

> **Deepa:** *Karthi, indha money, maama kitta sollap poriyaa?*<br>
> Karthi, this money — are you going to tell Uncle?

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Karthi:** *(quieter than everything before) ...Polaam, Deepa. Polaam.*<br>
> ...Let's go, Deepa. Let's go.

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

*[SFX: traffic swallows the scene]*
