# Captions — Ep 67 · The Permission Machine

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[Low voice, almost a whisper]*

> **Maya:** *Raj. Do you have a minute?*

*[Hushed]*

> **Raj:** *Yes. What's wrong? Why are we whispering?*

> **Maya:** *I need to talk about the ultimate extended family dread.*

> **Raj:** *Oh no. Who?*

> **Maya:** *maama.*<br>Uncle.

*[Sighs]*

> **Raj:** *The maternal uncle. Just saying the word makes me sit up straight.*

> **Maya:** *Right? Because every family has one. namma maama irukkaarula...*<br>Right? Because every family has one. You know our uncle, right...

> **Raj:** *'You know our uncle, right...' Oh, I know him. The strict one.*

> **Maya:** *The house where you walk in, and you just feel bayam.*

> **Raj:** *Pure 'fear'.*

> **Maya:** *Because you can't just do anything. You have to ask permission for every single breath. The -laamaa machine.*

> **Raj:** *The permission machine. 'May I...'*

> **Maya:** *Yes. naan {verb}-laamaa? May I do this verb?*

> **Raj:** *So you walk into his periya house...*

> **Maya:** *And you don't just take a seat. No way. You hover. You look at the chair. And you whisper to your cousins... utkaaralaamaa?*

> **Raj:** *'Shall we sit?'*

> **Maya:** *utkaaralaamaa? The tension is crushing. Because if you just sit down without asking, you know his rule. pidikkaathu.*

> **Raj:** *'Doesn't like it.' He doesn't like it.*

> **Maya:** *Right. So you stand. And someone has to be the brave one to speak to him. You nudge your brother. kekkalaamaa?*

> **Raj:** *'Shall we ask?'*

> **Maya:** *Yes. kekkalaamaa? And when you finally do speak maama kitta...*

> **Raj:** *'Near Maama.' When you're standing with him...*

> **Maya:** *You have to use the ultimate polite gear. The -nga machine is bolted to everything. You can't just say anuppu for 'send'.*

> **Raj:** *You have to say anuppunga.*

> **Maya:** *Exactly. anuppunga. Everything gets that respectful -nga.*

> **Raj:** *So you're sweating, asking utkaaralaamaa?, adding -nga to every verb... it's a gauntlet.*

> **Maya:** *It is. But here's the thing. Here is the big twist.*

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Raj:** *A twist?*

> **Maya:** *Yesterday, I was talking to the older aunts. And you know what sonnaanga?*

> **Raj:** *What did 'they say'?*

> **Maya:** *sonnaanga... that maama is actually terrified of us.*

> **Raj:** *Wait. What?*

> **Maya:** *He's so scared of offending the nieces and nephews. He thinks we're these modern, fragile guests. He's sitting there, stiff with anxiety, wishing we would just relax, grab a plate, and eat!*

> **Raj:** *So while we are paralyzed in the corner whispering kekkalaamaa?...*

> **Maya:** *He's paralyzed in the chair, hoping we just make ourselves at home.*
