# Captions — Ep 65 · The Phone Call

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: Phone ringing. Three times. Four. A sharp click. The line opens to heavy silence.]*

> **Caller (M):** *Maama... vanakkam.*<br>
> Uncle... hello.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maama (M):** *Enna venum?*<br>
> What do you want?

> **Caller (M):** *Maama, kovam vendaam. Aanaa oru nimisham kudunga.*<br>
> Uncle, don't be angry. But give me one minute.

> **Maama (M):** *Enakku neramilla. Seekkiram sollu.*<br>
> I don't have time. Tell me quickly.

> **Caller (M):** *Nethu oru thappaachu... aanaa enakku puriyala.*<br>
> Yesterday a mistake happened... but I don't understand.

> **Maama (M):** *Enna puriyala?*<br>
> What don't you understand?

> **Caller (M):** *Annaagitta ketten. Anna sonnaanga... enga azhagaana kalyaanam ungalukkup pidikkaathu.*<br>
> I asked older brother. Older brother said... you don't like our beautiful wedding.

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Maama (M):** *Appadiyaa?! Avan appadi sonnaanaa?*<br>
> Is that so?! Did he say that?

> **Caller (M):** *Aamaa maama. Ippo neramillannaa, enakku phone pannunga.*<br>
> Yes uncle. If you don't have time now, call me.

> **Maama (M):** *Ippo neram irukku. Aanaa enakku kovam irukku.*<br>
> I have time now. But I am angry.

> **Caller (M):** *Appuramaa kooppiduren. Eppa free-yaa iruppeenga?*<br>
> I will call later. When will you be free?

> **Maama (M):** *Naalaikku neram irukku.*<br>
> I have time tomorrow.

> **Caller (M):** *Sari maama. Appuramaa pesalaamaa?*<br>
> Okay uncle. Shall we speak later?

> **Maama (M):** *Sari.*<br>
> Okay.

*[SFX: The call drops gently, leaving a quiet dial tone.]*
