# Captions — Ep 64 · The Tea Table

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

> **Nina:** *The tea table in a Tamil extended family home is essentially a demilitarized zone. Whatever misunderstanding happened earlier in the day... you sit down at four o'clock, the snacks come out, and there is a very quiet opportunity to fix things without anyone losing face.*

> **Kabilan:** *Exactly. Yesterday, things got a little tense with my uncle. My maamaa.*

> **Nina:** *Maamaa.*<br>Uncle.

> **Kabilan:** *So, later, we were both sitting at the table. Dead silent. Usually, if I want something, I just announce the need to the room. Coffee venum. I want coffee.*

> **Nina:** *But venum is a demand. "I want." If the bridge is shaking, you don't demand. You ask for a transaction. Hand to hand.*

> **Kabilan:** *Right. I looked at the tea flask. It was right near my uncle. Maamaa kitta irukku.*

> **Nina:** *It is near uncle. Maamaa kitta irukku.*

> **Kabilan:** *Now, I could just reach across the table and take it. Aanaa... I needed to break the ice.*

> **Nina:** *Aanaa. But.*

> **Kabilan:** *So I looked at him. I didn't say venum. I shifted to the polite ask. I said, "Konjam tea kudunga."*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Nina:** *Kudunga. Give me. You bolted that polite -nga onto the end. "Give me a little tea."*

> **Kabilan:** *Yeah. It’s softer. He poured it. Passed it over. Then he pushed the plate of snacks toward me. Now, the stakes are real here. If he offers food, I have to take it. Illannaa...*

> **Nina:** *Illannaa. If not...*

> **Kabilan:** *If not, the peace treaty fails. He looked at me and said, "Innum konjam saappidunga."*

*[Softly]*

> **Nina:** *"Eat a little more."*

> **Kabilan:** *And I didn't even want it. Enakku pidikkaathu. I don't like those snacks. But... sari. Okay. I took one. Seekkiram ate it. Quickly.*

> **Nina:** *The peace offering is accepted. The bridge is repaired.*

*[Smiles]*

> **Kabilan:** *Just then, my elder brother walks in. My anna. He hasn't read the room at all. He just sits down and declares, "Enakku coffee venum!"*

*[Laughs quietly]*

> **Nina:** *Trashing the delicate ecosystem.*

> **Kabilan:** *My uncle didn't even look up. He just poured him a cup of tea and said, "Coffee illannu sonnaanga."*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Nina:** *Wait, I caught that little quote tag at the end. -nnu sonnaanga. "They said that..." "They said there is no coffee." So wonderfully passive.*

> **Kabilan:** *Yep. My anna was confused, but he just sat down and drank the tea. You have to respect the kudunga at the table.*
