# Captions — Ep 71 · The Family Blockade

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: Bathroom fan running, muffled party/kitchen noise outside. A phone dials and connects.]*
> **Host B (M):** *Hello? Please tell me you have a minute.*

> **Host A (F):** *Yeah, I'm here. Where are you? You sound like you're hiding.*

> **Host B (M):** *I am. I'm locked in the bathroom.*

> **Host A (F):** *What? Why? I thought you were leaving?*

> **Host B (M):** *I am trying to! I have to catch my train. Station-ukku poganum.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*
> **Host B (M):** *But my mother-in-law is blocking the door.*

> **Host A (F):** *[Laughs] Oh no. Did you tell her you're running late?*

> **Host B (M):** *Yes! I said, 'Neram aachu.' I told her I have to go. Station-ukku poganum.*

> **Host A (F):** *And what did she say?*

> **Host B (M):** *'Ukkarunga-nu sonnanga.'*<br>'She told me to sit down.'

*[Pause: 1 sec]*
> **Host B (M):** *She literally just pointed at the chair. 'Ukkarunga, saappidunga.'*

> **Host A (F):** *She's not taking no for an answer.*

> **Host B (M):** *Exactly! Every time I try to stand up, she hits me with this rapid-fire Tamil guilt trip.*

> **Host A (F):** *What is she saying?*

> **Host B (M):** *I don't know! Aanaa, she talks so fast.*

> **Host B (M):** *I don't catch half of it. Enakku puriyala.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*
> **Host B (M):** *The only word I understand is 'saappidunga.'*

> **Host A (F):** *So you just sat back down?*

> **Host B (M):** *Yes! I just awkwardly sit back down.*

> **Host A (F):** *Well, she's just being nice. Just eat a little bit. Konjam saappidunga.*

> **Host B (M):** *I can't! Vayiru full! I already ate so much.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*
> **Host B (M):** *And you know I don't like eating this much. Enakku pidikkaadhu.*

> **Host A (F):** *You need to be firm. Just say you're in a hurry. You have an excuse.*

> **Host B (M):** *Right. I'll just say, 'Avasaram irukku.'*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*
> **Host B (M):** *'Station-ukku poganum, avasaram irukku.' That's polite, right?*

> **Host A (F):** *Yeah, that works perfectly. Avasaram irukku.*

> **Host A (F):** *But you have to actually leave the bathroom first to say it.*

*[SFX: A gentle knock on the door, muffled voice from outside: "Thambi? Neram aachu, veliya vaanga!"]*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*
> **Host B (M):** *Oh god. She found me.*

> **Host A (F):** *Good luck!*

> **Host B (M):** *Sari, naan appuramaa kooppiduraen!*<br>Okay, I'll call you later!

*[SFX: Call disconnects]*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *He just went straight to the point: 'Station-ukku poganum.'*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Aanaa, that didn't work against the mother-in-law...*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Let's listen to that moment again.*

*[SFX: Tape rewind]*

> **Host B (M):** *She literally just pointed at the chair.*
