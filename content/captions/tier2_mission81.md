# Captions — Ep 81 · The Guest and the Ghost

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

[SFX: The distant sound of a car engine cutting out. The rhythmic 'tick-tick' of a cooling engine. Birds chirping in a quiet Coimbatore residential street.]

> **Narrator (M):** You stand at the iron gate of the house. Your bags are heavy, your shirt is sticking to your back after the long drive, but the door is already swinging open.

[SFX: Sound of a heavy wooden door creaking open, followed by the excited chatter of bangles and quick footsteps.]

> **Narrator (M):** Two women rush out. You recognize them from the old photos. The sisters-in-law. They are beaming, their hands ready to take your bags.

> **Host A (F):** **Vaanga! Vaanga!**
> Welcome! Welcome!

> **Host B (M):** **Akka, inga paarunga!**
> Sister, look here!

> **Host A (F):** **Romba santhosham!**
> Very happy!

> **Narrator (M):** The greeting lands with a warmth that catches you off guard. *Romba santhosham.* Very happy. She isn't just saying it; she’s radiating it. 

> **Host B (M):** **Aama, romba santhosham. Ulla vaanga.**
> Yes, very happy. Come inside.

> **Narrator (M):** They usher you inside. The house is cool, smelling of incense and old wood. They won't let you carry a single thing.

[SFX: Footsteps moving from gravel to echoing tile. The sound of a ceiling fan whirring at high speed.]

> **Host A (F):** **Ukkaarunga.**
> Sit.

> **Narrator (M):** *Ukkaarunga.* Sit. You take a seat on the heavy teak sofa.

> **Host B (M):** **Atthai! Seekiram vaanga!**
> Aunt! Come quickly!

> **Narrator (M):** He’s calling for his *Atthai*. Your paternal aunt. She’s the one who organized everything.

[SFX: Slow, shuffling footsteps on tile.]

> **Host A (F):** **Atthai varaanga.**
> Aunt is coming.

> **Narrator (M):** An older woman enters the room. She stops dead when she sees you. The smile she was wearing doesn't disappear, but it freezes.

[Pause: 2 sec]

> **Host B (M):** **Atthai, enna sonneenga?**
> Aunt, what did you say?

> **Narrator (M):** He asks her what she said. But she hasn't said a word yet. She’s just staring at your face.

> **Host A (F):** **Paarunga atthai... avanga kangal.**
> Look aunt... their eyes.

> **Narrator (M):** *Avanga kangal.* Their eyes. She’s looking at your eyes and talking about you in the respectful third person—*Avanga*.

> **Host B (M):** **Aama... periya otrumai.**
> Yes... a big similarity.

> **Narrator (M):** *Periya.* A big similarity. The energy in the room has shifted. The chaotic joy is gone, replaced by a heavy, quiet awe.

> **Host A (F):** **Atthai, sollunga.**
> Aunt, tell us.

> **Narrator (M):** *Sollunga.* Tell us. She wants the aunt to speak, to confirm what they are all seeing. 

> **Host B (M):** **Unakku theriyuma?**
> Do you know?

> **Narrator (M):** He looks at you. *Unakku theriyuma?* Do you know? Do you know who you look like?

> **Host A (F):** **Romba... romba santhosham.**
> Very... very happy.

> **Narrator (M):** She says it again, but this time, *Romba santhosham* sounds different. It’s not a greeting anymore. There are tears in her eyes. It’s the happiness of seeing a ghost.

[Pause: 3 sec]

[SFX: The low hum of the ceiling fan fades into soft, melodic Carnatic flute music.]

> **Analyst Maya (F):** That shift is so heavy, Raj. From the "Welcome!" energy to that sudden, quiet realization.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** **Aama, Maya.** It started with that high-energy greeting. **Romba santhosham!**

> **Analyst Maya (F):** Right. *Romba santhosham.* It’s the standard "very happy to meet you," but the way they said it at the end... it felt like they were seeing someone they’ve missed for years.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** Exactly. **Atthai** was just shocked. "**Periya otrumai**" — a big similarity.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** And using "**avanga**" to talk about the guest, even though he's right there. It shows so much respect, but also a little bit of distance, like they are looking at a portrait.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** **Aama.** It’s that moment where you realize you aren't just a guest. You are a memory.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** "**Sollunga**" — they were almost begging the aunt to say it out loud. 

> **Analyst Raj (M):** "**Unakku theriyuma?**" Do you know? I don't think he does, Maya. Not yet.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** It’s a lot to take in. **Romba santhosham**... but with a lot of history behind it.

[SFX: Music swells slightly and then fades.]
