# Captions — Ep 88 · The Negative Machine

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

[SFX: Sound of a door closing, keys hitting a wooden table. The gentle hum of a ceiling fan.]

> **Host B (M):** I’m back! Or... what’s left of me is back.

> **Host A (F):** [Laughs] You look like you walked across the whole city. Was it that bad?

> **Host B (M):** **Appappa... romba alaichal.** It was just a total mess from the start.
> Oh man... so much wandering around.

> **Host A (F):** Why? What happened? Did you find the shop?

> **Host B (M):** **That’s the thing. Kadai enga irukkunnu theriyala.**
> That's the thing. I didn't know where the shop was.

> **Host A (F):** You couldn't find it? But I gave you the map!

> **Host B (M):** I know, I know. I looked at the map, but the street names... **puriyala.**
> I know, I know. I looked at the map, but the street names... I didn't understand.

[Pause: 1 sec]

> **Host A (F):** **Appadiya?!** Even with the GPS?
> Is that so?! Even with the GPS?

> **Host B (M):** **GPS signal varala.** There was no network there. I tried to call your brother to ask for directions, but **call panna mudiyala.**
> The GPS signal didn't come. There was no network there. I tried to call your brother to ask for directions, but I couldn't make the call.

> **Host A (F):** [Chuckling] Oh no. So you were just wandering around?

> **Host B (M):** Yeah. I asked a few people on the corner. But what they said... **summa solraanga.** One person sent me left, the other sent me right.
> Yeah. I asked a few people on the corner. But what they said... they're just saying whatever. One person sent me left, the other sent me right.

> **Host A (F):** [Laughing] Wait, what did they actually say? 

> **Host B (M):** **Enna sonneenga?** I couldn't even hear them over the traffic!
> What did you (all) say? I couldn't even hear them over the traffic!

[Pause: 1 sec]

> **Host A (F):** So you never got the parcel? 

> **Host B (M):** No. By the time I found the place, it was locked. **Neram aachu.** It was just one of those days.
> No. By the time I found the place, it was locked. It got late. It was just one of those days.

> **Host A (F):** Hey, come here. **Utkaarunga. Freeya vidunga.** It’s just a parcel.
> Hey, come here. Sit down. Just let it go.

> **Host B (M):** [Sighs, smiling] **Aama aama.** It’s actually funny now. All that walking for nothing.
> Yes, yes. It’s actually funny now. All that walking for nothing.

> **Host A (F):** It's not for nothing. You got some exercise! 

> **Host B (M):** Exercise? **Palagipochu.** I'm just glad to be home.
> Exercise? I'm used to it. I'm just glad to be home.

[Pause: 2 sec]

> **Analyst Maya (F):** Poor guy. That feeling of the world just refusing to cooperate—we’ve all been there.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** Totally. But did you hear how he was describing the "failure"? It all had this specific rhythmic ending. *Theriyala, puriyala, mudiyala.* 

> **Analyst Maya (F):** Exactly! The "Negative Machine." That "la" sound at the end of the verbs is like a shutter closing. 

> **Analyst Raj (M):** It really is. Like when he said "**puriyala**"—*puriyala*. It’s not just "I don't understand," it’s like the understanding simply didn't arrive. 

> **Analyst Maya (F):** And "**mudiyala**"—*mudiyala*. We hear that all the time in Coimbatore when someone is frustrated or exhausted. "I can't even." But here, he literally couldn't make the call. The situation didn't allow it. 

> **Analyst Raj (M):** [Laughs] And my favorite part was the "**summa solraanga**"—*summa solraanga*. They’re just talking. They don't know the way, but they'll give you directions anyway just to be polite!

> **Analyst Maya (F):** It’s the "tender disaster" vibe. Even though nothing worked—the signal **varala**, the shop **theriyala**—he’s not angry. 

> **Analyst Raj (M):** Because he’s home now. And she gave him the best advice for a day like that: "**freeya vidunga**"—*freeya vidunga*. Just let it go.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** If only we could all negate our problems that easily with a little "la" at the end. 

> **Analyst Raj (M):** [Laughs] If only! 

[Pause: 1 sec]

[SFX: Soft outro music fades in]
