# Captions — Ep 63 · Kudunga: The Softest Scam at the Tea Table

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: Late afternoon — tea cups clinking, low family murmur, two people step aside]*

> **Host A:** *Saayangaalam tee — andha table-la naan onnu paathen.*<br>
> Evening tea — at that table, I noticed something.

> **Host B:** *Ennaavadhu?*<br>
> What is it?

> **Host A:** *Nee scam pannine.*<br>
> You ran a scam.

> **Host B:** *Aiyo, scam-aa?! Illave illa!*<br>
> Aiyo, a scam?! No, absolutely not!

> **Host A:** *Aama. Soft-aa. Seekkirama. Clean-aa.*<br>
> Yes. Softly. Quickly. Cleanly.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Mudhalla — akka kitta — "andha thattu kudunga."*<br>
> First — to Elder Sister — "please give me that plate."

> **Host B:** *Aama, thattu ava side-la irundhuchu.*<br>
> Yes, the plate was on her side.

> **Host A:** *Appuram — maama kitta — "adhu kudunga."*<br>
> Then — to Uncle — "give me that."

> **Host B:** *Adhuvum andha side-la dhaan...*<br>
> That was on that side too...

> **Host A:** *Appuram — thambi kitta — "oru tee kudunga."*<br>
> Then — to Younger Brother — "give me a tea."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Aanju nimisham aagalai — un munnaadi ellaame!*<br>
> Not even five minutes passed — everything was in front of you!

> **Host B:** *(laughs) Adhanaal ennaavadhu?*<br>
> So what of it?

> **Host A:** *Nee "venum" sollave illa!*<br>
> You never said "I want"!

> **Host B:** *Aama. Venum sondhaa — announcement-aayidum.*<br>
> Yes. If you say "I want" — it becomes an announcement.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Adhendhna solre?*<br>
> What do you mean by that?

> **Host B:** *"Venum"-nnaa — "enna venum? yen venum? evvalavu venum?" — questions varum.*<br>
> If you say "I want" — "What do you want? Why? How much?" — questions come.

> **Host A:** *(laughs) Saridhaan!*<br>
> That's true!

> **Host B:** *"Kudunga"-nnaa — hand-to-hand. Seekkiram. Questions illa.*<br>
> If you say "give me" — hand-to-hand. Quickly. No questions.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Aanaa — onnu ketkiren. Oru sweet — adhu unakku pidikkaadhu-nnu theriyume?*<br>
> But — let me ask you something. One sweet — you know you don't like it, right?

> **Host B:** *Aama.*<br>
> Yes.

> **Host A:** *Adhaiyum "kudunga"-nnu kette?!*<br>
> You asked for that too with "give me"?!

> **Host B:** *Theriyala... table-la vandhuchu, ketten.*<br>
> I don't know... it came around on the table, I asked for it.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *(delighted) Pidikkaadha onnaiyum kudunga-nnu kette — appuram enna panne?*<br>
> You asked for something you don't even like — then what did you do?

> **Host B:** *(laughs) Akka kitta kuduttutten!*<br>
> I gave it to Elder Sister!

> **Host A:** *(laughs) Idhu scam illa — idhu system!*<br>
> This isn't a scam — this is a system!

> **Host B:** *Kudunga system. Family table-la ippadidhaan irukum.*<br>
> The kudunga system. That's just how it works at the family table.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Naalaikku saayangaalam — naan andha corner seat-la ukkaraanum.*<br>
> Tomorrow evening — I need to sit in that corner seat.

> **Host B:** *Yen?*<br>
> Why?

> **Host A:** *Ella side-layum — maama, akka, thambi — kudunga-nnu sollave.*<br>
> On every side — Uncle, Elder Sister, Younger Brother — so I can say "give me."

> **Host B:** *(laughs) Ippo purinjudhaa?*<br>
> Now you get it?

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

*[SFX: Tea cups and family voices return — warm, unhurried]*
