# Captions — Ep 77 · The Family Update Call

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.<br>
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: A mobile phone ringing. Two rings. A click — the call connects.]*

> **Host A (F):** *Halo!*<br>
> Hello!

> **Host B (F):** *Halo! Enna solreengk?*<br>
> Hello! What are you saying?

> **Host A (F):** *News solren — Namma thambi irukkaan-la… avan naalaikke varraan!*<br>
> Got some news — you know our younger brother, right… he's coming tomorrow!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host B (F):** *Aamaavaa?! Really?*<br>
> Is that so?! Really?

> **Host A (F):** *Aama! Naalaikke kilamburaan-nu sonnan.*<br>
> Yes! He said he's setting off tomorrow.

> **Host B (F):** *Romba nalla irukku.*<br>
> That's really good.

> **Host A (F):** *Aanaa — Namma akka irukkaang-la… avanga vara maattaanga-nu sonnaanga.*<br>
> But — you know our elder sister, right… they said she won't be able to come.

> **Host B (F):** *Aiyyo. Yen?*<br>
> Oh no. Why?

> **Host A (F):** *Udombu sariyilla-nu. Rest edukkanum-nu doctor sonnaanga.*<br>
> Not well, apparently. The doctor said she needs to rest.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host B (F):** *Poor thing. Seekkiram sariyaattum-nu sollunga avanga kitta.*<br>
> Poor thing. Tell her we hope she gets better soon.

> **Host A (F):** *Aama… ketten. Slowly better aagudhu-nu sonnaanga.*<br>
> Yes… I asked. They said it's slowly getting better.

> **Host A (F):** *Adhukku appuram — Namma maama irukkaang-la… avanga specially varranga!*<br>
> After that — you know our uncle, right… he's coming specially!

> **Host B (F):** *Maamaavaa?!*<br>
> Uncle?!

> **Host A (F):** *Aama. Azhagaana surprise illaiyaa?*<br>
> Yes. Beautiful surprise, isn't it?

> **Host B (F):** *Romba nalla irukku. Avanga varradu periya vishayam.*<br>
> Really good. Their coming is a big deal.

> **Host A (F):** *Aama! Namma atthai irukkaang-la — avangalum varranga-nu kettaanga!*<br>
> Yes! You know our aunt, right — heard she's also coming!

> **Host B (F):** *Aamaavaa! Super!*<br>
> Is that so! Super!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A (F):** *Aama… Namma pakkatthu veettukaarangk irukkaang-la… avangalum help panraanga.*<br>
> Yes… you know our next-door neighbours, right… they're helping too.

> **Host B (F):** *They're so good, really.*

> **Host A (F):** *Aama. Naanga pannuvom.*<br>
> Yes. We'll do it.

> **Host B (F):** *Naan enna pannanum-nu sollunga.*<br>
> Tell me what I should do.

> **Host A (F):** *Piragu solren. Ippo rest edunga.*<br>
> I'll tell you later. Rest now.

> **Host B (F):** *Sari sari. Vaanga!*<br>
> Okay okay. Welcome (goodbye)!

*[SFX: A gentle click as the call ends.]*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Did you hear how she opened every single piece of news?*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Ainja thadavai.*<br>
> Five times.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Namma thambi irukkaan-la — Namma akka irukkaang-la — Namma maama irukkaang-la —*<br>
> Our younger brother, right — Our elder sister, right — Our uncle, right —

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Same shape. Different person every time.*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *That -la at the end. "Right? You know them?"*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *It's not an introduction — she already knows they both know these people.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Namma is doing it too — "ours."*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *She's pulling the listener into the same family before she's even said a word.*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *And the stakes are real — Namma akka vara maattaanga.*<br>
> And the stakes are real — our sister won't be coming.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *That's genuine worry. Not just news.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *And maama making the trip specially. Adhu periya vishayam.*<br>
> And uncle making the trip specially. That's a big deal.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *This is how Coimbatore works. The whole family in one phone call.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Aama aama.*<br>
> Yes yes.
