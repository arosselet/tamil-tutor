# Captions — Ep 83 · Coimbatore Heat

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

[SFX: The low drone of a ceiling fan. The distant chirp of crickets. The clink of a coffee cup against a saucer.]

> **Host A:** The air is so heavy tonight. Not even a breeze on the verandah.

> **Host B:** *Aama.* It’s that Coimbatore heat.
> Yes. It’s that Coimbatore heat.

[Pause: 1 sec]

> **Host A:** Leave him be, he’s enjoying his coffee. We were talking about the family. *Namma Suresh irukkaan-la…*
> Leave him be, he’s enjoying his coffee. We were talking about the family. You know our Suresh, right...

> **Host B:** You know our Suresh, right? The one from the village?

> **Host A:** *Aama. Avanukku kalyanam-nu sonnaanga.*
> Yes. They said he’s getting married.

> **Host B:** Oh! They said he’s getting married? That’s good news, isn’t it?

> **Host A:** But wait. *Attai enna sonnaanga theriyuma?*
> But wait. Do you know what auntie said?

> **Host B:** What did she say? She usually knows all the trouble.

> **Host A:** *Anga ennamo prachanai-aam.* I hear there is some problem or other over there.
> I hear there is some problem or other over there.

> **Host B:** *Prachanai-aa?* A problem? Again?
> A problem? A problem? Again?

> **Host A:** *Aama. Alaichal jaasthi-nu sonnaanga.* They said the hassle is just too much. All that running around for the property papers.
> Yes. They said the hassle is too much. They said the hassle is just too much. All that running around for the property papers.

> **Host B:** *Alaichal thaan.* It *is* exactly a hassle. Every wedding in this family becomes a marathon.
> It is a hassle. It *is* exactly a hassle. Every wedding in this family becomes a marathon.

[Pause: 2 sec]

> **Host A:** But then, I called him yesterday. *Nethu pesinen.*
> But then, I called him yesterday. I spoke (to him) yesterday.

> **Host B:** And? Is he angry?

> **Host A:** *Illa.* He sounded... tired. He said, "*Niraiya per summa solraanga.*"
> No. He sounded... tired. He said, "Many people are just talking."

> **Host B:** "Many people are just talking"? Making it up?

> **Host A:** *Aama.* He just wants peace. He told me, "*Athai vittudu.*"
> Yes. He just wants peace. He told me, "Drop it."

> **Host B:** "Drop it." Let it go.

> **Host A:** Exactly. *Vittudu. Appuram...* we just talked about the old house.
> Exactly. Drop it. And then... we just talked about the old house.

> **Host B:** [Sighs] *Sari sari.* Maybe we should also let it go. Too much talk is also *jaasthi*.
> [Sighs] Okay, okay. Maybe we should also let it go. Too much talk is also too much.

> **Host A:** You’re right. Let’s just finish the coffee.

[SFX: The sound of a final sip, then the fan continues to hum into the night.]
