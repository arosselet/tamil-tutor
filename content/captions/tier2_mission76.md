*[SFX: The low hum of a refrigerator. Rhythmic chopping of vegetables. Two sisters, Amma and Atthai, speak in low, hushed tones. The perspective is from the hallway outside the kitchen.]*

> **Amma (F):** *Atthai… theriyumaanga? Namma thambi irukkaanla…*<br>
> Aunt… do you know? Our younger brother, you know…

> **Atthai (F):** *Aamaa…*<br>
> Yes…

> **Amma (F):** *Ennamo prachanai… nethu ketaen.*<br>
> Some problem… I heard yesterday.

> **Atthai (F):** *Appidiyaa?! Yaaru sonnaanga? Thambi sonnaangalaa?*<br>
> Is that so?! Who said? Did brother say?

> **Amma (F):** *Illa… veliya sonnaanga. Niraiya alaichal.*<br>
> No… they said outside. Lots of running around.

> **Atthai (F):** *Kashtam… thambi kitta keteengalaa?*<br>
> Tough… did you ask brother?

> **Amma (F):** *Aamaa, thambi kitta ketaen. Aamaa-nu sonnaanga.*<br>
> Yes, I asked brother. He said yes.

*[SFX: A floorboard creaks. The nephew steps into the kitchen doorway. The chopping stops. The women look up, their heavy tone vanishing instantly.]*

> **Nephew (M):** *Yaaru?*<br>
> Who?

*[Pause. Amma's voice becomes immediately soft and tender.]*

> **Amma (F):** *Vaa… inga vaa. Ukkaaru.*<br>
> Come… come here. Sit.

> **Nephew (M):** *Yaaru? Enna prachanai?*<br>
> Who? What problem?

> **Amma (F):** *Namma thambi irukkaanla… avanukku ennamo prachanai.*<br>
> Our younger brother, you know… he has some problem.

> **Nephew (M):** *Thambi sonnaangalaa?*<br>
> Did brother say?

> **Amma (F):** *Illa… nethu veliya ketaen.*<br>
> No… I heard outside yesterday.

> **Nephew (M):** *Thambi kitta keteengalaa?*<br>
> Did you ask brother?

> **Amma (F):** *Aamaa… thambi kitta ketaen. Konjam alaichal-nu sonnaanga.*<br>
> Yes… I asked brother. He said a bit of running around.

> **Atthai (F):** *Aamaa. Adhaan konjam kavalai. Aanaa bayam illa… paravaayilla. Seekkiram sariyaayidum.*<br>
> Yes. That's why it's a little worry. But no fear… it's fine. It will get better soon.

> **Amma (F):** *Sari sari… ippo konjam thanni kudi.*<br>
> Okay okay… now drink some water.
