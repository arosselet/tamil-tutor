**Narrator (M):** You push open the heavy front door, kicking off your formal sandals. The house is quiet, but you know they are waiting for the report. You and your cousin exchange a look—the mission was a total success.

*[SFX: Sound of heavy wooden door closing, keys rattling on a glass table]*

**Host B (M):** *Naanga vandhuttom! Athai, maama... naanga vandhom!*<br>We have arrived! Aunt, uncle... we came!

**Host A (F):** *[Laughing] Mission accomplished. Appuram, kalyanam romba nalla irundhuchu.*<br>Mission accomplished. And then, the wedding was very good.

**Narrator (M):** Your aunt calls out from the kitchen, asking how you two managed the crowd at the wedding hall. You lean against the wall, pride in your voice.

**Host B (M):** *Sema kootam theriyuma. Aana, naanga ulla ponom.*<br>It was a huge crowd, you know. But, we went inside.

**Host A (F):** *Exactly. First, the food. Saapaadu neraiya irundhuchu.*<br>Exactly. First, the food. There was a lot of food.

**Host B (M):** *And it was so spicy! Konjam kaaram, aana naanga saappittom.*<br>And it was so spicy! A little spicy, but we ate.

*[Pause: 1.5 sec]*

**Narrator (M):** You describe the chaos of the seating area—the heat, the noise, the relatives looking for a reason to complain.

**Host A (F):** *It was such a hassle. Sariyana alaichal.*<br>It was such a hassle. A total grind.

**Host B (M):** *But we handled the seating chart like pros. Anga akka irundhaanga. Naanga avanga kitta ponom.*<br>But we handled the seating chart like pros. Elder sister was there. We went to her.

**Host A (F):** *We found the best spot right under the fan. Appuram, anga irundhu ellaathaiyum paarthom.*<br>We found the best spot right under the fan. And then, we saw everything from there.

**Narrator (M):** You tell her how you spotted the groom’s side trying to hoard the sweets, and how you intervened.

**Host B (M):** *We didn't let them take everything! Naanga sariya pannom.*<br>We didn't let them take everything! We did it right.

**Host A (F):** *We got the extra sweets for our table. Naanga adhai pannom!*<br>We got the extra sweets for our table. We did that!

*[Pause: 1.0 sec]*

**Narrator (M):** Your uncle asks if you saw the jewelry everyone was gossiping about. 

**Host B (M):** *Oh, we saw it. Naanga paarthom. Romba nalla irundhuchu.*<br>Oh, we saw it. We saw. It was very good.

**Host A (F):** *But the best part? We escaped before the heavy rain started. Naanga veettuku vandhom!*<br>But the best part? We escaped before the heavy rain started. We came home!

**Narrator (M):** You collapse onto the sofa, the pride of a job well done washing over you. You went, you ate, you conquered.

*[SFX: Distant sound of rain starting outside]*

*[Pause: 2.0 sec]*
