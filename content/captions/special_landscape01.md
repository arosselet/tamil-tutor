# Captions — The Landscape Tape · Tamil Machines, the Whole Map

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

---

*[SFX: Papers settling on a table — two voices lean in.]*

> **Maya:** *This one's different.*

> **Raj:** *Different how?*

> **Maya:** *No scene. No intercept. Just the map. The whole landscape. ~30 machines.*

> **Raj:** *Aama, that's fair. It's time to actually look at what's been built.*<br>
> Yes (*aama*) — time to read the map.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Person tails first. The last syllable IS the pronoun — that's the whole principle.*

> **Raj:** *-en is "I." Saappittéen — I ate. Vareen — I'm coming. Sonnéen — I said. The verb carries its own subject.*<br>
> -én tail = I. *சாப்பிட்டேன், வரேன், சொன்னேன்.*

> **Maya:** *-aanga is "they" — or one respected elder. Vandhaanga, sonnaanga. The deference is baked into the grammar.*<br>
> -ஆங்க tail = they/respected elder. *வந்தாங்க, சொன்னாங்க.*

> **Raj:** *-chu is "it." Aachu — it happened. Mudinjuchu — it wrapped up. The world did something on its own.*<br>
> -சு tail = it. *ஆச்சு, முடிஞ்சுச்சு.*

> **Maya:** *In-progress: -om is "we." Ponom, saaptom. The trip is a "we" trip — this tail is going to matter.*<br>
> -ஓம் tail = we (in-progress). *போனோம், சாப்பிட்டோம்.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *And -nga — the polite "you" tail. Not yet formally installed. But it's already everywhere. Sollunga, ukkarunga. Preview for now.*<br>
> -ங்க tail = polite you (preview only). *சொல்லுங்க, உக்காருங்க.*

> **Maya:** *The law though — the real law.*

> **Raj:** *Sollu.*<br>
> Tell me. (*சொல்லு*)

> **Maya:** *A -ttu word — saappittu, poyittu — is a comma. "Having done that, having eaten, having gone..." The chain keeps building. The tail is the period.*<br>
> *-ட்டு* / *-ட்டு* = comma (participle chain). The tail = the period. Land every sentence on a tail.

> **Raj:** *Land every sentence on a tail. The wind-up needs the throw. You can't stop mid-chain and leave it hanging.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Ask machines. Section two. Venum versus kudunga — this is the heart of it.*<br>
> *வேணும்* vs *குடுங்க* — the heart of the ask section.

> **Raj:** *Venum announces to the universe. "Tea venum!" The ceiling is the addressee. The need is filed with the room; the room will handle it.*<br>
> *வேணும்* = I want/need (announces to the room, no one person asked).

> **Maya:** *Kudunga is directed. "Oru tea kudunga" — I'm asking you. Specifically. That's trust in a single syllable.*<br>
> *குடுங்க* = give/pass me (asks a specific person). ஒரு tea குடுங்க.

> **Raj:** *The whole table runs on kudunga. Family members quietly passing things to each other all evening before anyone even announces.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *The polite lever — verb plus -nga — is in-progress as a named machine. But it's already active. Kudunga, sollunga, niruttunga. One syllable politens every verb.*<br>
> verb + -ங்க = polite form (in-progress as named machine). *குடுங்க, சொல்லுங்க, நிறுத்துங்க.*

> **Raj:** *Three question frames, all firing.*

> **Maya:** *Enga? — where is something? Bus stop enga?*<br>
> *எங்க?* = where? *Bus stop எங்க?*

> **Raj:** *Irukka? — is there, does it exist? Coffee irukka? Neram irukka?*<br>
> *இருக்கா?* = is there? *Coffee இருக்கா? நேரம் இருக்கா?*

> **Maya:** *Evvalo? — how much?*<br>
> *எவ்வளோ?* = how much?

> **Raj:** *Two previews — pakkathula ___ irukka? for nearby, and verb plus laama? for "may I, shall we?" — ukkaralaama?, polaama? These are coming.*<br>
> Preview only: *பக்கத்துல ___ இருக்கா?* (nearby); verb + *லாமா?* (may I / shall we?) — *உக்கரலாமா?, போலாமா?*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Must and can. Section three. Verb plus -num — must. Poganum, pananum, sollanum. In-progress.*<br>
> verb + *-னும்* = must (in-progress). *போகணும், பண்ணணும், சொல்லணும்.*

> **Raj:** *The can machines — mudiyala, can't right now. And mudiyaadhu — no chance, ever. That's a harder door.*<br>
> *முடியல* = can't. *முடியாது* = can't ever (stronger).

> **Maya:** *And -idum — the hands-off future. Sariyaagidum. It'll sort itself out. A particular kind of Tamil shrug.*<br>
> verb + *-இடும்* = it'll sort itself. *சரியாயிடும்.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *The root lore. Mudi.*<br>
> *முடி* = the root.

> **Maya:** *Mudi means finish. Mudinjuchu — it finished. Mudichittén — I finished it.*<br>
> *முடி* = finish. *முடிஞ்சுச்சு* it finished. *முடிச்சிட்டேன்* I finished it.

> **Raj:** *So mudiyum — "can" — is literally "it'll come to completion." And mudiyaadhu — "can't" — is "it will never finish itself."*<br>
> *முடியும்* = can (it'll complete). *முடியாது* = can't (it won't finish).

> **Maya:** *Tamil doesn't say you lack ability. It says the thing won't finish.*

> **Raj:** *Adhuthaan. Adhu personal illa. The completion fails. Not the person.*<br>
> *அதுதான்* = that's exactly it. *அது personal இல்ல* = it's not personal.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Done machines. Two families.*

> **Raj:** *Aachu family — the it-tail. Aachu, poachu, mudinjuchu. Things that completed themselves. Neram aachu — time's up. Pasi poachu — hunger's gone.*<br>
> *ஆச்சு* family (it-tail): *ஆச்சு, போச்சு, முடிஞ்சுச்சு, நேரம் ாச்சு, பசி போச்சு.*

> **Maya:** *Indha family fires fully.*<br>
> This family (*இந்த family*) is fully firing.

> **Raj:** *And verb plus -ttu — the completive click. Vandhuttén — I'm here, done, settled. Saaptuttén — ate, done. Mudichittu vareen — I'll wrap up and come. In-progress.*<br>
> verb + *-ட்டு* completive: *வந்துட்டேன், சாப்பிட்டுட்டேன், முடிச்சிட்டு வரேன்.*

> **Maya:** *And the goodbye.*

> **Raj:** *Poyittu vareen. "I'll go and come back." Coimbatore doesn't say goodbye. It says I'll return.*<br>
> *போயிட்டு வரேன்* = the goodbye. "I'll go and come back" — the return is assumed.

> **Maya:** *(quietly) Aama. The return is assumed.*<br>
> *ஆமா.* Yes.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *Time and manner. -ureen versus -veen.*<br>
> *-உரேன்* (now) vs *-வேன்* (later).

> **Maya:** *Pooreen — going right now. Pooveen — I'll go later. The present-future contrast baked into the tail. Same verb, different ending, different time.*<br>
> *போறேன்* (now) ↔ *போவேன்* (later). Same verb, tail changes.

> **Raj:** *Noun plus -la — in, at, on. Canada-la, veetla, phone-la. The placement marker. Firing solid.*<br>
> noun + *-ல* = in/at/on. *Canada-ல, வீட்ல, phone-ல.*

> **Maya:** *The adverb frame — X plus -aa — makes anything "-ly." Nalla, medhuvaa, seekkiramaa. Preview only — not yet a named machine. But it's in the ear from a hundred encounters.*<br>
> Preview: X + *-ஆ* = makes adverbs. *நல்லா, மெதுவா, சீக்கிரமா.*

> **Raj:** *And verb plus -ndhaa, illannaa — if and otherwise. Illannaa ennaagidum? In-progress — the shape is heard.*<br>
> verb + *-ந்தா* / *இல்லன்னா* = if / otherwise. *இல்லன்னா என்னாகும்?*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *The antifreeze kit. This is different from vocabulary — it's a survival tier.*

> **Raj:** *Puriyala — I don't understand. Firing.*<br>
> *புரியல* = I don't understand. Firing.

> **Maya:** *Konjam medhuvaa sollunga — a little slower please. In-progress. A fired repair line is a pass. Every single time. Freezing is the only fail.*<br>
> *கொஞ்சம் மெதுவா சொல்லுங்க* = say it slower. A fired repair line = a pass.

> **Raj:** *Oru nimisham — one moment. Theriyaadhu — I don't know. Paravaayilla — it's okay. All firing. These five are the circuit breakers.*<br>
> *ஒரு நிமிஷம்* one moment · *தெரியாது* I don't know · *பரவாயில்ல* it's okay.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Backchannels. The seat-holders.*

> **Raj:** *Without them the other person talks into silence. Aama aama — yeah, keep going. Seri seri — right right. Appadiyaa?! — oh really. In-progress.*<br>
> *ஆமா ஆமா* yeah keep going · *சரி சரி* right right · *அப்படியா?!* oh really (in-progress).

> **Maya:** *Adhaan — exactly, that's the thing. Appuram? Veera enna? — and then? what else? They invite more. They say: keep going, I'm here.*<br>
> *அதான்* exactly · *அப்புறம்?* and then? · *வேற என்ன?* what else?

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *The table.*

> **Maya:** *Romba nallarukku. The melt line. Praise beats asking — seconds arrive unasked. That's the whole tactic, built into one phrase.*<br>
> *ரொம்ப நல்லாருக்கு* = THE melt line. Praise → seconds arrive unasked.

> **Raj:** *Suvaiyaa irukku — it's tasty. Podhum, saaptuttén — enough, I've eaten, done. Vayiru full — add it for effect.*<br>
> *சுவையா இருக்கு* tasty · *போதும், சாப்பிட்டுட்டேன்* enough, done · *வயிறு full* stomach full.

> **Maya:** *Neenga saapittingalaa? — asking her back. That one question is a full move. It turns the whole table.*<br>
> *நீங்க சாப்பிட்டீங்களா?* = did you eat? (asking her back — the whole move).

> **Raj:** *Konjam thanni kudunga — water, please. Inga niruttunga — stop right here, for a driver.*<br>
> *கொஞ்சம் தண்ணி குடுங்க* water please · *இங்க நிறுத்துங்க* stop here.

> **Maya:** *And then —*

> **Raj:** *Poyittu vareen.*<br>
> *போயிட்டு வரேன்* — the goodbye.

> **Maya:** *Always the goodbye.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *Thirty machines. That's the whole landscape.*

> **Maya:** *Every machine is one pattern times every verb you own. One machine firing — that's fifty sentences. Not memorized. Just the machine and the verb slots.*

> **Raj:** *The lessons install them. Indha map — it just shows the terrain.*<br>
> *இந்த map* — this map shows the terrain. The lessons install the machines.

> **Maya:** *The map fills in as you go.*

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

*[SFX: A quiet settle — the map laid down, the conversation complete.]*
