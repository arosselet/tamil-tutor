# Captions — Ep 70 · The Hostage Situation

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.<br>Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Raj, I survived a hostage situation yesterday.*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *A hostage situation? In Coimbatore?*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Yes. At my aunt's house.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Athai veetla.*<br>At my aunt's house.

*[SFX: Low suspenseful cinematic drone]*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Ah. Say no more.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *I was already incredibly late for my train. My bag was packed.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Athai kitta, "neram aachu"nnu sonnen.*<br>I told my aunt, "It's getting late."

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *You tried the gentle approach.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Exactly. I told her I had to leave immediately.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Station-ukku poganum"nu sonnen.*<br>I told her I had to go to the station.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *And she completely ignored you?*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Completely.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Avangalukku puriyala.*<br>She didn't understand.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Or maybe she just didn't care. She just pointed at a chair.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Ukkarunga, ukkarunga"nnu sonnanga.*<br>She said, "Sit down, sit down."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *The classic trap. First they make you sit.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Right! And then out comes the food.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Konjam saappidunga"nnu sonnanga.*<br>She said, "Eat a little."

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Let me guess. You tried to explain your schedule again.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *I did! I was practically begging.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Enakku romba avasaram irukku"nnu sonnen.*<br>I said, "I am in a huge hurry."

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Station-ukku poganum!"*<br>"I have to go to the station!"

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Did it work?*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *No! My uncle joined in.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Appuram maama sonnanga.*<br>Then my uncle spoke up.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Onnu mattum saappidunga"nnu sonnanga.*<br>He said, "Just eat one."

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Just one. It's never just one idli, Maya.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *I know! The more I said "avasaram irukku", the more they said "saappidunga".*<br>I know! The more I said "I'm in a hurry", the more they said "eat".

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *A classic standoff. So what did you do?*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *I got so desperate, I actually lied.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *I looked at the food and panicked.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *"Enakku idli pidikkaathu"nnu sonnen.*<br>I said, "I don't like idli."

*[Pause: 2 sec]*
*[SFX: Record scratch]*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *You told a South Indian aunt you don't like idli?*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *Periya thappu, Maya.*<br>Big mistake, Maya.

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *I know! It made it worse.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Avangalukku kovam.*<br>They got angry.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *So how did you finally escape?*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *I just grabbed my bag.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Avasarama veliya ponen.*<br>I went out in a hurry.

*[SFX: Door slam]*

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *You just ran?*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Vera enna? What else could I do?*<br>What else? What else could I do?

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Nera station-ukku ponen.*<br>I went straight to the station.

> **Analyst Raj (M):** *That is amazing.*

> **Analyst Maya (F):** *Aama. Vanthutten. Safe and sound.*<br>Yes. I arrived. Safe and sound.
