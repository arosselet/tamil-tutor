# Captions — Ep 82 · The Unexpected Festival

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

> **Narrator (M):** You step out of the taxi into the humid night air of Coimbatore. The gate creaks as you push it open. After years of being a voice on a WhatsApp call, you are finally standing on the porch. Your Aunt—your Atthai—swings the door open before you can even knock.

> **Atthai (F):** **Ennappa! Ippadhan varreengala? Vaanga, vaanga!**
> Hey! Are you only just coming now? Come in, come in!

> **Narrator (M):** You drop your bags and give her a wide smile.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Atthai, vandhutten!**
> Aunt, I've arrived!

> **Atthai (F):** **Eppadi irukkeenga? Romba naal-aachu!**
> How are you? It’s been a long time!

> **Protagonist (M):** **Naan nalla irukkeen. Romba naal-aachu Atthai.**
> I am doing well. It’s been a long time, Aunt.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Narrator (M):** The house smells of jasmine and roasting spices. Your Maama—your uncle—wanders out from the kitchen, wiping his hands on a towel. He looks you up and down, amazed you actually made it.

> **Maama (M):** **Canadavula romba kulir-nu kelvi-patten. Inga eppadi irukku?**
> I heard it’s very cold in Canada. How is it here?

> **Protagonist (M):** **Anga romba kulir Maama. Naanga nethu dhaan vandhom. Ippo Coimbatore-la irukkom!**
> It’s very cold there, Uncle. We only arrived yesterday. Now we are in Coimbatore!

> **Maama (M):** **Romba sandhosham. Ooru eppadi irukkunnu paarunga.**
> Very happy. See how the town is.

> **Narrator (M):** You follow them into the dining room. The table is a battlefield of bowls and banana leaves. The heat from the sambar hits your face. You head for the sink.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Kai kazhuvittu varreen.**
> I'll wash my hands and come.

> **Atthai (F):** **Seekkiram vaanga. Inga utkaarndhu pesunga.**
> Come quickly. Sit here and talk.

*[SFX: Sound of water running, then chair dragging on a tile floor]*

> **Narrator (M):** They pile your leaf high. Rice, poriyal, and a heap of chicken curry. Your Atthai hovers with a ladle like a threat.

> **Atthai (F):** **Innum konjam saapidunga.**
> Eat a little more.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Atthai, naane eduthukkaren!**
> Aunt, I will take it myself!

> **Maama (M):** **Kaaram eppadi irukku?**
> How is the spice level?

> **Protagonist (M):** **Kaaram paravailla Maama, pazhagippochu. Idhellam enakku pudikkum.**
> The spice is fine Uncle, I'm used to it. I like all of this.

> **Narrator (M):** You take a huge bite. It’s significantly spicier than anything in Toronto, but you don't care.

> **Atthai (F):** **Konjam thanni kudungappa avanukku.**
> Give him some water, pa.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Nandri Maama.**
> Thanks, Uncle.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Narrator (M):** You eat until you can barely move. Atthai approaches with a third helping of biryani. You raise your hands in defense.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Vendaamma, vayiru neranjiduchu!**
> No, ma, my stomach is full!

> **Maama (M):** **Paravaillaye, Tamizh nalla pesureenga.**
> Not bad, you speak Tamil well.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Konjam konjam puriyudhu Maama. Ippo naan Coimbatore-kaaran maadhiri aayitten!**
> I understand a little bit, Uncle. Now I’ve become like a Coimbatore man!

> **Atthai (F):** **Sari, inime-dhaan mukkiyamaana vishayam irukku. Appuram paarkkalaamnu solla mudiyaadhu.**
> Okay, but from now on is the important thing. You can't say "we'll see later."

> **Narrator (M):** Something in her tone changes. The "Delight" is still there, but there’s a twinkle of a secret. The turn is coming. She pulls out a calendar.

> **Atthai (F):** **Adutha vaaram ungalukku pirandhanaal, theriyuma?**
> Next week is your birthday, do you know?

> **Protagonist (M):** **Aama Atthai, theriyume.**
> Yes Aunt, I know.

> **Atthai (F):** **Annaikku oru periya vishesham irukku.**
> There is a big event on that day.

> **Narrator (M):** You look at Maama. He’s suddenly very busy looking at his phone.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Visheshama? Atthai, avanga enna sonnaanga?**
> An event? Aunt, what did they say?

> **Atthai (F):** **Avanga onnum sollala. Naanga dhaan mudivu pannom. Oorla irukkira ellaarukkum solliyaachu.**
> They didn't say anything. We are the ones who decided. Everyone in the village has already been told.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Ellaaruma? Enna Atthai solreenga?**
> Everyone? Aunt, what are you saying?

> **Maama (M):** **Summa solraangannu ninaikkaadheenga. Periya pandhal poda porom.**
> Don't think she's just saying it. We are going to put up a huge marquee.

> **Narrator (M):** You realize your "quiet trip home" has just turned into a hundred-person village event. You’ve been home for two hours and you’ve already been "volunteered" for a festival.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Enakku onnum theriyaadhu!**
> I don't know anything about this!

> **Atthai (F):** **Ippo therinjiruchulla? Nalla irunga. Naalaikku neera kadaikki poganum, pattu vetti edukka!**
> You know now, right? Be well. Tomorrow we must go straight to the shop to get a silk dhoti!

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Narrator (M):** You sit on the porch, listening to the crickets and the distant sound of a Coimbatore bus horn. The surprise is out. You came for a rest, but the "village" has other plans.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Sari Atthai... Appuram paarkkalaam.**
> Okay Aunt... See you later.

*[SFX: Distant night sounds of Coimbatore fading out]*
