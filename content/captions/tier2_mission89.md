# Captions — Ep 89 · The Repair at the Office

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: The ambient roar of a crowded government office. Muffled fans, the rhythmic thud of a rubber stamp, and a low ceiling of overlapping conversations.]*

> **Narrator (M):** You are standing in a humid, crowded government office. The queue behind you is pressing in, and the clerk behind the glass is not looking at you. He’s looking at his phone, then at his colleague, then back at you. You need to know exactly when to come back for your permit.

> **Narrator (M):** If you miss the date, you start the whole month over. The clerk starts talking, but he isn’t talking about your permit. He’s starting a gossip-train.

> **Clerk (M):** **Namma Sumathi irukkanga-la... avanga pona vaaram oru phone vaanginaanga.**
<br>You know our Sumathi, right? She bought a phone last week.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Narrator (M):** "You know Sumathi, right? She bought a phone last week." He’s speaking Coimbatore-fast, burying the process in the chatter. You don't know who Sumathi is. You don't care about the phone. But the queue is watching. You want to look like you belong here. You decide to bluff.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Aama aama. Appadiyaa?!**
<br>Yes, yes. Is that so?!

> **Clerk (M):** **Aama, adhaan naalaikku varanum, sari-ya?**
<br>Yeah, that's why you have to come tomorrow, okay?

> **Protagonist (M):** **Sari sari. Aama. Appadiyaa?!**
<br>Okay, okay. Yes. Is that so?!

> **Clerk (M):** *[SFX: Loud, final THUD of a stamp]* **Adutha aalu! Next!**
<br>Next person! Next!

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Narrator (M):** You walk out into the sunlight, feeling like a local. You navigated the office. You said the right things. 

> **Narrator (M):** But three seconds later, the panic hits. *Naalaikku*—tomorrow? Or did he say *adutha vaaram*—next week? You realized you were so busy saying "Appadiyaa" to look smart that you didn't hear a single instruction. You bluffed, and now you’re lost.

> **Narrator (M):** Let’s try that again. 

*[SFX: Rewind sound—a sharp digital zip back to the office noise.]*

> **Narrator (M):** You are back at the glass. The clerk leans in, eyes shifting to his friend in the next booth. 

> **Clerk (M):** **Namma Sumathi irukkanga-la... avanga pona vaaram oru phone vaanginaanga.**
<br>You know our Sumathi, right? She bought a phone last week.

> **Narrator (M):** There it is. The "You-know-her-right?" trap. The social pressure to nod along is huge. But this time, you defy it. You break the bluff.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Yaaru? Puriyala.**
<br>Who? I don't understand.

> **Clerk (M):** *[SFX: Sound of clerk pausing, slightly annoyed]* **Sumathi-nga! Avanga naalaikku varala, adhaan neenga naalaikku varanum.**
<br>It's Sumathi! She isn't coming tomorrow, that's why you have to come tomorrow.

> **Narrator (M):** He’s still too fast. It’s a blur of syllables. The queue behind you groans, but you don't budge. You force the repair.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Oru nimisham. Konjam medhuva sollunga.**
<br>One minute. Please say it a little slowly.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Clerk (M):** *[SFX: Sighing]* **Sari. Medhuva solren.**
<br>Okay. I'll say it slowly.

> **Clerk (M):** **Naalaikku... kaalaila... varanum. Puriyudha?**
<br>Tomorrow... in the morning... you must come. Understand?

> **Narrator (M):** You’ve got the date. But you need to be certain. One last check to make sure the bluff is dead.

> **Protagonist (M):** **Enna sonneenga? Innoru thadava sollunga.**
<br>What did you say? Please say it one more time.

> **Clerk (M):** **Naalaikku varanum!**
<br>You have to come tomorrow!

> **Narrator (M):** You look at him. He looks at you. You didn't look like a pro, and you definitely didn't look cool. 

> **Protagonist (M):** **Sari-nga. Naalaikku varren.**
<br>Okay (respectful). I will come tomorrow.

> **Narrator (M):** You walk out. You have the permit date. You have your pride. You traded a moment of social awkwardness for the actual information you needed. 

> **Narrator (M):** In this office, the repair is the only thing that works.

*[SFX: Final office ambiance fades into a single, clean stamp thud.]*
