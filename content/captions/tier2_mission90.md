# Captions — Ep 90 · The One Who Handles Everything

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: chairs scraping into a circle, a wobbly fan going thuk, thuk, thuk, family voices overlapping]*

> **Host A:** *Okay, the whole gang's dragged their chairs into one big circle — and somebody's fan is making that awful sound already.*

> **Host B:** *And the suitcase from the wedding is still sitting up in the loft since morning. Nobody's touched it.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Watch this. Before anyone even finishes the sentence —*

> **Host B:** *Naan paathukkaren!*<br>I'll take care of it!

> **Host A:** *— see? He's already up, already got the fan in his hands.*

> **Host B:** *Indha fan naan paathukkaren. Rendu nimisham maattum.*<br>This fan, I'll handle it. Just two minutes.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *This is Mama. He cannot let a task pass him by. It's not even helpfulness at this point — it's a whole personality.*

> **Host B:** *Maama, adhu avasiyam illa. Naanga pannikkarom.*<br>Uncle, that's not necessary. We'll do it.

> **Host A:** *He waves that off before she even finishes.*

> **Host B:** *Illa illa. Idhu avasiyam. Naan seiya mudiyum.*<br>No no. This is necessary. I can do it.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Now watch — one task done, he's already halfway up toward the loft for the suitcase.*

> **Host B:** *Pettiyum naan thaan thookaren. Keezha kondu varren.*<br>I'll carry the suitcase too. I'll bring it down.

> **Host A:** *Somebody protests — the box is heavy, let the young ones do it.*

> **Host B:** *Avasiyam illaiyaa? Appadeengalaa? Naan seiya mudiyum thaan!*<br>Not necessary? Is that so? I certainly can do it!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *And now, mid-lift, the story starts. Every family has this uncle, and every family knows exactly where this is going.*

> **Host B:** *Adhanaal thaan — naan oru thadava, kalyaanathula, thanni vandhuchu —*<br>That's exactly why — this one time, at a wedding, water came —

> **Host A:** *Here we go. This is the flood story. It grows a little every year.*

> **Host B:** *Thanni perisaa irundhuchu! Naane ellaathaiyum paathukkitten.*<br>The water was huge! I handled everything myself.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Bigger every telling. Last year it was ankle-deep. This year —*

> **Host B:** *Muzhu veedum thanni! Aanaa naan paathukkaren illaiyaa, adhanaal thaan onnum aagala!*<br>The whole house was flooded! But I handled it, right, that's exactly why nothing happened!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *The room's laughing along, half believing it. Then the cousin, gently, not even looking up from her tea —*

> **Host B:** *Maama... appadi illa.*<br>Uncle... it wasn't like that.

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Dead silence for half a second. Everyone's holding their breath — is he going to get worked up about it?*

> **Host B:** *[laughs] Sari sari… chinnadhaa thaan irundhuchu. Aanaa naan thaan paathukkitten, adhu unmai!*<br>[laughs] Okay okay… it was just small. But I did handle it, that's true!

> **Host A:** *And there it is — story shrinks, pride stays exactly where it was.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host B:** *Petti keezha vandhuduchu! Paarunga, appadiye irukku — onnum aagala!*<br>The suitcase made it down! Look, it's just as it was — nothing happened!

> **Host A:** *Box survives the trip down, nothing broken, and he's already looking around for the next thing to grab.*

> **Host B:** *Innum enna irukku? Naan paathukkaren!*<br>What else is there? I'll handle it!

> **Host A:** *Somebody, please, just let the man sit down for five minutes.*

*[SFX: laughter rising over the circle of chairs, the fan clicking off — fixed, or at least quiet for now]*
