# Captions — Ep 58 · Five Lines at the Auto Stand

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: auto stand — idling engines, a horn blast, a driver shouting a destination]*

> **B:** *sari, kelu. innaiku auto stand-la oru scene paathen.*<br>
> Okay, listen. Today I saw a scene at the auto stand.

> **A:** *ennaachu?*<br>
> What happened?

> **B:** *Canada-lerndhu vandha oru mapillai — auto venum-nu ninnaaru.*<br>
> A son-in-law visiting from Canada — he stood there wanting an auto.

> **A:** *aama, appuram?*<br>
> Yeah, and then?

> **B:** *driver seekkiram seekkiram kettaan — "enga poreenga? route sollunga! seekkiram!"*<br>
> The driver fired away, fast-fast — "Where are you going? Tell me the route! Quickly!"

*(pause)*

> **A:** *ada. driver machine gun maadhiri pesuvaanga — panic varuvadhu normal-dhaan.*<br>
> Oh man. Drivers talk like a machine gun — panicking is only normal.

> **B:** *naanum appadi-dhaan ninaichen. aana — avaru "konjam medhuva sollunga"-nnu sonnaaru.*<br>
> I thought so too. But — he said: **"say it a bit slower, please."**

*(pause)*

> **A:** *konjam medhuva sollunga?*<br>
> "Say it a bit slower"?

> **B:** *aama! driver medhuva aanaan. aana marupadiyum kettaan.*<br>
> Yes! The driver slowed down. But he asked again.

> **A:** *mapillai-kitta purinjadha?*<br>
> Did the mapillai understand?

> **B:** *puriyala. adhaan — "enna sonneenga?"-nnu kettaaru.*<br>
> He didn't. So — he asked: **"what did you say?"**

*(pause)*

> **A:** *ada!*<br>
> Oh!

> **B:** *driver marupadiyum sonnaan. indha thadava medhuva sonnaan.*<br>
> The driver said it again. This time he said it slowly.

> **A:** *purinjudha?*<br>
> Did he get it?

> **B:** *konjam. aana — "innoru thadava sollunga"-nnu kettaaru.*<br>
> A little. But — he asked: **"say it one more time."**

*(pause)*

> **A:** *moonu thadava kettaara?!*<br>
> He asked three times?!

> **B:** *moonu thadava. naan ninaichen driver kovam varuvaan-nu.*<br>
> Three times. I thought the driver would get angry.

> **B:** *aana illa — nalla sonnaan, nalla sonnaan.*<br>
> But no — he said it kindly, said it kindly.

*(pause)*

> **A:** *appuram?*<br>
> Then?

> **B:** *ippo best part kelu. mapillai driver-kitta kettaaru — "thamizh-la eppadi solradhu?"*<br>
> Now listen to the best part. The mapillai asked the driver: **"how do you say it in Tamil?"**

*(long pause)*

> **A:** *ennadhu?!*<br>
> What?!

> **A:** *thamizh-la — eppadi solradhu-nnu — thamizh-laye kettaara?*<br>
> He asked — how to say it — in Tamil itself?!

> **B:** *thamizh-laye kettaaru. driver medhuva sonnaan. mapillai repeat panninaaru.*<br>
> In Tamil itself. The driver said it slowly. The mapillai repeated it.

*(pause)*

> **B:** *driver-oda face paakkanum — "appadiya?!"-nnu sonnaan.*<br>
> You should have seen the driver's face — he said: **"oh, really?!"**

> **A:** *appadiya?!*<br>
> Oh really?!

> **B:** *adhu mariyaadhai. "ivaru try panraaru"-nnu purinjudhu avanukku.*<br>
> That's respect. He understood: "this man is *trying*."

*(pause)*

> **A:** *auto kidaichadha?*<br>
> Did he get the auto?

> **B:** *kidaichudhu. mapillai-ye vandhu sonnaanga — annaave rate kammi panninaan-nu.*<br>
> He got it. The mapillai himself came and told us — the driver even cut the rate.

> **A:** *andha five lines-laye oru power irukku.*<br>
> There's a power in those five lines themselves.

---

## Breakdown

> **Maya:** *"thamizh-la eppadi solradhu?" — indha question-la oru twist irukku.*<br>
> "How do you say it in Tamil?" — there's a twist in this question.

> **Raj:** *aama. thamizh-la kettu, thamizh-la eppadi solradhu-nnu kekkuraaru. That loop — that stopped the driver cold.*<br>
> Yes. Asking *in Tamil* how to say something in Tamil.

> **Maya:** It's a meta-question. Asking in Tamil how to say something in Tamil. The driver didn't expect that circuit.

*(pause)*

> **Raj:** *Coimbatore-la oru vishayam — yaaro try panraanga-nnu therinjaa… driver maarittaan.*<br>
> One thing about Coimbatore — when people see someone's trying, they respond differently. The driver changed.

> **Maya:** *And look at the sequence. konjam medhuva sollunga — slow down. enna sonneenga? — what did you say? innoru thadava sollunga — again. ovvoru line-um konjam time kodukkudhu.*<br>
> …and every one of those lines buys a little time.

*(pause)*

> **Raj:** *panic-aagala. ovvoru step-um calm-aa, steady-aa.*<br>
> No panic. Every step calm, steady.

> **Maya:** *appadiya?!* — not mockery. The driver read it as something real. Commitment.

> **Raj:** *andha kit words mattum illa. "naan inga irukken, help pannunga"-nnu solradhu adhu. auto stand-la adhu podhum.*<br>
> It's not just the kit words. It says: "I'm here, help me out." At the auto stand, that's enough.

*(pause)*

> **Maya:** *adhaan.*<br>
> Exactly.
