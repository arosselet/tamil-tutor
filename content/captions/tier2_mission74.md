# Captions — Ep 74 · The Departure Trap

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

> **Maya:** *Today, we are analyzing a very specific kind of danger. The Departure Trap.*

> **Raj:** *Oh, the Indian Auntie Departure Trap. This is legendary family lore.*

> **Maya:** *It happens when someone is running late. The bags are packed. They are standing at the door. And then, she strikes.*

> **Raj:** *Namma anna irukkan-la...*<br>You know our brother...

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *You know our brother. He walked right into this trap last week.*

> **Maya:** *He should have known better. What happened?*

> **Raj:** *So, he had a train to catch. He told our aunt,*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Station-ukku poganum."*<br>"I need to go to the station."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *"Station-ukku poganum."*<br>"I need to go to the station."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Using that -kku poganum frame. I need to go to the station.*

> **Raj:** *Right. And he tried to make it sound urgent. He said,*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Avasaram irukku."*<br>"I am in a hurry."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *"Avasaram irukku."*<br>"I am in a hurry."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Avasaram is a hurry, or an emergency. I am in a hurry.*

> **Raj:** *But here's the thing about this specific aunt. She physically blocks the door.*

*[SFX: plate clinking]*

> **Raj:** *And she's holding a plate of hot idlis.*

> **Maya:** *The weapon of choice.*

> **Raj:** *She looks at his packed bags. She hears*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Avasaram irukku."*<br>"I am in a hurry."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *And she just says,*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Innum konjam saappidunga."*<br>"Eat a little more."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *"Eat a little more."*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *It's delivered so sweetly, but it is an absolute command.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *"Innum konjam saappidunga."*<br>"Eat a little more."

> **Raj:** *It's a battle of wills! He says,*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Illa illa, vayiru full-aa irukku."*<br>"No no, stomach is full."

> **Maya:** *Ah, he added the -aa sound to "full."*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Full-aa.*<br>Full-ly.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *He turned it into an adverb. My stomach is full-ly.*

> **Raj:** *He's trying to be polite!*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Vayiru full-aa irukku."*<br>"Stomach is full."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *But she doesn't move. So he panics and tells a terrible lie.*

> **Maya:** *What did he say?*

> **Raj:** *He says,*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Enakku idli pidikkathu."*<br>"I don't like idlis."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *"Pidikkathu?"*<br>"Don't like?"

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *I don't like idlis? To an auntie in Coimbatore? That's not just a lie, that's an insult.*

> **Raj:** *I know! It was pure panic.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Station-ukku poganum!"*<br>"I need to go to the station!"

> **Maya:** *Did she let him go?*

> **Raj:** *Of course not. She just smiled and said,*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Idli super-aa irukku. Innum konjam saappidunga."*<br>"Idli is super. Eat a little more."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *She used the -aa right back at him.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *"Super-aa irukku."*<br>"It is super."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *It is super-ly good. Eat a little more.*

> **Raj:** *It's flawless logic. You are in a hurry? Eat faster.*

> **Maya:** *So how did it end?*

> **Raj:** *He ate three more idlis. And then...*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *"Auto-kku poganum, avasaram irukku," -nnu sonnaanga.*<br>"I need to go to the auto, I'm in a hurry," they said.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *"I need to go to the auto, I'm in a hurry," they said.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *"-nnu sonnaanga."*<br>"...they said."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *He survived, but he definitely missed that train.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *Maya, next time you are at the door, just run. Don't even say goodbye.*
