# Captions — Ep 91 · Ten Minutes Before the Car

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: a kitchen at full boil — a pressure cooker beginning its climb, a spoon scraping a pan, then a phone ringtone cutting through, answered fast.]*

> **Host A:** *Aamaa akka, sollunga!*<br>
> Yes akka, tell me!

> **Host A:** *Anga venaam — anga vaiyen!*<br>
> Not there — put it there!

*[SFX: a zipper catching, dragging, stuck]*

> **Host B:** *Athai... bag-ai thooka mudiyala!*<br>
> Auntie... I can't lift the bag!

> **Host A:** *Oru nimisham akka. Naan paathukkaren — nee poi bag paaru!*<br>
> One minute akka. I'll handle it — you go look at the bag!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host B:** *Athai, ippodhaan vandhen — kaaru velila vandhuduchu!*<br>
> Auntie, I just got here — the car's already outside!

> **Host A:** *Sari sari... paththu nimisham irukku, paravaayilla.*<br>
> Okay okay... there's ten minutes, it's fine.

*[SFX: chopping, a sizzle]*

> **Host B:** *Ey, romba vaasanaiyaa irukku! Enna samaikkureenga?*<br>
> Hey, it smells so good! What are you cooking?

> **Host B:** *Samaiyal innum mudiyalaiyaa?*<br>
> Isn't the cooking done yet?

> **Host A:** *Innum konjam samaikkuren! Poi bag zip pannu!*<br>
> I'm cooking a little more! Go zip the bag!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Every time it's like this, akka.*

> **Host A:** *Ovvoru thadavaiyum ippaditthaan... kadaisi paththu nimisham ippaditthaan odum.*<br>
> It's like this every single time... the last ten minutes always run like this.

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Nee poanukku munnaadi time confirm pannittiyaa?*<br>
> Did you confirm the time before you left?

> **Host B:** *Athai, kaappi kudunga poara munnaadi!*<br>
> Auntie, give me coffee before we go!

> **Host A:** *Ippo kudukkaren, oru nimisham!*<br>
> I'm giving it now, one minute!

*[SFX: a small container lid clicking open]*

> **Host B:** *Antha mittaai packet paatikku kidaichuthaa?*<br>
> Did grandma get that candy packet?

> **Host A:** *Sinna container-la vachirukken... kudu avanga kitta!*<br>
> I kept it in the small container... give it to her!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host B:** *Innum konjam saapidunga, athai!*<br>
> Eat a little more, auntie!

> **Host A:** *Venaamma, vayiru niraiyinjiduchu — neramum illa!*<br>
> No thanks, I'm full — and there's no time!

*[SFX: a car horn, distant]*

> **Host B:** *Athai, kaar vandhuduchu!*<br>
> Auntie, the car's here!

> **Host A:** *Akka, oru nimisham.*<br>
> Akka, one minute.

> **Host A:** *Idhu enna velai ippo? Yaaru ticket paathukittu irukkiyaa?*<br>
> What's going on now? Who's been watching the ticket?

> **Host B:** *Naan thaan... confirm aagala innum!*<br>
> It's me... it hasn't confirmed yet!

> **Host A:** *Kudu, naane paakkaren!*<br>
> Give it here, I'll check myself!

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Aamaa, aachu. Confirm aayiduchu.*<br>
> Yes, done. It's confirmed.

*[SFX: a zipper finally closing]*

> **Host B:** *Mudinjuduchu! Bag redi!*<br>
> It's done! Bag's ready!

> **Host A:** *Akka... ketkuthaa?*<br>
> Akka... can you hear me?

> **Host A:** *Ovvoru thadavaiyum ippaditthaan poarom, aanaa...*<br>
> We go like this every single time, but...

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Sari, summaa solranga ippo poara neram illannu... aanaa unmaithaan.*<br>
> Okay, they just say there's no time to go now... but it's true.

> **Host A:** *Varen akka, paathukko.*<br>
> I'm off now akka, take care.

*[SFX: a door opening, footsteps toward it]*

> **Host B:** *Athai, vaanga! Kaar nikkuthu!*<br>
> Auntie, come on! The car's waiting!

> **Host A:** *Varen!*<br>
> Coming!

> **Host A:** *Vaikkaren akka... adutha thadavai seekiram vaa!*<br>
> I'm hanging up akka... come sooner next time!

*[SFX: door closing, a car door shutting, an engine pulling away. Silence.]*

*[Pause: 3 sec]*

> **Maya:** *That kitchen was pure chaos right up until the door shut.*

> **Raj:** *The thing that got me — every single person asks her a question, and she never actually answers it head-on. It's always what her hands are doing.*

> **Maya:** *Right — "Enna samaikkureenga?" — and she just says "samaikkuren." Doesn't even name the dish.*<br>
> "What are you cooking?" — and she just says "I'm cooking."

> **Raj:** *Same with the coffee. Nobody gets a straight answer, they get "kudukkaren." She's not stopping to explain — she's just moving.*<br>
> "I'm giving it."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Very athai energy, honestly. Ten minutes to a flight, nobody's got time for nouns.*

> **Raj:** *Nouns can wait.*

> **Maya:** *That line to her sister got me though — "Ovvoru thadavaiyum ippaditthaan." Every single time, it's like this.*

> **Raj:** *Yeah. She says it so fast, almost buries it under the noise. But it's there.*

> **Maya:** *And then she catches herself — "summaa solranga" — like she's talking herself out of feeling it, mid-suitcase.*<br>
> "they just say."

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *That's such a real move. Push the goodbye down till the car's actually outside.*

> **Maya:** *And ten seconds later she's a completely different person on that phone — "vaikkaren, vaa!"*<br>
> "I'm hanging up, come!"

> **Raj:** *That's the whole house in one phone call.*
