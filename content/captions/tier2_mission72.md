# Captions — Tier 2, Mission 72: ஒரு வாய் சாப்பிடுங்க

> **Host A (F):** *Nee kettiya? Avan train vittuttaan.*

> **Host B (M):** *Enna? Epudi?*
> What? How?

> **Host A (F):** *Amma vida maattaanga. "Oru vaay saapidungka"-nu — adu oru vaay illa, oru plate aaitu.*
> Amma wouldn't let him go. "Just one bite" — turned into a full plate.

> **Host B (M):** *Aama aama. Anda veetla idhe maadiri nadakkum.*
> Yeah yeah. That's how it goes in that house.

> **Host A (F):** *Avan bag edututu kadhavu kitta ninnan. "Avasaram irukku, station-ku poganum"-nu sonnaan.*
> He stood at the door with his bag. Said "I'm in a hurry, I need to go to the station."

> **Host B (M):** *Sariyana sonnaan. Adukku enna aachu?*
> He said exactly the right thing. What happened?

> **Host A (F):** *Amma sonnanga — "Ukkarunga."*
> Amma said — "Sit down."

> **Host B (M):** [playing the boy] *Avasaram irukku. Naan poganum. Station-ku poganum.*
> I'm in a hurry. I have to go. I need to go to the station.

> **Host A (F):** [as Amma] *Oru nimisham irungka, maapilla.*
> Just one moment, son-in-law.

> **Host B (M):** [boy] *Illa, neram aachu! Naan poganum!*
> No, time's up! I have to go!

> **Host A (F):** [Amma] *Ukkarunga. Innum konjam saapidungka.*
> Sit down. Eat just a little more.

> **Host B (M):** [boy, louder] *Station-ku poganum! Train neram aachu!*
> I need to go to the station! The train time is up!

> **Host A (F):** [Amma, serving food] *Konjam rice... konjam kuzhambu... indha.*
> A little rice... a little gravy... here.

> **Host B (M):** [boy] *Podhum, podhum! Naan...*
> Enough, enough! I...

> **Host A (F):** [Amma] *Oru vaay mattum.*
> Just one bite.

> **Host B (M):** [breaking scene] *Idu comedy-aa? Tragedy-aa?*
> Is this comedy? Or tragedy?

> **Host A (F):** *Coimbatore-la idu comedy. Veliya irundhu paatthaa.*
> In Coimbatore this is comedy. If you're watching from outside.

> **Host B (M):** [boy] *Naan pogalaama? Avasaram romba irukku.*
> Can I go? I'm really in a hurry.

> **Host A (F):** [Amma] *Konjam thanni kudungka.*
> Have a little water.

> **Host B (M):** [quiet defeat] *Avanga kettakave illa...*
> She didn't even hear me...

> **Host A (F):** *"Pogalaama"-nu kettaa, anda veetla "thanni kudungka"-nu padhil varum.*
> Ask "can I go?" in that house and the answer is "have some water."

> **Host B (M):** [boy, bag in hand] *Okay. Naan poganum. Auto veliya nikkudu. Station-ku poganum.*
> Okay. I have to go. Auto is waiting outside. I need to go to the station.

> **Host A (F):** [Amma, calling out] *Appa! Maapilla poganum-nu sollraanga!*
> Appa! Son-in-law says he needs to go!

> **Host B (M):** [as Thatha] *Enna! Coffee kudikkalaya? Sollungka, enna venum?*
> What! You haven't had coffee? Tell me, what do you want?

> **Host B (M):** [boy] *Enakku onnnum vendam. Naan poganum!*
> I don't want anything. I have to go!

> **Host A (F):** [Thatha] *Ukkarunga ukkarunga. Train-ku poganum-nu sollriya? Evvalavu manikku?*
> Sit down sit down. You say you need to catch the train? What time?

> **Host B (M):** [boy] *Patthukku!*
> At ten!

> **Host A (F):** [Thatha] *Patthukka. Ippo ombadhu naarpattiyanju aachu. Konjam nillungka.*
> At ten? It's already nine forty-five. Wait a moment.

> **Host B (M):** [out of scene] *Anju nimisham! Station oru kilometre thoorum irukku!*
> Five minutes! The station is a kilometre away!

> **Host A (F):** *Avanga calculate panni — "Anju nimisham irukkay"-nu sollittaanga!*
> They calculated and said "you've still got five minutes"!

> **Host B (M):** [boy] *Avasaram irukku! Naan poganum, please...*
> I'm in a hurry! I have to go, please...

> **Host A (F):** [Amma from kitchen] *Maapilla! Oru vaay saapidungka-nu sonnen-la? Innum saapidala!*
> Son-in-law! I said just one bite, didn't I? You still haven't eaten!

> **Host B (M):** *Avanugalukku "poganum" puriyala. "Avasaram irukku" puriyala.*
> They don't understand "I have to go." They don't understand "I'm in a hurry."

> **Host A (F):** *Onnne onnu puriyum.*
> One thing they understand.

> **Host A (F):** *"Saapidungka."*
> "Eat."

> **Host B (M):** [eating one bite, resigned] *Sari. Oru vaay saapittten. Naan poganum ippo. Station-ku poganum.*
> Okay. I had one bite. I have to go now. I need to go to the station.

> **Host A (F):** [Amma] *Aama aama. Pongka pongka. Aanaa — konjam kuzhambu?*
> Yes yes. Go go. But — a little gravy?

> **Host A (F):** *Idu — no exit. No exit!*
> This is — no exit. No exit!

> **Host B (M):** *Train vittuttaan.*
> He missed his train.

> **Host A (F):** *Avasaram irukku-nu sonnaalum... station-ku poganum-nu sonnaalum...*
> Even if you say I'm in a hurry... even if you say I need to go to the station...

> **Host B (M):** *... "Innum konjam saapidungka."*
> ... "Eat just a little more."

> **Host A (F):** *Avan romba nallaa irukku-nu sonnaan, poga-um podhu.*
> He said "it's really good" as he was leaving.

> **Host B (M):** *Aamaa. Adu thaappu.*
> Yeah. That was a mistake.

> **Host A (F):** *Adhaanaal thaanda seconds vandhuchi.*
> That's exactly why the seconds came.
