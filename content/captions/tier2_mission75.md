# Captions — Ep 75 · The Person Toggle

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: Home afternoon — ceiling fan, cups clinking in the next room]*

> **A:** *namma Ramesh irukkaan-la… ennamo prachanai-aam.*<br>
> You know our Ramesh, right… apparently there's some kind of problem.

> **A:** *yaaro sonnaaangga — naanum ketten. Exactly ennannu theriyala.*<br>
> Someone said something — I heard it too. Not sure exactly what.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

*[SFX: Footsteps, a door opening]*

> **B:** *[entering] enna prachanai?*<br>
> What's the problem?

> **A:** *[low] theriyala! Just ketten — yaaro sonnaaangga, avvlothan.*<br>
> I don't know! I just heard it — someone said so, that's all.

> **B:** *yaaru kitta kettingga?*<br>
> Who did you ask?

> **A:** *[checking] akka kitta ketten. aana akkaavum properly theriyala-nnu sonnaaangga.*<br>
> I asked Akka. But Akka said she didn't properly know either.

*[Pause: 0.5 sec]*

> **B:** *[curious] akka yaaru kitta kettaaangga?*<br>
> Who did Akka ask?

> **A:** *… adhu theriyala.*<br>
> … I don't know that.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **B:** *So Ramesh kitta yaarum ner-la kekkala?*<br>
> So nobody actually asked Ramesh directly?

> **A:** *[sighing] aamaangga! naangga elaarum kettom. aana avanga kitta yaarum kekkala!*<br>
> Yes! We all heard about it. But nobody asked him!

*[Pause: 0.5 sec]*

> **B:** *[starting to laugh] ennamo prachanai-nnu kettom, aana…*<br>
> We heard there's some problem, but…

> **A:** *[laughing] ketkave prachanai-aa aachu!*<br>
> Even the asking has become a problem!

*[SFX: Both laughing, fan humming on]*
