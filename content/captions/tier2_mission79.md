# Captions — Ep 79 · Sunday Family Lunch

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

> **Andrew (M):** *Sunday family lunch. We’re all crammed around a small dining table. My aunt—athai—is serving, orchestrating the meal like a battlefield commander. Her food is amazing, and her pride in it is absolute.*

*[SFX: Clinking of steel plates, noisy dining room chatter]*

> **Andrew (M):** *I’m pinned in the corner. Everything is out of reach. The rule at this table is: you don't reach across, you ask someone to pass it. The chicken gravy is incredible, but man, it is kaaram. Pure fire. The water jug is right next to my cousin, Siva.*

> **Andrew (M):** *Siva, konjam thanni kudunga.*<br>
> Siva, give me some water.

*[SFX: Water pouring into a steel tumbler]*

> **Aunt (F):** *[From across the table] thambi! nethu enga poneenga?*<br>
> Younger brother! Where did you go yesterday?

*[Pause]*

> **Andrew (M):** *The room is so loud, I can barely hear her over the clatter.*

> **Andrew (M):** *enna sonneenga?*<br>
> What did you say?

> **Aunt (F):** *nethu evening! phone pannen, aana edukala. enga poneenga?*<br>
> Yesterday evening! I called on the phone, but you didn't pick up. Where did you go?

> **Andrew (M):** *naanga veliya ponom.*<br>
> We went out.

> **Andrew (M):** *To the new restaurant on the main road.*

> **Aunt (F):** *epadi? saapaadu nalla irundhucha?*<br>
> How was it? Was the food good?

> **Andrew (M):** *It’s a trap. She’s definitely testing me.*

> **Andrew (M):** *romba nalla irundhuchu. aana, unga samayaldhaan best!*<br>
> It was very good. But, your cooking is the best!

> **Aunt (F):** *apadi sollunga! indha periya piece vai!*<br>
> Say it like that! Keep this big piece!

> **Andrew (M):** *sari sari. romba pidichiruku!*<br>
> Okay okay. I like it very much!

> **Andrew (M):** *And I mean it. adhukapuram... the spice kicks in. Another wave of heat.*

> **Andrew (M):** *athai, romba pidichiruku... aana konjam kaaram.*<br>
> Aunt, I like it very much... but it's a little spicy.

> **Aunt (F):** *apadi illa! kaaram kammidhaan. naan paathudhaan potten.*<br>
> It's not like that! The spice is low. I made it carefully.

> **Andrew (M):** *She refuses to accept it's spicy. My mouth is burning. I desperately need something plain to balance it out. The rice bowl is near Siva.*

> **Andrew (M):** *Siva, konjam saadham kudunga.*<br>
> Siva, give me some rice.

> **Andrew (M):** *I mix the rice to cut the heat, but it needs something else.*

> **Andrew (M):** *andha uppu kudunga.*<br>
> Give me that salt.

> **Andrew (M):** *I take a breath, managing the spice. Across the table, there's a tray of cut cucumbers.*

> **Andrew (M):** *Siva, andha plate-a kudunga.*<br>
> Siva, give me that plate.

> **Aunt (F):** *thambi... innum konjam saapidunga.*<br>
> Younger brother... eat a little more.

> **Andrew (M):** *The ultimate test of the guest. She is walking toward me with the gravy bucket. If I leave any opening, she will serve another full round. I have to shut it down firmly but respectfully.*

> **Andrew (M):** *vendaamma, vayiru full.*<br>
> No need ma, stomach is full.

> **Aunt (F):** *ey, paravalla, konjama vai?*<br>
> Hey, no problem, keep a little bit?

> **Andrew (M):** *vendaam, vayiru nirainjiduchu!*<br>
> No need, stomach is full!

> **Andrew (M):** *I nod politely and lean back from the leaf. She smiles, satisfied. The battle is won.*
