# Captions — Ep 93 · The Safe Question

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: afternoon quiet, a fan turning slowly]*

> **Host A:** *So — quiet day. What's on your mind?*

> **Host B:** *My cousin called today. Afternoon, out of nowhere.*

> **Host A:** *The one you haven't spoken to in a while?*

> **Host B:** *Yeah. Nobody said anything was wrong. It just... went quiet between us. [Pause: 1 sec] Weeks of it.*

> **Host A:** *And today she broke it.*

> **Host B:** *Sort of. Listen — this is how it went.*

*[SFX: phone rings twice, then picks up]*

> **Host B:** *Halo, akka.*
> <br>
> Hello, akka (older sister).

> **Host A:** *Thambi! Eppadi irukka?*
> <br>
> Younger brother! How are you?

> **Host B:** *Nalla irukken akka. Neenga?*
> <br>
> I'm doing well, akka. You?

> **Host A:** *Naanum nallathaan. [Pause: 1 sec] Niraya naala pesala naam.*
> <br>
> I'm well too. We haven't talked in a long time.

> **Host A:** *Enna aachu?*
> <br>
> What happened?

> **Host B:** *Onnum illa akka. work jaasthiya irunthuchu.*
> <br>
> Nothing, akka. Work's been a lot.

> **Host A:** *Sari... [Pause: 1 sec] Enna samaikkira?*
> <br>
> Okay... what are you cooking?

> **Host B:** *Samaikkuren akka, saambaar maadhiri edho.*
> <br>
> I'm cooking, akka — something like sambar.

> **Host A:** *Appadiyaa. Namma amma recipe thaane?*
> <br>
> Is that so. Our mother's recipe, right?

> **Host B:** *Aamaa akka.*
> <br>
> Yes, akka.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Namma pakkathu paatti irukkaarla... theriyumaa, avanga grandchild vandhirukkaamaam.*
> <br>
> You know our neighbor grandma... apparently her grandchild has come to visit.

> **Host B:** *Aamaa, kelvippatten.*
> <br>
> Yeah, I heard.

> **Host A:** *Summa solranga, unmaiyo illaiyo theriyala.*
> <br>
> That's just what people are saying — don't know if it's true.

> **Host B:** *Amma inga irukkaarla... illa, irukkala, veliye poyirukkanga.*
> <br>
> Is mom here... no, she's not, she's gone out.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host A:** *[Pause: 1 sec] Thambi... yenna naan ninachen...*
> <br>
> Younger brother... because I thought...

> **Host B:** *Illa akka, appadi illa. [Pause: 1 sec] Ippo pesa mudiyala, samaikkura neram.*
> <br>
> No akka, it's not like that. Can't talk now, it's cooking time.

> **Host A:** *Sari sari, paravaayilla. [Pause: 1 sec] Enna samaikkira?*
> <br>
> Okay okay, it's fine. What are you cooking?

> **Host B:** *Kaapi poduren akka, porunga vekkuren.*
> <br>
> I'm making coffee, akka — wait, I'll put it on.

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Host A:** *Sari thambi... innoru thadava ketkuren. Enna samaikkira?*
> <br>
> Okay, younger brother... I'll ask one more time. What are you cooking?

> **Host B:** *Samaikkuren akka... [Pause: 1 sec] Kaaram kaaramaa, namma ooru maadhiri.*
> <br>
> I'm cooking, akka... spicy, like back home.

> **Host A:** *[laughs softly] Un kai ruchi innum athe maadhiri irukkaa?*
> <br>
> Is your hand's taste still the same?

> **Host B:** *Theriyala akka, neenga vandhu saapittu sollunga.*
> <br>
> I don't know, akka — come eat and tell me.

> **Host A:** *Vaaikku nalla irukkumnu nambaren. [Pause: 2 sec] Sari... naan varen, adutha vaaram.*
> <br>
> I'm hoping it'll taste good. Okay... I'll come, next week.

> **Host B:** *Vaanga akka. [Pause: 1 sec] Poyittu varen.*
> <br>
> Come, akka. [Pause: 1 sec] I'll go and come back.

*[SFX: phone clicks off]*

> **Host A:** *[Pause: 1 sec] She never actually said "I'm still upset," did she.*

> **Host B:** *No. Neither did I.*

> **Host A:** *Three times, same question.*

> **Host B:** *[Pause: 1 sec] That was the whole call, really.*

---

> **Analyst Maya:** *Okay, the thing that got me — she asks "Enna samaikkira?" three separate times. Same words, every time.*

> **Analyst Raj:** *And he never once just says the dish name and leaves it there.*

> **Analyst Maya:** *Right, it's always the verb first — "Samaikkuren," then whatever he's making trails after. If he'd just said "Saambaar" and stopped—*

> **Analyst Raj:** *—that's the door shutting. Answering with the noun alone would've meant "don't ask me anything real."*

> **Analyst Maya:** *So the question was never about food.*

> **Analyst Raj:** *[Pause: 1 sec] The line I keep coming back to is "Un kai ruchi innum athe maadhiri irukkaa?" — literally, is your hand's taste still the same. That's not a recipe question either.*

> **Analyst Maya:** *It's "are you still you." [Pause: 1 sec] Nobody apologized once in that whole call.*

> **Analyst Raj:** *Didn't need to. She called. He picked up. That's the whole apology.*
