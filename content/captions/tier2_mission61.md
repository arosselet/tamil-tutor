# Captions — Ep 61 · The Hassle Humblebrag

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[Sighs heavily]*

> **Raj:** *Maya, I am completely exhausted today.*

> **Maya:** *Ennachu? What is going on?*<br>What happened? What is going on?

> **Raj:** *We have a big family kalyanam coming up. And it is just non-stop work. Namma mama irukkarla...*<br>We have a big family wedding coming up. And it is just non-stop work. You know our uncle, right...

*[Laughs]*

> **Maya:** *Sari, here we go. The classic gossip opener. You start with namma—our—then the person, and end it with a la. Namma mama irukkarla... "You know our uncle, right..."*

> **Raj:** *Aama, exactly. Namma mama irukkarla... He called today and gave me so many tasks. I had to move all these old boxes. Pazhaiya petti... periya petti! I tried to move them, but thookka mudiyala!*<br>Yes, exactly. You know our uncle, right... He called today and gave me so many tasks. I had to move all these old boxes. Old box... big box! I tried to move them, but I couldn't lift them!

> **Maya:** *So you are dealing with the classic family alaichal.*

> **Raj:** *Alaichal. Exactly. The hassle. The endless running around. It is just so much alaichal. Everything was a rush. Suddenly neram aachu. My whole day, just gone. Tired aachu.*<br>Hassle. Exactly. The hassle. The endless running around. It is just so much hassle. Everything was a rush. Suddenly time passed. My whole day, just gone. I got tired.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Maya:** *Can I tell you a secret about this though? In a Tamil extended family?*

> **Raj:** *What? That I should just tell them mudiyathu?*

> **Maya:** *Illa, Raj. Complaining about all this alaichal... it is actually a humblebrag.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Raj:** *Wait. What? How is this exhaustion a brag?*

> **Maya:** *Because if you aren't given any alaichal... it means you aren't considered important to the family.*

*[SFX: record scratch]*

> **Raj:** *Appadiya?!*<br>Is that so?!

> **Maya:** *Aama aama! If they just look at you and say, "Raj, inga ukkarunga, nalla saappidunga"... and they don't give you any tasks? It means you are basically just a guest.*<br>Yes yes! If they just look at you and say, "Raj, sit here, eat well"... and they don't give you any tasks? It means you are basically just a guest.

> **Raj:** *Oh wow.*

> **Maya:** *Aanaa, if they give you all the alaichal? It is a badge of honor. It means you are trusted. You are part of the inner circle.*

> **Raj:** *So when I am complaining to my cousins about my alaichal...*

> **Maya:** *You are secretly just saying, "Look how much the family needs me."*

> **Raj:** *This changes everything. Now I feel like I need to do the work seekkirama—wait, seekkiram is quick, so if I add the adverb ending, it becomes seekkirama?*

> **Maya:** *Aama! The aa sound turns the word into an adverb. Seekkiram is quick, seekkirama is quickly.*

> **Raj:** *Perfect. I am going to go back and handle all my alaichal very santhoshama. Wait, santhosham is happy... so santhoshama!*

> **Maya:** *You are getting the hang of it. Enjoy your badge of honor!*
