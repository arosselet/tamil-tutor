# Captions — Ep 97 · Exact Change

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means. <br> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

*[SFX: Dinner winding down — plates, low kitchen clatter, a TV murmuring in the next room]*

> **Mama:** *Paati, neenga saapitteengala?* <br> Paati, did you eat?

> **Paati:** *Aama, romba nalla irundhuchu.* <br> Yes, it was really good.

> **Deepa:** *Paati, innum konjam venuma?* <br> Paati, do you want a little more?

> **Paati:** *Venda, Deepa. Vayiru full.* <br> No, Deepa. Stomach's full.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Paati:** *Konjam nillunga. Naan athaikku oru phone pannitu varen.* <br> Wait a bit. I'll make a call to Athai and come back.

> **Karthi:** *Ippova, Paati? Avanga veliya irukkangale.* <br> Now, Paati? She's out though.

> **Paati:** *Adhanala thaan. Ellam mudinjachaanu kekkanum.* <br> That's exactly why. I need to ask if everything's finished.

*[SFX: Phone dialing, a ring, then the click of pickup]*

> **Athai:** *Hello Paati, naan veliya irukken. Appuramaa pesalaama?* <br> Hello Paati, I'm out. Can we talk later?

> **Paati:** *Oru nimisham. Idha sollitu vekkaren.* <br> One minute. I'll just say this and hang up.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Mama:** *[quietly] Vittudu.* <br> Let it go.

> **Karthi:** *Naan onnum sollala.* <br> I didn't say anything.

> **Paati:** *Athai, veliya evlo aachu?* <br> Athai, how long have you been out?

*[Pause: 1.5 sec]*

> **Paati:** *Appadiyaa? Sari sari.* <br> Is that so? Okay, okay.

> **Paati:** *Adhukku appuram... meedhi evlo irukkunu enakku theriyum.* <br> After that... I know how much is left over.

> **Karthi:** *Paati, endha meedhi?* <br> Paati, which leftover?

> **Paati:** *Enakku ellam theriyum, Karthi.* <br> I know everything, Karthi.

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Deepa:** *Enna aachu, Paati? Yaaru adhu?* <br> What happened, Paati? Who is it?

> **Paati:** *Onnum aagala, Deepa. Naan athai kitta pesaren.* <br> Nothing happened, Deepa. I'm talking to Athai.

> **Deepa:** *Enakkum kudu! Hi sollanum!* <br> Give it to me too! I want to say hi!

> **Paati:** *Konjam nillu, Deepa.* <br> Wait a bit, Deepa.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Paati:** *Athai, naalaikku naan saapidarathukku meedhi kaasula nallathaa edhaachum vaanguga.* <br> Athai, for my meal tomorrow, buy something good with the leftover money.

> **Karthi:** *Paati, adhu... naan paakkaren.* <br> Paati, that... I'll look into it.

> **Paati:** *Appo?* <br> So?

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Mama:** *Paati, onnum illa. Sari aagidum.* <br> Paati, it's nothing. It'll be fine.

> **Paati:** *Sari aaganume, Maama. Naan summa thaan kekkuren.* <br> It needs to be fine, Mama. I'm just asking.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

*[SFX: A small scuffle — the phone changing hands]*

> **Deepa:** *Athai! Hi!*

> **Athai:** *Yendi, sollu seekiram. Naan veliya irukken.* <br> Hey, say it quickly. I'm outside.

> **Deepa:** *Athai, eppa varuva?* <br> Athai, when will you come?

> **Athai:** *Oru nimisham, Deepa. Paati kitta kudu.* <br> One minute, Deepa. Give it to Paati.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Paati:** *Athai, ippo neram aachu. Ponga.* <br> Athai, it's gotten late now. Go on.

> **Paati:** *Naalaikku eppa free-ya iruppa?* <br> When will you be free tomorrow?

> **Paati:** *Time sollu, naan confirm panren.* <br> Tell me the time, I'll confirm.

> **Paati:** *Enakku phone pannu, naan paathukkaren.* <br> Call me, I'll take care of it.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Paati:** *Sari Athai. Naalaikku pesalaam.* <br> Okay Athai. We'll talk tomorrow.

*[SFX: Phone click — the line goes dead]*

*[Pause: 2 sec]*

> **Mama:** *Sari... saapidalaama?* <br> Okay... shall we eat?

> **Paati:** *Aama, Maama. Saapidalaam.* <br> Yes, Mama. Let's eat.
