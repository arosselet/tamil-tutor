# Captions — Ep 80 · Dinner with My Aunt

> **Follow-along sheet** — the line as *sound* · what it means.
> Passes 1–2: listen with this open. Pass 3+: put it away — **blind is the win.**

> **Host (M):** *Dinner with my paternal aunt—my athai—is less of a meal and more of a royal audience.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host (M):** *She runs the dining table with absolute pride and authority. Her bizarre obsession? She always serves exactly five of everything, and she takes personal offense if you don't eat every single bite.*

> **Host (M):** *This morning, I woke up late.*

> **Host (M):** *methuva ezhunthen.*<br>I woke up slowly.

> **Host (M):** *I walked outside to join the family.*

> **Host (M):** *veliya ponen.*<br>I went outside.

*[SFX: Clinking plates and lively chatter]*

> **Host (M):** *The table was enormous and chaotic, packed with serving dishes. My athai was presiding at the head of the table.*

> **Aunt (F):** *naanga niraiya samaichom! saapidunga.*<br>We cooked a lot! Eat.

> **Host (M):** *Everything I needed was marooned at the far end of the table. I couldn't just reach across, and I definitely couldn't just announce what I wanted—that's rude here.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host (M):** *I had to ask them to pass things. First, I needed water, which was near my brother.*

> **Host (M):** *konjam thanni kudunga.*<br>Please give a little water.

> **Host (M):** *He slid it over.*

*[SFX: Glass sliding on wood]*

> **Host (M):** *Next, my food was missing salt. I looked at my sister.*

> **Host (M):** *konjam uppu kudunga.*<br>Please give a little salt.

> **Host (M):** *My aunt noticed. Her culinary pride was stung. She leaned in, eyes narrowing. They asked me what was wrong.*

> **Host (M):** *avanga kettaanga.*<br>They asked.

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host (M):** *I had to answer carefully. She loves it when people praise her bold flavors.*

> **Host (M):** *kaaram jaasthi... aanaa romba nallaa irukku.*<br>Spicy is too much... but very good.

> **Host (M):** *She beamed, satisfied. But then she noticed I hadn't taken the main dish. She pointed to a massive serving container at the end of the table.*

> **Aunt (F):** *unakku anju irukku!*<br>For you, there are five!

> **Host (M):** *I was already stuffed, and her food was fiery red. I needed to get the container away from her reach so she wouldn't force it onto my plate.*

> **Host (M):** *antha periya petti kudunga.*<br>Give that big box.

> **Host (M):** *I took it from my uncle, but served myself none.*

*[Pause: 1 sec]*

> **Host (M):** *I had to face her and refuse the food directly.*

> **Host (M):** *vendaam. vayiru full. romba kaaram pidikkaathu.*<br>I don't want. Stomach full. I don't like very spicy.

*[SFX: Sudden silence]*

> **Host (M):** *A hush fell over the table. To my athai, refusing her food is treason. I am telling you, the tension was thick.*

> **Host (M):** *solren... kovam jaasthi!*<br>I'm telling you... the anger was too much!

> **Host (M):** *I decided to make a quick exit before she could escalate the situation.*

> **Host (M):** *athukkappuram... neraa veliya ponen.*<br>After that... I went straight outside.
