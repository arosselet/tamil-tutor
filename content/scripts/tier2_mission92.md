# Tier 2, Mission 92 — The Cousin on the Line

[SFX: evening crickets, a ceiling fan's low hum]

**Host A (F):** Okay, you had that face all through dinner. What happened.

**Host B (M):** My cousin called. Three streets over, that one.

**Host A (F):** Oh no.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host B (M):** [laughs] Let me just tell it to you the way it went.

[SFX: old landline ringing, twice]

**Host B (M):** So the phone rings, right when the stove's just getting going.

**Host A (F):** Of course it is.

**Host B (M):** I pick up. First thing out of her mouth —

**Host B (M):** "நம்ம மாமா இருக்கார்ல, அவரு பேசாம இருந்தா ரொம்ப நேரம் ஆகும்."

**Host A (F):** Classic warm-up. Get you comfortable before the real question.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host B (M):** Exactly. Two minutes of that, and then —

**Host B (M):** "சரி சரி, அது சரி, இன்னிக்கு என்ன சமைக்கிற?"

**Host A (F):** There it is.

**Host B (M):** And what do I say. The easiest thing in the world.

**Host B (M):** "சாம்பார்."

**Host A (F):** One word.

**Host B (M):** One word. I thought that closed it.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host B (M):** It did not close it.

**Host B (M):** "சாம்பாரா? உன் அம்மா கை ருசி உனக்கு வந்துச்சான்னு பாக்கணும்."

**Host A (F):** [laughs] She's already inviting herself to judge it.

[SFX: a lid clatters against a pot in the background]

**Host B (M):** Right on cue, something clangs behind me, and she jumps on it —

**Host B (M):** "இது என்ன?"

**Host B (M):** "இது ரசம்."

**Host A (F):** That one's fair, though. She's just asking what's on the stove.

**Host B (M):** Sure. But two minutes later, same question comes back around —

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host B (M):** "என்ன சமைக்கிற?"

**Host A (F):** Again?!

**Host B (M):** Again. And I still hadn't learned.

**Host B (M):** "சாம்பார் தான், இன்னும் கொஞ்சம்."

**Host A (F):** Still just the dish. Still no shame.

**Host B (M):** None. So she needles harder —

**Host B (M):** "எதுக்கு இவ்ளோ நேரம் ஆகுது?"

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host B (M):** And I panic and grab the first excuse in the drawer —

**Host B (M):** "இப்போ தான் வந்தேன், அதனால தான்."

**Host A (F):** "அப்படியா?!"

**Host B (M):** [laughs] That's exactly what she said, sweet as anything, not buying one word of it.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host B (M):** So I try to just end the call —

**Host B (M):** "இப்போ பேச முடியல், பண்ணிண்டு இருக்கேன்."

**Host A (F):** Did that work.

**Host B (M):** Not even a little. I even downplay it —

**Host B (M):** "சின்ன அளவு தான், ஒண்ணும் ஸ்பெஷல் இல்ல."

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host A (F):** She's not going to let a small pot stop her.

**Host B (M):** No. One more pass.

**Host B (M):** Very gentle this time.

**Host B (M):** "சரி சரி… கடசியா, என்ன சமைக்கிற?"

**Host A (F):** Third time.

**Host B (M):** Third time. And I don't know what happened — maybe I just gave up trying to dodge it —

**Host B (M):** "சாம்பார் சமைக்குறேன்."

[Pause: 2 sec]

**Host A (F):** Oh, you actually said the whole thing.

**Host B (M):** The whole thing. And the second I said it, her voice changed.

**Host B (M):** "வாய்க்கு ரொம்ப ருசியா இருக்கும் போல!"

**Host B (M):** "சரி, நான் கொஞ்சம் நேரம் கழிச்சு வரேன்."

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host A (F):** So she just... invited herself. Straight out.

**Host B (M):** Straight out. No more circling.

**Host B (M):** The second I sounded like I actually knew what I was doing in that kitchen, she dropped the whole act.

**Host A (F):** [laughs] So she wasn't testing the sambar.

**Host B (M):** She was testing me.

[SFX: fan hum continues, fades slightly]

**Host A (F):** And did she come.

**Host B (M):** She came. Sambar wasn't even ready.

**Host A (F):** [laughs] Of course it wasn't.

---

**Host A (F):** That "kai ruchi" line she opened with — hand's taste — that's not really about the hand, is it.

**Host B (M):** No, it's the whole compliment in two words. Whoever taught you cooks through you, basically.

**Host A (F):** So it's a compliment and a test at the same time.

**Host B (M):** Every single line out of her mouth was both. Pleasant on top, digging underneath.

**Host A (F):** And she never once said what she actually wanted.

**Host B (M):** Never had to. She just kept circling the pot till I said it right.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host A (F):** Poor sambar never stood a chance.

**Host B (M):** [laughs] None at all.
