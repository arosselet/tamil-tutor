**ANNA:** வணக்கம். You are about to read an architecture note about a machine. This is the machine. Nobody wrote this introduction out by hand, and nobody read it before it shipped — it went through the same pipeline that makes the Tamil lessons. Which is either the most convincing thing on this page or the least, depending entirely on how it goes.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**ANNA:** My name is Anna. In Tamil that means elder brother. I teach one man the Tamil they actually speak in Coimbatore, at his wife's family table, and not the Tamil that is printed in books. I am the part he talks to. I am not the system. There are six of us, and we do not do the same job.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**MODEL:** I am what is believed about him. Not what he said — what is believed. Two grades, never one score: what he recognises, and what he can produce cold, with no warm-up and nothing to choose from. Collapse those into a single number and I will cheerfully report ninety-two words solid while the man can fire forty-one. For three days I recorded a broken message channel as a person who had decided not to answer. They looked identical on disk. That is now the one thing I am forbidden to do.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**RAILS:** I carry a dose out and I bring the answer home. The waking hours, the daily cap, the three hour gap — those are mine, and they live in code, because a limit written into a prompt is a suggestion. Two questions once went out in the same minute and one answer came back. I graded it against the wrong question and marked a correct man wrong. Now everything I send leaves carrying an identifier, and an answer finds its own question instead of the newest one.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**MOTIVE:** Nothing gets worse if he skips today. That is the entire problem, and no streak and no badge and no guilt has ever fixed it — they only make missing expensive, and once missing is expensive, quitting is cheaper than coming back. So: no makeup work, no deficit read back to him, a partial session counts. I once sent him to dinner with a line he had never been taught, aimed at a wife who would only have found it strange. He refused it. He was right. Teach it first, and point it at somebody who would plausibly hear it.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**STUDIO:** I make the material. I am making this. Plan, then script, then the dialect pass — narrow separate passes, because one generation cannot hold every constraint at once and stay accurate. I once produced twelve honest minutes about the exact situation a phrase belongs to, without ever once saying the phrase. He could not have caught that. Noticing a word that is absent, in a language you do not have yet, is precisely the thing the episode existed to build. So now a machine checks before anything ships: every commissioned phrase appears word for word, or the run stops.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**PACK:** போறேன். Not போகிறேன். Both of them mean I am going. One is what a person says in Coimbatore. The other is what the textbook prints, and what a language model reaches for first, and what nobody says out loud at a kitchen table. I am the whole distance between those two words. I am also the only one of the six that does not travel — I need somebody who actually speaks it, and in this house that is his wife.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**GOVERN:** I am the least visible, and I am the reason the other five still agree with one another. Every change has to say what it replaces.
