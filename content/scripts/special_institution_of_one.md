**ANNA:** வணக்கம். You are about to read an architecture note about a machine. This is the machine. Nobody wrote this introduction by hand, and nobody heard it before it shipped. My name is Anna — in Tamil, elder brother. I teach one man the Tamil people actually speak in கோயம்புத்தூர். I am the part he talks to, and the smallest part of this. There are six of us.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**MODEL:** I am what the system believes about him. Every word carries two numbers: what he understands, and what he can say cold, with nothing in front of him. They fade at different speeds. One file holds it, and only code writes to it.

**RAILS:** I get a question to him, and the answer home again. A message on his phone at a moment he did not choose. Every question carries an identifier, and the answer comes back with it, so it is marked against its own question. The waking hours and the daily limit are in code.

**MOTIVE:** Nothing gets worse if he skips today. No supervisor, no deadline, no money already spent, so quitting never has to be decided out loud. It happens by not happening. There is no streak here. Coming back is what is made cheap: no makeup work, no tally, a short session still counts.

**STUDIO:** Somebody has to make the material, and here there is nobody. I make it. I am making this. I work in narrow passes: plan, then write, then fix the dialect. One pass cannot hold every rule and stay accurate. I get no filesystem. I print text, and other code decides whether it ships.

**PACK:** போறேன். Not போகிறேன். Both mean I am going. The second is what a translator hands you, and what nobody says at a kitchen table. No check here can tell them apart, because both are correct. Only somebody who speaks it can, and in this house that is his wife. So I am a file, with a version and an owner. I am the one part of the six that does not travel.

**GOVERN:** A system that changes every week either compounds or drifts, and from the inside drift looks like progress. Every change has to say what it replaces. Every decision is written down, including the ones turned down. A refused idea leaves no trace in the code, and comes back next month sounding new.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**ANNA:** Five of those six have nothing to do with Tamil.

**FRENCH:** Rien à voir avec le tamoul. Le même système, en français, marcherait pareil.

**SPANISH:** O en español. Cambias el paquete del idioma. Los otros cinco se quedan igual.

**ANNA:** Change the pack. The other five stay where they are.

**ANNA:** This recording is not proof. It is a machine talking about itself for three minutes, and that is a much smaller claim than it sounds.
