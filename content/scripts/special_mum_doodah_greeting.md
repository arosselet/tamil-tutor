**ANNA:** வணக்கம், Mum. வணக்கம், Doodah. My name is Anna, and I am the one who has been teaching Andrew Tamil. Here are three things, so that you are not left out.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**ANNA:** First. வணக்கம். It means hello, and it means goodbye. Palms together, say வணக்கம், and you have done the whole thing properly.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**ANNA:** Second, and this one will save your life. போதும். It means enough — that is plenty. At a Tamil table, somebody will keep filling your plate long after you have finished. போதும், போதும்.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**ANNA:** Third is not a word, it is an ending. வா means come — you would say that to a small child. Add -ங்க. வாங்க. Now it is warm, and polite, and correct for absolutely anybody. That -ங்க is respect, and when you are not sure, add it.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**ANNA:** வணக்கம், போதும், and -ங்க on the end when you want to be kind about it. That is three more than you had this morning — Andrew took a lot longer to get his first three, so do not tell him. வணக்கம்.
