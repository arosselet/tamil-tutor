# Tier 2, Mission 86 — The Family That Never Decides

[SFX: A ceiling fan turning slowly. A cricket commentary murmuring from a TV in the next room. Somewhere behind it, a suitcase zip.]

**Host B (M):** நேரம் ஆச்சு. **கிளம்பலாமா?**

**Host A (F):** ஆமா ஆமா. கிளம்பலாம்.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host A (F):** ஆனா... இப்போ traffic ரொம்ப ஜாஸ்தி.

**Host B (M):** ஜாஸ்தியா?

**Host A (F):** **முடிஞ்சா** இன்னும் **பத்து** நிமிஷம். அப்புறம் கிளம்பலாம்.

**Host B (M):** சரி. **உட்காரலாமா?**

**Host A (F):** மாமா என்ன சொல்றாங்க தெரியுமா?

**Host B (M):** என்ன?

**Host A (F):** முதல்ல சாப்பிடலாம், **அப்புறம் பார்க்கலாம்**-னு.

**Host B (M):** எனக்கு **வயிறு ஃபுல்**-ஆ இருக்கு. **நேரா** கிளம்பலாம்.

**Host A (F):** கொஞ்சம் **பேசலாமா**? Bags door **பக்கம்** இருக்கு. அவசரம் இல்ல.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host B (M):** அத்தை என்ன சொன்னாங்க?

**Host A (F):** **கடைசில பாக்கலாம்**-னு சொன்னாங்க.

**Host B (M):** ...கடைசில.

[SFX: A suitcase lifted off the floor. Two footsteps. A door. Out on the road, one auto horn.]

**Host A (F):** அத்தை **பெட்டி** எடுத்துட்டாங்க.

**Host B (M):** அவங்க வெளிய போறாங்க!

**Host A (F):** ஐயோ. **சீக்கிரம் கிளம்புங்க**!

[Pause: 2 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** Eight suggestions. Zero decisions.

**Analyst Raj (M):** And one aunt with a suitcase. That is a Coimbatore departure, start to finish.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Nobody in that hall said no, Raj. Did you notice that?

**Analyst Raj (M):** Nobody said yes either. That's the whole trick.

**Analyst Maya (F):** And the entire thing ran on one tail. One. **லாம்**.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** Say it on its own, Maya. **லாம்**.

**Analyst Maya (F):** **லாம்**.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Now watch what it eats.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Take கிளம்பு. To leave, to set off.

**Analyst Raj (M):** **கிளம்ப-லாம்**. We can leave.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Take சாப்பிடு. To eat.

**Analyst Raj (M):** **சாப்பிட-லாம்**. We can eat.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Take போ. To go. **போ-லாம்**.

**Analyst Raj (M):** உட்காரு, to sit. **உட்காரலாம்**.

**Analyst Maya (F):** பேசு, to talk. **பேசலாம்**.

**Analyst Raj (M):** The verb keeps changing. **லாம்** never changes.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** And notice what it refuses to do. It never orders anybody.

**Analyst Raj (M):** It only says: this is possible.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Somebody else can decide. Not me. I only mentioned it.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Then there's one more sound, and it changes the job completely.

**Analyst Maya (F):** **ஆ**. Right at the end.

**Analyst Raj (M):** கிளம்பலாம் becomes **கிளம்பலாமா?** Shall we leave?

**Analyst Maya (F):** உட்காரலாம் becomes **உட்காரலாமா?** Shall we sit?

**Analyst Raj (M):** பேசலாம் becomes **பேசலாமா?**

**Analyst Maya (F):** Verb plus **லாம்** is a suggestion. Plus **ஆ**, and it's a question.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** My brother's boy found a third job for it. He came to the door yesterday and asked me, "நான் வெளிய **போலாமா?**"

**Analyst Maya (F):** May I go outside. Same tail — now it's permission.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Suggestion, question, permission. One machine, three jobs.

**Analyst Maya (F):** And in that hall this morning it had a fourth one. This is my favourite.

**Analyst Raj (M):** The wall.

**Analyst Maya (F):** **அப்புறம் பார்க்கலாம்.** We'll see later.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** It sounds like an open door. It's a closed one.

**Analyst Maya (F):** She wanted to stay home. She never once said she wanted to stay home.

**Analyst Raj (M):** She said traffic. She said food. She said the bags are near the door.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Every reason was about somebody else's comfort. Flawless.

**Analyst Raj (M):** And he wanted to be gone twenty minutes earlier. He never said that either.

**Analyst Maya (F):** He said **நேரா கிளம்பலாம்**. Straight out, let's go. Polite as anything.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Two people, opposite plans, and not one rude word between them.

**Analyst Maya (F):** **லாம்** did all the fighting for both of them.

**Analyst Raj (M):** And nobody moved for forty minutes. That's the **அலைச்சல்**, Maya. Not the traffic. The hall.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** So how does a family like that ever get out of the house?

**Analyst Raj (M):** Somebody picks up a bag. That's it. That's the decision.

**Analyst Maya (F):** The aunt. Who proposed nothing all morning.

**Analyst Raj (M):** In a Coimbatore house the decision is never announced. It's carried out the door.

[Pause: 2 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** Now. The word I actually want to open up. **பார்க்கலாம்**.

**Analyst Raj (M):** The polite escape hatch of the entire language.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Its root is **பார்**. To look. To see.

**Analyst Raj (M):** **பார்த்தேன்.** I saw. The same **பார்**, sitting right at the front.

**Analyst Maya (F):** So **பார்க்கலாம்** is, literally, we may see. Nothing is decided. Only the looking is promised.

**Analyst Raj (M):** English does exactly the same thing. "We'll see."

**Analyst Maya (F):** Has "we'll see" ever once meant yes? In the history of families?

**Analyst Raj (M):** Never. Not one time.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** And the one she sent out last — **கடைசில பாக்கலாம்**.

**Analyst Raj (M):** We'll see at the end. The verdict that decides nothing.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Hear what the mouth does to the ர் there. Careful and slow, it's **பார்க்கலாம்**.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Fast, in a doorway, with a bag in one hand — **பாக்கலாம்**.

**Analyst Maya (F):** **அப்புறம் பாக்கலாம்**. **கடைசில பாக்கலாம்**.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Nobody has time for that ர் when the auto is waiting.

**Analyst Maya (F):** But the long ஆ stays. It always stays. And that ஆ is about to earn its keep.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** Because there's a word that lives next door to it and means something completely different.

**Analyst Maya (F):** **பக்கம்.** Side. Direction.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Short. Flat. **பக்கம்.**

**Analyst Maya (F):** ஒன்னு, **ரெண்டு**. Two beats and it's finished.

**Analyst Raj (M):** No long ஆ. And no **பார்** anywhere inside it.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** **பக்கம்.**

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** **பாக்கலாம்.**

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** **பக்கம்.**

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** **பாக்கலாம்.**

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** One of them is a place. The other one is a plan.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Put both of them in an auto and the difference gets very loud.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Go on.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Somebody gets in and says, "temple **பக்கத்துல** நிறுத்துங்க."

**Analyst Maya (F):** Stop beside the temple. That's a place. The driver stops. Everybody is happy.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Now the same person says, "temple **பாக்கலாம்**."

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** Ah. Let's go and look at a temple.

**Analyst Raj (M):** That's not a stop. That's an outing.

**Analyst Maya (F):** He smiles, he takes the long road, and thirty minutes later somebody is standing in front of a temple wondering how they got there.

**Analyst Raj (M):** And the meter is still running.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** So — a place is **பக்கம்**. Short, flat, no ஆ.

**Analyst Raj (M):** A plan is **பாக்கலாம்**. Long, with **பார்** hiding at the front of it.

**Analyst Maya (F):** And when the word wanted is "beside", the useful one is **பக்கத்துல**.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Bus stop **பக்கத்துல**. Hotel **பக்கத்துல**.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Same **பக்கம்**, with the -ல tail on it. In, at, beside.

**Analyst Raj (M):** One tail turns a place into a location. Another tail turns a verb into a suggestion.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Tamil is mostly tails, Raj. The words just come along for the ride.

[Pause: 2 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** Right. It's nearly four o'clock. Coffee **குடிக்கலாமா?**

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** ...**கடைசில பாக்கலாம்.**

**Analyst Raj (M):** Ayyo. And that is exactly how it starts.

[SFX: The ceiling fan again, the cricket commentary swelling for a moment, then a door closing.]
