# Tier 2, Mission 62 — இடும் & பாத்துக்கறேன்: The Shrug and the Promise

[SFX: Evening veranda — tea glasses clinking, a ceiling fan turning, distant street sound]

**Host B (M):** கல்யாணம் கஷ்டமா இருக்கு.

**Host A (F):** அது சரியாயிடும், மாமா.

**Host B (M):** எனக்கு கொஞ்சம் பயமா இருக்கு.

**Host A (F):** எல்லாம் நல்லா ஆயிடும்.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host B (M):** இந்த சாப்பாடு காரமா இருக்கு. எனக்கு பிடிக்காது.

**Host A (F):** சீக்கிரம் பழகிடுவீங்க, மாமா.

**Host B (M):** (tiredly) நான் டயர்ட்டா இருக்கேன். ஆனா நீ எப்பவும் ஒரே மாதிரி சொல்றியே — "ஆயிடும், ஆயிடும்."

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Host A (F):** (softly) இல்ல மாமா. இன்னும் ஒண்ணு இருக்கு.

**Host A (F):** நான் பாத்துக்கறேன். எல்லாத்தையும் நான் பாத்துக்கறேன்.

[Pause: 2 sec]

[SFX: Veranda sounds fade]

**Analyst Maya (F):** Okay. That tea-table scene — every single worry, same response.

**Analyst Raj (M):** சரியாயிடும். It'll sort itself out.

**Analyst Maya (F):** The wedding's stressful — சரியாயிடும். She's scared — ஆயிடும். The food's too spicy — சீக்கிரம் பழகிடும்.

**Analyst Raj (M):** Not "I will fix it." Not even "someone will fix it." Just — it will happen. On its own.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** சரி, let's take that apart. -இடும். Where does that even come from?

**Analyst Raj (M):** இடு. An old auxiliary verb — "to set down," "to finish off doing." Same family as விடு.

**Analyst Maya (F):** Right — the completive. "I did the thing, and set it down. Done."

**Analyst Raj (M):** Now bolt a future ending onto that completive. முடிஞ்சு plus இடும். "It will have gotten itself finished."

**Analyst Maya (F):** A done-deal future. Fated, almost.

**Analyst Raj (M):** ஆமா. புக் பண்ணிடும். சொல்லிடும். போயிடும். Any clean, single verb — it just slots on.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** But it doesn't slot onto everything, does it.

**Analyst Raj (M):** இல்ல. Only a clean single verb. "கூட்டிட்டு வரோ" — a compound, two verbs stuck together — you can't staple -இடும் onto that.

**Analyst Maya (F):** So what happens to the messy ones?

**Analyst Raj (M):** That's exactly where பாத்துக்கறேன் steps in.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** Which is the opposite move, actually. இடும் removes the person from the sentence. பாத்துக்கறேன் puts a person right back in the middle of it.

**Analyst Raj (M):** நான் பாத்துக்கறேன். Not "it'll happen." "I will personally see to it."

**Analyst Maya (F):** பார் — to look, to see. Plus த்துக்க — doing it for yourself, on yourself.

**Analyst Raj (M):** "I will look after — for myself — to it." That's the literal shape.

**Analyst Maya (F):** அழகான வார்த்தை, that. The whole ethic of taking responsibility, sitting right inside the grammar.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** (quieter) Can I tell you something, Maya?

**Analyst Maya (F):** என்ன ஆச்சு?

**Analyst Raj (M):** That vignette — that's exactly how my grandmother used to sound. என் பாட்டி.

**Analyst Maya (F):** சரியாயிடும்?

**Analyst Raj (M):** ஆமா. Every worry, every single evening — she'd wave it off the same way. சரியாயிடும்.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** She said that to you.

**Analyst Raj (M):** எல்லா நாளும் சொன்னாங்க. Every single day of my childhood.

[Pause: 2 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** (gently) She's not around anymore, is she.

**Analyst Raj (M):** இல்ல. Four years now.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Raj (M):** ஆனா — in the last year, something changed. She couldn't say "சரியாயிடும்" anymore and mean it.

**Analyst Maya (F):** So what did she say instead?

**Analyst Raj (M):** She stopped shrugging. என் அம்மா கிட்ட ஒரு நாள் சொன்னாங்க — "நான் பாத்துக்கறேன்."

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** She moved from letting go — to holding on.

**Analyst Raj (M):** ஆமா. From "it'll fix itself" to "I will fix it." Right at the end.

[Pause: 2 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** And now?

**Analyst Raj (M):** (small laugh) இப்போ நான் தான் அது சொல்ற ஆளு. Last month, my mother's knee was hurting, worrying about a doctor's bill —

**Analyst Raj (M):** and it just came out of me. "அம்மா, நான் பாத்துக்கறேன்."

**Analyst Maya (F):** The word found you.

**Analyst Raj (M):** It waited for me, actually.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**Analyst Maya (F):** So somewhere in that tea-table scene we heard — someone grows up to say the same word, one day.

**Analyst Raj (M):** எப்பவும் அப்படித்தான் நடக்கும். The shrug runs out. Someone always has to actually pick it up.

**Analyst Maya (F):** இடும் lets you rest. பாத்துக்கறேன் is what you say when you're done resting.

**Analyst Raj (M):** (quiet, warm) அழகான, இல்லையா?

**Analyst Maya (F):** ஆமா. A beautiful shift.

[Pause: 1 sec]

[SFX: Evening veranda ambiance briefly returns — a glass set down gently — then fades]
