**ENGLISH_HOST:** Tamil. Eighty million speakers, older than Latin — and if you grew up in Canada, you have exactly none of it. Good. Zero is the honest place to start. You'll be talking back to aunties in under a minute.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**TAMIL_GUEST:** At a Tamil dinner table, refusing food is a martial art. Your maama — your uncle — piles a second scoop onto your plate. You say: போதும். Enough.

**TAMIL_HOST:** He ignores you. Everyone ignores you. Third scoop. போதும், போதும்!

**TAMIL_GUEST:** And when it's delicious — it is always delicious — you say: ரொம்ப நல்லா இருக்கு. Really, really good. Praise, by the way, works better than refusal. Praise is the exit visa.

**TAMIL_HOST:** So. The aunty reaches for the rice spoon. Your plate is full. What do you say?

[Pause: 2 sec]

**TAMIL_GUEST:** போதும். You've been learning Tamil for forty seconds, and you already talk back to aunties.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**ENGLISH_HOST:** That worked because every line sat one step past what you held — and you held nothing. That's the whole idea. A coach that knows exactly which words you have, and builds one step past them, every day. And in some languages, you aren't starting from nothing.

**FRENCH_HOST:** Le français, par exemple. Neuf ans à l'école. Le vocabulaire est encore là, dans votre tête — il dort, c'est tout.

**FRENCH_GUEST:** Nine years of French class. Still in there. Asleep, not gone.

**FRENCH_HOST:** Et vous venez de le prouver — vous m'avez comprise, sans traduction.

**FRENCH_GUEST:** You understood her just now, no translation. The words were never the problem. The problem is the half-second search — when the waiter in Montréal is standing there, and the kitchen is busy.

**ENGLISH_HOST:** And when he answers you in English, he isn't judging you. He heard the search, and he did the polite thing — he met you at your level. That's all anyone ever does. The reps exist to move the level, until the politeness comes back in French.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**ENGLISH_HOST:** It's not an app you remember to open. The coach reaches you first — your phone buzzes: one line, real situation, answer in the language. Judged, scored, gone, eight seconds. A podcast feed soaks what you strained in chat this morning. And it tracks one honest number: of everything you recognize, how much can you fire cold — out loud, no warm-up, no multiple choice. No streaks. No guilt. No owl.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**SPANISH_HOST:** ¿Y si tienes un viaje a México en tres semanas? No necesitas perfección — necesitas cincuenta frases que funcionan.

**SPANISH_GUEST:** Fifty phrases that work, burned down against a real date. A deadline changes the math, and the system counts what fires cold right up to the boarding gate.

[Pause: 1 sec]

**TAMIL_HOST:** This one wasn't built as a product. It was built for one seat at a Tamil dinner table, by the one who sat there silent — tired of being the ghost at it. இனிமே நான் பேசுவேன்.

**TAMIL_GUEST:** "From now on, I'll speak." That was the whole mission statement. The repository is open — bring your own language, your own dinner table, your own level.

**FRENCH_HOST:** Et la prochaine fois, à Montréal — peut-être qu'il vous répondra en français. On y va?

**TAMIL_HOST:** போலாமா?

[Pause: 1 sec]
