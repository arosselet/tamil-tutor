*This is the entire gap between 29/34 and 34/34. Everything else in survival is done.*

| ◐ | **innoru thadava sollunga** | இன்னொரு தடவ சொல்லுங்க | Say it once more |
| ◐ | **mudinja** | முடிஞ்சா | If possible / if you can — the one-word softener |

| ○ | **aama aama** | ஆமா ஆமா | Yeah, yeah — the backchannel that **holds your seat** while someone keeps talking |

**ஆமா ஆமா is the one to want.** It is the cheapest item left and it does the most
work: it is what you say when you have followed maybe half of it and want the other
person to keep going instead of stopping to check on you.

## 8. 👂 The ear — never produce these, just catch them

*Catch is **3/12** and it is the starving axis. Your ear is ahead of your mouth: you
take tone off a line before you take the words. These are the ones to let wash over you.*

| | Hear it | Script | It means |
|---|---|---|---|
| ✅ | **jasthi** | ஜாஸ்தி | Too much / extra |
| ◐ | **vittudu** | விட்டுடு | Drop it, let it go — *this is where a story gets killed* |
| 👂 | **summa solraanga** | சும்மா சொல்றாங்க | They're just talking / making it up |
| 👂 | **ennamo prachanai** | என்னமோ பிரச்சனை | Some problem or other |
| 👂 | **theriyuma?** | தெரியுமா? | You know…? — a real gossip opener |
| 👂 | **alaichal** | அலைச்சல் | The hassle, the running-around |
| 👂 | **namma X irukkaan-la…** | நம்ம X இருக்கான்ல… | "You know our X, right…" — **THE** gossip opener |
| 👂 | **-னு சொன்னாங்க** | frame:quote-nu | "…she said that" — every reported line runs on it |
| 👂 | **-ஆம்** | frame:hearsay-aam | "apparently / they say" |
| 👂 | **-தான் / -ஏ / -உம் / -ஓ** | frame:spice-dhaan | the sass clitics — just, -self, even, some-or-other |
| 👂 | **-த்துக்க** | frame:myself-thukka | do-it-for-myself — எடுத்துக்கறேன், பாத்துக்கோங்க |

> **The known shape of your ear:** you decode the *middle* of an exchange and miss
> where it **lands**. *நீ சொல்றது புரியுது* read as confusion when it was surrender;
> *seri seri, vittudu* heard as filler when it was the topic being shut down.
> On this trip: when a story ends, ask yourself who won.

## 10. In the air, and on landing

**Loaded for the flight:** three long-haul tapes (**room** 51 min, **machines** 29 min,
**inventory** 26 min), **M86 — The Family That Never Decides** (7.2 min, the -லாம்
machine), and **M74_v2**. That is about two hours. The tapes are on the feed; download
before you board.

**Two commands when you land** — knocks are held until you run the first one, and
nothing has been queued into the flight:

```bash
python scripts/sync_state.py update --quiet-until "" --timezone Asia/Kolkata
python scripts/show_status.py
```

The first clears the transit hold *and* moves the clock, so quiet hours stop being
New York's. Without it Anna would start knocking at 3am Coimbatore.

**Then the era turns.** The countdown and the burn rate come off the status line — in
country the table sets the pace, and a per-day quota is a lie. The field mission stops
being homework and becomes the input: what actually happened at her table is the
session, and it is real data, not a rehearsal.
