# Backdrop prompt — Anna intro demo video (README)

Not the podcast cover. `logo.jpg` stays as it is. This is the still image behind
`special_anna_intro_v10.mp3` in the README video, shown small (240×240) and square.

Subject: old Tamil poetry meeting the machine. No people, no dinner table, no title
lettering. One idea, one shape, legible at thumbnail size.

---

## The prompt

> Square 1:1 image. Warm, traditional hand-painted illustration: visible brushwork,
> soft edges, lamplit. Palette of terracotta, turmeric, brass, deep red and warm
> cream against soft indigo shadow. No neon, no vaporwave, no digital gloss.
>
> An open palm-leaf manuscript — a Tamil ஓலைச்சுவடி, narrow stacked leaves bound
> through a cord, lying on dark wood in the light of a small oil lamp. Etched Tamil
> letterforms run along the leaves, warm and worn, sized as texture rather than as
> a readable inscription.
>
> From the right-hand leaf, the etched lines lift off the surface and rise into the
> air, the strokes breaking apart as they go into a slow drift of small glowing
> points and fine connecting threads — a soft constellation of light spreading
> toward the upper corner. The transformation is gradual: script at the bottom,
> particles and filaments at the top, the same warm gold throughout.
>
> Composition: manuscript low and central, dark calm space above for the rising
> light. Strong single silhouette that still reads when scaled to a thumbnail.
> No text besides the letterforms on the leaves, no watermark, no user interface,
> no circuit-board imagery, no robots.
