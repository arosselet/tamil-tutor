When you land

Knocks are held until you run the first line, and nothing was queued into the flight — so nothing is sitting on an unreachable phone being overwritten. The second half of that command moves your clock; without it Anna starts knocking at 3am Coimbatore.

python scripts/sync_state.py update --quiet-until "" --timezone Asia/Kolkata
python scripts/show_status.py

In the air: three long-haul tapes (room 51 min, machines 29 min, inventory 26 min), M86 — The Family That Never Decides (7.2 min, the -லாம் machine), and M74_v2. About two hours. Download from the feed before you board.

Then the era turns. The countdown and the burn rate come off the status line — in country the table sets the pace, and a per-day quota is a lie. What happened at her table stops being homework and becomes the session.
