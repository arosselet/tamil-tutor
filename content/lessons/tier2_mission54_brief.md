# Tier 2, Mission 54 — The Stall Between Wants

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Transaction Frame Stack — five request/navigation machines (isthere, giveme, howmuch, want, where) firing in natural sequence at a stall transaction
- **Register:** embarrassment (from Scene Spec)
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** subtext — two people want opposite things under polite words (from Scene Spec)
- **Scenario Shape:** transaction
- **Location class:** market
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** vignette

## Scenario Context
A roadside tea stall in Coimbatore, peak morning rush. Host A (F) is in full transaction mode — she just wants to order and leave. Host B (M) prefers coffee but can't bring himself to say so after the stall vendor confirms only tea is available. Every question he asks orbits what he actually wants but never lands there. The vendor, for his part, is more curious about how long these two are in town than he is about taking their order. Under every polite exchange runs a current of things no one will just say outright. The embarrassment peak: Host B mutters a confession just as the tea arrives — and Host A catches it.

## Notes
Vignette form — Intercept only, no Breakdown. Trust the scene to carry the words. Five frames deploy in natural transaction order: isthere (x2) → giveme → howmuch → want → where. Chunk ஒரு மாசம் இருப்போம் answers the vendor's "how long?" question; it echoes once more when Host B savors the irony. Embarrassment register: Host B's indirect dance around tea vs. coffee is the subtext; his half-muttered "tea பிடிக்காது-ன்னு சொல்லலாம்ன்னு நினைச்சேன்" is the peak embarrassment moment. Contrasts well with M53 (suspicion, phone_call, kitchen), M52 (dread, story, extended_family), M51 (reconciliation, classic). Energy medium-loud: rush-hour stall, vendor chatter, ambient market noise. Unfenced ceiling: 1 word max (நாப்பது — price context answers it immediately).
