# Tier 2, Mission 72 — ஒரு வாய் சாப்பிடுங்க

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Obligation-Permission Rub — போகணும் (must go) and போகலாமா (may I go?) rubbing against the family's -ங்க command machine (உக்காருங்க, சாப்பிடுங்க)
- **Register:** embarrassment (from Scene Spec)
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** genre — the classic Tamil threshold comedy: the guest who cannot leave (from Scene Spec)
- **Scenario Shape:** dispute
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** classic (extended — long soak commission; target 10+ minutes)

## Scenario Context

A mutual friend of the two hosts missed his train because his mother-in-law kept feeding him
one more bite every time he tried to stand up. The hosts retell the scene, acting it out between
themselves — one takes the role of the trapped guest, the other rotates through amma, thatha,
and aachi. The comedy is fully genre-aware: this is THE classic Tamil sitcom situation, the
trapped guest at the threshold, and both hosts know exactly which beats are coming.

The embarrassment register lands on the guest: he fires every correct line (அவசரம் இருக்கு,
ஸ்டேஷன்கு போகணும், நான் போகணும்) and loses anyway.

The scene seed: the boy is running late for the train; the family fusses him to sit and eat one
more bite while he insists he has to go — அவசரம் இருக்கு / ஸ்டேஷன்கு போகணும் rubbing against
உக்காருங்க / சாப்பிடுங்க at the door.
