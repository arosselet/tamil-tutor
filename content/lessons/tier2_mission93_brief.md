# Tier 2, Mission 93 — The Safe Question

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Verb Answer — a question about *doing* demands a verb back, never the noun it's about. (சமைக்கிற? answered with சமைக்குறேன், never with the dish name alone.)
- **Register:** reconciliation
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** subtext — two people want opposite things under polite words
- **Scenario Shape:** gossip
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** low
- **Episode Form:** story

## Scenario Context

A cousin calls the house mid-afternoon — the kind of call that follows a silence neither side has named yet. She opens light, circling, but the one question she actually asks, three separate times, however the conversation drifts, is **என்ன சமைக்கிற?** It's the safe question: deniable, ordinary, a door that doesn't require either of them to apologize first. Underneath it, she wants to hear that things are normal again; he wants to prove it without being the one who says sorry. Every time she asks, the answer has to be the verb doing the work — naming the dish (சாம்பார் தான், காப்பி) is the noun-shaped exit that dodges the question, and worse, it's the tell that nothing's actually settled yet.

**Craft note on form:** M92 already ran this exact payload and it landed as a miss — both hosts *narrated in English*, with the Tamil sitting inside as reported evidence, so the question was never actually put to anyone. That is not what "story" form should mean here. One host still carries the telling (the frame stays a told tale, light/no Breakdown), but the phone call itself must be voiced live and direct — the cousin's line and his answer both fire as real quoted speech in the moment they happen, not summarized ("she asked what I was cooking"). The story wraps the scene; it doesn't flatten it.

## Word Payload

**NEW (4 words/phrases):**
- **கை** (*kai*) — Hand — thin cluster (body, 0/2 known); pairs with ருசி below
- **வாய்** (*vaai*) — Mouth — thin cluster (body, 0/2 known); the "tastes good in the mouth" angle
- **ருசி** (*ruchi*) — Taste — thin cluster (home_kitchen, 0/1 known); the fence already flags **கை ருசி** (hand's taste — a cook's skill) as the natural collocation. Land it as one idiom, not two loose nouns: the cousin's compliment/peace-offering, not a vocabulary drop.
- **என்ன ஆச்சு?** (*enna aachu*) — What happened? — thin cluster (questions); the check-in question that sits *under* the cooking question. This is the actual reconciliation move — let her ask it once, lightly, before circling to the safe question.

**EAR-ONLY weave (comprehension, no fire demanded):** தெரியுமா? (a real gossip opener) and சும்மா சொல்றாங்க (the dismissal — "they're just talking") both fit this scene's register better than almost any recent one: reconciliation runs on exactly this move, waving off whatever rumor caused the silence. Weave at least one in if it doesn't force the scene.

## Vocabulary Fence (the sea — build from these)

- frame:adverb-aa (medhuvaa) — {X}+ஆ → 'X-ly / as X' — நல்லா, மெதுவா, தண்ணியா, இன்ஜினியரா இருக்கேன்: one machine turns anything into an adverb
- frame:cant-முடியல் — {verb} முடியல் → can't {verb} — தூக்க முடியல், போக முடியல்
- frame:day-recap — {place} போனோம், ரொம்ப நல்லா இருந்துச்சு → we went to {place}, it was great (the nightly 'inniki enna panninga?' answer)
- frame:done-ittu (vandhutten) — {verb}+இட்டு/‑ட்டு → done-and-dusted / off-I-go — வந்துட்டேன், கழுவிட்டு வரேன், விட்டுடு, ஆயிட்டேன்: the completive already inside 7 deck items
- frame:giveme-noun — {noun} குடுங்க → give me {noun} (e.g. 'oru tea kudunga')
- frame:happened-aachu (aachu) — {V}+ச்சு / ஆச்சு → 'it happened / it's become' — ஆச்சு, போச்சு, முடிஞ்சுச்சு, நேரம் ஆச்சு: the it-verb the pool is already full of
- frame:hearsay-aam — …-ஆம் → 'apparently / they say' — the hearsay tag gossip runs on (varaanga-laam = apparently they're coming) (EAR ONLY)
- frame:howmuch-noun — {noun} எவ்ளோ? → how much is {noun}? (e.g. 'idhu evlo?' — Oracle: always the contracted 'evlo')
- frame:idum — {verb}+ஆகிடும்/-இடும் → it'll {verb} itself / it's-handled — the fated, hands-off future-completive: சரியாயிடும், முடிஞ்சிடும், போயிடும், புக் பண்ணிடும் — bolts onto a clean single verb, not a compound (use பாத்துக்கறேன் there instead)
- frame:if-ndhaa (thorandhaa) — {verb}+ந்தா / இல்லன்னா → 'if…' — வாய் திறந்தா…, இல்லன்னா என்ன ஆகும்?: the conditional
- frame:in-la (canada-la) — {noun}+ல → in/at/on — கனடாவுல, தமிழ்ல, வீட்ல, போன்ல: the everywhere locative
- frame:isthere-noun — {noun} இருக்கா? → is there {noun}? (e.g. 'coffee இருக்கா?')
- frame:mayi-laama (laama) — நான் {verb}-லாமா? → may I {verb}? (permission, e.g. 'ukkaralaama?')
- frame:nearby-noun — பக்கத்துல {noun} இருக்கா? → is there {noun} nearby?
- frame:needtogo-place — {place}-க்கு போகணும் → (I) need to go to {place}
- frame:negative-la — {verb-stem}+ல → didn't / doesn't / can't — புரியல், முடியல், தெரியல், கேட்கல், பண்ணல்: the everyday spoken negative (verb + illa, contracted)
- frame:obligation-ணும் — {verb}+ணும் → must {verb} — பண்ணணும், போகணும், சொல்லணும்
- frame:polite-nga (nga) — {verb}+ங்க → the polite ask — சொல்லுங்க, குடுங்க, உக்காருங்க, வாங்க: one machine, every verb he owns
- frame:present-future-toggle — -உறேன் (now) vs -வேன் (later) on any verb — போறேன்/போவேன், கேட்குறேன்/கேட்பேன்
- frame:quote-nu — …-னு சொன்னாங்க → 'she said that…' — the quotative -னு; every reported line in gossip hangs on it (EAR ONLY)
- frame:spice-dhaan (nethu dhaan) — the sass clitics: ‑தான் 'just/exactly', ‑ஏ '-self/very', ‑உம் 'even/any', ‑ஓ 'some-or-other' — நேத்து தான், நானே, ஒன்னும், என்னமோ (EAR ONLY)
- frame:want-noun — {English noun} வேணும் → I want {noun} (e.g. 'auto வேணும்')
- frame:we-om — {I-form}+ஓம் → we — வந்தோம், போனோம், இருப்போம், சாப்பிட்டோம்: the trip is a 'we' trip
- frame:where-noun — {noun} எங்க? → where is {noun}? (e.g. 'bus stop எங்க?')
- frame:youknow-la (namma thambi irukkaan-la) — நம்ம {person} இருக்கான்ல… → 'you know our {person}, right…' — THE gossip opener; the -ல tag means 'right?' (EAR ONLY, Oracle-confirmed)
