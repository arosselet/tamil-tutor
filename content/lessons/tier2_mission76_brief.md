# Tier 2, Mission 75 — The Overheard Problem

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Ask/Hear Toggle (*kel* vs *kitta kel*)
- **Register:** tenderness
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** stakes
- **Scenario Shape:** eavesdrop
- **Location class:** extended_family
- **Energy:** low
- **Episode Form:** story

## Scenario Context
A quiet, reflective story about a nephew overhearing his mother and aunt speaking in hushed, concerned tones in the next room. There are real stakes—someone in the family is dealing with an unspoken difficulty. As the sisters drop classic gossip openers about the situation, the nephew wanders in to ask "who?" to anchor the drift. The sisters pull him into the circle with gentle reassurance, clarifying the difference between what they heard and who they asked using the *kitta* postposition. The tone is deeply tender: a family dealing with a problem together, without talking down to the nephew.
