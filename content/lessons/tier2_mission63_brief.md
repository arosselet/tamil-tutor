# Tier 2, Mission 63 — குடுங்க: The Softest Scam at the Tea Table

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Give-Me Frame — frame:giveme-noun ({noun} குடுங்க) vs. the announcement pattern (வேணும்); the contrast between pointing-at-them (ask them to act) and pointing-at-yourself (announce a need).
- **Register:** delight
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** genre — scam (the tea-table caper: one person clears the snack spread using only குடுங்க, hand-to-hand, never once announcing வேணும், until the other notices and calls it out)
- **Scenario Shape:** gossip (two hosts reconstructing what just happened at the table)
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** medium
- **Episode Form:** vignette (Intercept only — no Breakdown; the scene carries the soak)

## Scenario Context
Late afternoon, tea time (சாயங்காலம்). Two friends have just been at the family tea table. Host A has noticed that Host B smoothly collected all the best snacks without ever announcing வேணும் ("I want") — instead deploying குடுங்க in low, directed asks, one person at a time, hand-to-hand, before anyone could raise a question. Away from the table now, Host A calls it a scam. Host B explains the system: வேணும் invites questions; குடுங்க is already the answer and the action in one move. The twist: Host B even used குடுங்க for a sweet she doesn't like — and then passed it to Elder Sister. Host A is converted and immediately plans to position herself better at tomorrow's tea.

The genre ingredient is a mini-heist reveal: allegation → evidence laid out → defense and reveal of the system → the accuser joins the operation.
