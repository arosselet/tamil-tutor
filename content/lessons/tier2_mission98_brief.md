# Tier 2, Mission 98 — The Biryani Cover-Up

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The 1st Person Plural (-ஓம் / *-om*) Machine in Past & Future (செஞ்சோம், போனோம், வாங்கினோம், இருப்போம்) contrasted against the 2nd Person honorific (-ங்க / *-nga*) and 3rd Person plural (-ஆங்க / *-aanga*).
- **Register:** mischief
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** stakes — If Paati catches them test-cooking a lavish biryani for her 75th birthday, she will scold them for wasting money, veto the feast entirely, and insist on curd rice.
- **Scenario Shape:** debrief
- **Location class:** kitchen
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** phone_call

## Scenario Context
Athai and Karthi are running a rogue test-cook in the kitchen for Paati's 75th birthday lunch. Karthi stepped out to the corner shop on his scooter to grab extra mint and curd, leaving Athai tending the simmering pot. Disaster strikes: Paati woke up from her afternoon nap early and walked straight toward the kitchen, drawn by the smell of ghee and spices. 

Athai ducks behind the pantry door and calls Karthi in a hushed, frantic whisper. Over the phone, they desperately rehearse and cross-check their cover story: Who went to the shop? Who bought what? Who cooked this? Every time Athai tries to blame Karthi, Karthi reminds her that they are in this together—it's a "we" operation (*நாங்க செஞ்சோம்*, *நாங்க போனோம்*). As Paati's footsteps get closer to the stove, they scramble to align their alibis before the lid comes off.

## Notes
- **Register check:** Keep the energy mischievous and conspiratorial. Athai and Karthi are partners-in-crime trying to avoid getting busted by Paati. 
- **Linguistic Focus:** The entire scene hinges on who did what. Athai tries to dump it on Karthi (*"நீ பண்ணுன"* / *"நீ சொன்ன"*), but Karthi forces the shared plural (*"நாங்க செஞ்சோம்"*, *"நாங்க வாங்கினோம்"*). Keep the `-om` sound prominent and rhythmic across multiple verbs so Andrew's ear latches onto the shared-action marker.
- **Form:** `phone_call`. Athai is in the kitchen with phone pressed to her ear; Karthi is at the corner shop or outside the gate. Light Breakdown following the call.
