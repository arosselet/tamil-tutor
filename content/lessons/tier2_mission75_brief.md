# Tier 2, Mission 75 — The Person Toggle

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Person Toggle — கேட்டேன் means HEAR when it stands alone; it means ASK when a person + கிட்ட is in the same clause. The presence or absence of கிட்ட after a human flips the meaning completely.
- **Register:** mischief (from Scene Spec)
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** turn — the scene flips on a reveal partway through (from Scene Spec)
- **Scenario Shape:** gossip
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** medium
- **Episode Form:** vignette (no Breakdown — the Intercept carries the payload alone)

## Scenario Context

Host A (F) is in the front room, mid-gossip about Ramesh — "என்னமோ பிரச்சனை-ஆம்" — but she only heard it second-hand and doesn't know what it is. Host B (M) wanders in and starts asking questions. The same verb (கேட்) keeps surfacing: sometimes Host A heard something (no person + கிட்ட), sometimes she asked someone (person + கிட்ட). The TURN comes when Host B traces the gossip chain all the way back and discovers that nobody in the chain ever asked Ramesh directly. Everyone "heard" (கேட்டேன் — standalone), but nobody "asked him" (அவன் கிட்ட கேட்டேன் — with person + கிட்ட). The whole problem is unverified. "கேட்கவே பிரச்சனை-ஆ ஆச்சு!" — even the asking has become a problem.

**Hard requirement:** The very first line must use frame:youknow-la — நம்ம {person} இருக்கான்ல… — to plant the referent before any hearsay lands. The 2026-07-25 bug was a gossip tape with -ஆம் and no referent; the listener had no answer to "who?" Do not repeat it.

## Word Payload

**NEW (3 core items from the soak order):**
- **கிட்ட** (*kitta*) — the person postposition; with a person, turns கேட் into "ask"; without it, கேட் is "hear"
- **பிரச்சனை** (*prachanai*) — problem / trouble (deepening from comfortable → solid; already in the scene seed)
- **கேட்டேன்** (*ketten*) — I heard / I asked (the flip word; must appear in both meanings audibly)

**CALLBACKS (from fence — weave naturally, don't force):**
- **அவங்க** (*avanga*) — they / respectful he/she [solid]
- **ஆனா** (*aana*) — but [solid]
- **நாங்க** (*naanga*) — we (exclusive) [solid]
- **சொன்னாங்க** (*sonnaaangga*) — they said [solid]

## Notes

- Vignette form: Intercept ONLY. No Breakdown. Trust the scene to deliver the payload.
- The teach point is structural: show கேட்டேன் standalone at least once (HEAR), then [person] கிட்ட கேட்டேன் at least once (ASK). The contrast must be audible across consecutive lines.
- Host A (F) is urban and code-switches naturally — give her English inserts like "properly", "Exactly", "Just". Host B (M) stays in Tamil longer.
- The mischief register builds from Host A's conspiratorial low voice to both laughing at the end.
- Unfenced words: hold to ≤ 2; context must answer them immediately if used.
