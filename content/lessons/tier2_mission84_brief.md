# Tier 2, Mission 84 — The Neighbor's Secret

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Hearsay Close (Stance markers)
- **Register:** dread
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** stakes
- **Scenario Shape:** eavesdrop
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** phone_call

## Scenario Context
Andrew is in the living room while Athai (paternal aunt) is in the kitchen on a phone call. The atmosphere is tense—she is speaking in hushed but urgent tones. She is listening to a heavy piece of neighborhood gossip regarding a legal or financial threat ("stakes") facing a mutual friend. Andrew only hears her side of the conversation. The scene focuses on how she shuts down or acknowledges the alarming information using the payload markers.

## Word Payload

**NEW (4 words):**
- **சும்மா சொல்றாங்க** (*summa solraanga*) — They're just talking / making it up (dismissal)
- **விட்டுடு** (*vittudu*) — Let it go / drop it (nee-form)
- **சரி சரி** (*seri seri*) — Right, right (backchannel agreement/conceding)
- **என்னமோ பிரச்சனை** (*ennamo prachanai*) — Some problem or other (vague gossip hum)

**CALLBACKS (5 words):**
- **சொன்னாங்க** (*sonnaanga*) — They said [recently-mastered]
- **தெரியுமா?** (*theriyuma*) — You know…? [comfortable]
- **பிரச்சனை** (*prachanai*) — Problem [overdue]
- **வேண்டாம்** (*vendam*) — I don't want [floor-gap]
- **பிடிக்காது** (*pidikkaadhu*) — I don't like [floor-gap]

## Notes
The focus is the **CLOSE** of the exchange. Use the payload to end beats. Athai should sound increasingly worried (dread) as she hears about the "ennamo prachanai," but then uses "vittudu" or "summa solraanga" to try and push the fear away or end the call. The stakes involve the "ennamo prachanai" being serious enough to cause "payam" (fear).
