# Tier 1, Mission 87 — The "Nothing Works" Errand

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Negative Machine (`-ல`). Shifting from memorized chunks (*puriyala*, *mudiyala*) to a generative negative suffix that can be bolted onto any verb stem.
- **Register:** suspicion
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** genre (a noir-ish street scam/runaround)
- **Scenario Shape:** transaction
- **Location class:** street
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** classic

## Scenario Context
An errand in a crowded Coimbatore side-street turns into a suspicious runaround. A "helpful" stranger claims to know the shopkeeper Andrew is looking for, but every attempt to verify facts hits a wall. The shop is shut, the phone won't connect, and the stranger's story changes with every beat. The stranger seems to be enjoying the chaos of the "nothing works today" disaster a little too much.

## Word Payload

**NEW (5 words):**
- **தெரியல** (*theriyala*) — I don't know (the -la machine on 'know')
- **கேட்கல** (*ketkala*) — I can't hear / didn't hear (the -la machine on 'hear')
- **வரல** (*varala*) — It's not coming / didn't come (the -la machine on 'come')
- **பண்ணல** (*pannala*) — Didn't do / haven't done (the -la machine on 'do')
- **என்ன சொன்னீங்க?** (*enna sonneenga?*) — What did you say? (The "keep-going" repair tool)

**CALLBACKS (5 words):**
- **புரியல** (*puriyala*) — I don't understand [recently-mastered]
- **முடியல** (*mudiyala*) — I can't / It's not possible [recently-mastered]
- **அலைச்சல்** (*alaichal*) — The hassle / the running-around [overdue]
- **சும்மா சொல்றாங்க** (*summa solraanga*) — They're just talking / making it up [overdue]
- **எவ்ளோ ஆகும்?** (*evlo aagum?*) — How much will it be? [struggled]

## Vocabulary Fence (the sea — build from these)
The Architect must build the scene's connective tissue from this pool. Words outside it are the +1 — they must be answerable from context within seconds.

- **frame:negative-la** (The machine: verb-stem + la)
- **frame:cant-முடியல** (verb + mudiyala)
- **frame:polite-nga** (verb + nga)
- **தெரியும்** (know)

- **அதான்** (that's why)
- **என்னமோ பிரச்சனை** (some problem or other)

## Notes
**The Person Toggle:** Contrast the stranger's "They are saying" (*solraanga*) with Andrew's need to say "I don't know/hear/understand" (*theriyala/ketkala/puriyala*). 
**The "Keep-Going" Test:** Explicitly pit *puriyala* (exchange closer) against *enna sonneenga?* (exchange opener). The stranger should try to walk away when Andrew says *puriyala*, forcing Andrew to use *enna sonneenga?* to stay in the loop.
