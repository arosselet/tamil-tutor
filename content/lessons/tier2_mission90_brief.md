## Core Targets

- **Register:** pride

- **Scenario Shape:** dispute
- **Location class:** extended_family
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** vignette (Intercept only, no Breakdown — trust the scene)

## Scenario Context

A family gathering, not a meal this time — chairs dragged into a circle, someone's fan needs fixing, someone's bag needs carrying down from the loft. There is one relative who cannot let a task pass him by: before anyone finishes the sentence, he's already declared **நான் பாத்துக்கிறேன்**. It isn't helpfulness exactly — it's a standing claim on competence, and everyone at the table both loves and quietly clocks it. Tonight he's mid-retelling of some past feat — wiring, a flood, a wedding-day crisis, embellished a size larger than it happened — when a cousin, gently, without malice, undercuts the version: **அப்படி இல்ல**. The room holds its breath for half a second, he laughs it off, and the story continues, slightly smaller. Pride intact, exaggeration corrected — that's the whole scene.
