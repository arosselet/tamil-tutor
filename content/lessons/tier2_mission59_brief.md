# Tier 2, Mission 59 — கடைசில பாக்கலாம் — The Family Verdict

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Conditional Machine + We-Forms (frame:if-ndhaa + frame:we-om)
- **Register:** defiance (Scene Spec — someone quietly immovable under mounting pressure)
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** subtext (Scene Spec — two people want opposite things under polite words)
- **Scenario Shape:** transaction (booking flight tickets)
- **Location class:** home_social (family tea table)
- **Energy:** medium
- **Episode Form:** phone_call (Scene Spec — naturalistic call; light Breakdown)

## Scenario Context

Booking day for the India trip. Host A calls Host B to lock in a flight booking — they need to commit today before the price expires. Host B is at the family tea table, surrounded by background chai-and-chatter chaos. Under each polite conditional ("if the price is OK," "if aunt comes," "if possible"), he's running out the clock — and Host A can feel it. Neither says what they actually mean. The family's verdict arrives via relayed word: "கடைசில பாக்கலாம்." The table has spoken; the call is over.

**Defiance register:** Host B is not aggressive — he is quietly immovable. Every new "if" is a gentle but firm wall. Host A's defiance arrives at the end: she washes her hands of it with a parting shot. Neither breaks; neither yields.

**Subtext:** the audience knows "கடைசில பாக்கலாம்" means no long before Host A admits defeat.

## Notes

- முடிஞ்சா and கடைசில பாக்கலாம் are UNSEEN — first soak. They land in answering contexts: the call itself makes meaning obvious. Anna cold-fires them in the next session.
- frame:if-ndhaa: build a cascade of conditionals (irundha / ok-na / vandha / kidaiccha / illainna / mudinja shapes). Seven-plus instances in the Intercept.
- frame:we-om: weave all three target shapes — வாரோம், போலாம், பாக்கலாம் — through the booking negotiation. The India trip is a "we" trip; every decision takes the plural.
- Unfenced non-payload Tamil: cap at 2 (அம்மா — universally obvious; அத்தை — obvious from booking context).
- Breakdown obsesses over ONE beat: "கடைசில பாக்கலாம்" as the Tamil family non-decision decision — the polite "no" that can never be refused because it was never said.
