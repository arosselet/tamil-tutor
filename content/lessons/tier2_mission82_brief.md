# Tier 1, Mission 14 — The CBE Arrival: Three Rooms

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The We-Tense Toggle (-ஓம்) vs. Location Locking (-ல)
- **Register:** delight
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** turn (The scene flips on a reveal partway through)
- **Scenario Shape:** callback_heavy
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** narrated_drama

## Scenario Context
The first evening at the family home in Coimbatore. It is a whirlwind of sensory overload and overlapping voices. You move from the gate (arrival), to the dining table (the feast), to the porch (the gossip). The "turn" comes during the post-dinner coffee: you realize the aunties aren't just being nice—they’ve been plotting a "surprise" for the rest of your trip.

## Notes
**The 1pl-past Slip-Fix:** The Narrator must emphasize the difference between the intended "We came" (வந்தோம் - vandom) and the learner's recurring "Must go" (போகணும் - poganum) slip. The script should use "வந்தோம்" and "இருக்கோம்" repeatedly to lock in the trip identity.
**Location Fix:** Contrast "கனடாவுல" (in Canada) and "ஊர்ல" (in the village) against the -க்கு (to) suffix he overuses.
