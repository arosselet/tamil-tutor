# Tier 2, Mission 78 — The Wedding Recap

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The "We" Recap (-ஓம் past tense)
- **Register:** pride
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** genre (The "Heist" style debrief / The Recap)
- **Scenario Shape:** debrief
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** narrated_drama

## Scenario Context
Two cousins have just returned home from a sprawling family wedding. They are reporting back to the rest of the house about their "mission." There is a sense of accomplishment and pride—they navigated the crowds, secured the good food, and handled the family politics perfectly. The focus is entirely on collective action: what *we* did, where *we* went, and how *it* was.

## Notes
**CRITICAL CONSTRAINT:** Per the Twin Law, do NOT use the -ஏன் (-en) "I" ending in this script. The learner is struggling with I vs. We interference. This entire episode must strictly use -ஓம் (-om) for all first-person actions to solidify the "We" pattern. The Narrator will use English to bridge the scene, but all Tamil verbs must be "We" or "It" forms. 

The Dramatic Ingredient (Genre) should treat the wedding recap like a successful operation—pride in how "we" handled the Aunties and "we" got the best seats.
