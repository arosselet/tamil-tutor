# Tier 1, Mission 81 — The Welcome Reveal

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Delight Fragment (The Smile-and-Say)
- **Register:** grief/nostalgia
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** turn (the scene flips on a reveal partway through)
- **Scenario Shape:** callback_heavy
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** medium
- **Episode Form:** narrated_drama

## Scenario Context
A threshold moment. You arrive at the family house in Coimbatore. The sisters-in-law are waiting at the door. There is the expected chaotic joy of a first meeting, but as you step inside, you realize they aren't just celebrating your arrival—they are looking at you because you look exactly like someone they lost. The "turn" shifts from high-energy welcome to a quiet, nostalgic realization.

## Word Payload

**NEW (1 word):**
- **ரொம்ப சந்தோஷம்** (*romba santhosham*) — Very happy (to meet you). The primary "handshake" fragment.

**CALLBACKS (5 words):**
- **அத்தை** (*atthai*) — Aunt (paternal) [overdue]
- **பெரிய** (*periya*) — Big [recently-mastered]
- **உனக்கு** (*unakku*) — To you / For you [floor-gap]
- **சொல்லுங்க** (*sollunga*) — Tell/Say (respectful) [floor-gap]
- **அவங்க** (*avanga*) — They / He-She (respectful) [recently-mastered]

## Notes
- **The Turn:** The scene should start with the "Delight" of the meeting. The transition to "Grief/Nostalgia" happens when the family mentions a "periya" similarity to a past relative.
- **The Payload:** *Romba santhosham* is the target. The Narrator should prompt it or anchor the moment around it. Use it as the emotional beat for each introduction.
