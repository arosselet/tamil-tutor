## Scenario Context

Host B just came from the auto stand and has a story. A mapillai (son-in-law type — Canada visitor) was trying to get an auto when the driver hit him with a rapid-fire machine-gun of questions. Instead of freezing, the visitor deployed the five anti-freeze lines one at a time — slowing the driver down, buying time, pulling a meta-move that stopped the driver cold ("தமிழ்ல எப்படி சொல்றது?"). The stakes: does the stranger survive with his dignity and get the auto, or does he freeze? The pride register builds with each rescue — the driver's "அப்படியா?!" is the moment of earned respect. Host A provides backchannels and sharp reactions; Host B performs both sides of the encounter.

This is the teach-first episode for all five phrases — their first soak, the dose that makes them volley-legal.
