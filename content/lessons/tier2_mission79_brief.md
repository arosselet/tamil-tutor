# MASTER LESSON PLAN

## 1. THE COMMISSION: "குடுங்க" UNDER LOAD
**Payload**: `குடுங்க` (Give / Pass it to me)
**The Twin Law (STRICTLY ENFORCED)**: `வேணும்` (I want/need) and all `வேணும்`-forms are BANNED from this script. No character may announce a need. Every ask on this tape MUST point at another person's hand using `குடுங்க`.
**Target Moves**: `தண்ணி குடுங்க`, `உப்பு குடுங்க`, `கொஞ்சம் சாதம் குடுங்க`, `plate-a குடுங்க`.

## 2. SCENE SPECIFICATION
* **Form**: `narrated_drama` (Inner monologue interwoven with spoken dialogue)
* **Register**: `pride`
* **Ingredient**: `character` (A vivid, specific person with a tic, obsession, or lie)
* **Seed**: A crowded table mid-meal. Everything he needs is in someone else's reach, and the only way to eat is to ask THEM to pass it.

## 3. CAST & DYNAMIC
* **The Character**: **அத்தை (Aunt)**. She is intensely proud of her cooking and orchestrates the table like a battlefield commander. Her obsession is ensuring everyone eats exactly what she dictates, and she takes any refusal as a personal challenge.
* **The Dynamic**: Andrew is pinned at the table. He must navigate her pride (`pride` register) while surviving the spicy food by asking others to pass him things (`குடுங்க`). 

## 4. SLIP LEDGER (MANDATORY INCLUSIONS)
These are live slips. They MUST be woven into the conversation so Andrew can practice the correct forms:
* **Slip 1**: `1pl-past-om` -> **Target**: `போனோம்` (We went). *Context: Test whether it landed. He previously slipped with 'poganum'.*
* **Slip 2**: `past-tense` -> **Target**: `இருந்துச்சு` (It was). *Context: Test whether it landed. He previously slipped with 'iruka'.*
* *Execution*: அத்தை asks about a recent trip/outing. Andrew responds that they went (`போனோம்`) and it was good (`நல்லா இருந்துச்சு`).

## 5. EAR-ONLY SOAK (THE PAIR DRILL)
* **Hear it**: `இன்னும் கொஞ்சம் சாப்பிடுங்க` (Eat a little more) – The Aunt's pressure line.
* **Answer it**: `வேண்டாம்மா, வயிறு ஃபுல்` or `வேண்டாம், வயிறு நிறைஞ்சிடுச்சு` (No thanks, stomach full) – Andrew's required refusal. 
*(Note: Drill the PAIR. He must hear the push to practice the refusal).*

## 6. FOCUS SET, TRIP DECK & HINTED TARGETS (WEAVE IN)
Embed these seamlessly. Do not break the scene to force them.
* **The Praise**: `ரொம்ப பிடிச்சிருக்கு` (I really like it) — Use this to satisfy the Aunt's pride.
* **The Correction**: `அப்படி இல்ல` (No, it is not like that) 
* **Backchannels & Contrasts**: 
  * `சரி சரி` (Right, right) — *Contrast: He slipped with 'am am'. Force 'sari sari' for agreement.*
  * `என்ன சொன்னீங்க?` (What did you say?) — *Contrast: He slipped with 'puriyala'. Force 'enna sonneenga' to reopen.*
  * `சொல்லுங்க` (Tell/Say) — *Contrast: He slipped with 'pesa'. Force the polite form.*
* **Core Vocab**: `வை` (Keep/Put), `காரம்` (Spicy), `பெரிய` (Big), `வெளிய` (Outside), `ஆனா` (But), `அதுக்கு அப்புறம்` (After that).

## 7. STRUCTURAL BEATS (FOR THE ARCHITECT)
* **Beat 1: The Trap.** Narrated setup of the crowded, noisy meal. `அத்தை` is serving with pride. The food is `காரம்` (spicy). Andrew needs relief but can't reach it. He asks someone nearby: `கொஞ்சம் தண்ணி குடுங்க`.
* **Beat 2: The Interrogation.** `அத்தை` demands to know about yesterday. Andrew uses the slip targets: `நாங்க வெளிய போனோம்... ரொம்ப நல்லா இருந்துச்சு`. She might mishear or challenge him, prompting: `என்ன சொன்னீங்க?`
* **Beat 3: The Escalation.** The meal continues. Andrew needs something else passed to manage the meal: `உப்பு குடுங்க` or `கொஞ்சம் சாதம் குடுங்க`.
* **Beat 4: The Pushback.** `அத்தை` insists he eat more: `இன்னும் கொஞ்சம் சாப்பிடுங்க`. Andrew holds his ground with the drilled refusal: `வேண்டாம்மா, வயிறு ஃபுல்`.

**DIRECTOR'S NOTE TO ARCHITECT**: Remember the Twin Law. If the word `வேணும்` appears anywhere in the Tamil dialogue, the script fails. Build the scene entirely around `குடுங்க`.
