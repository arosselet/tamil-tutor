# Tier 2, Mission 95 — Trichy Road: Paati's Secret Present

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Want/Refuse Negation — வேணும் vs. வேண்டாம் surfaced in whole sentences under load, scaffolded by the price-question frame (எவ்ளோ ஆகும்?)
- **Register:** embarrassment
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** character — a vivid, specific person: a tic, an obsession, a lie
- **Scenario Shape:** transaction
- **Location class:** market
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** vignette (Intercept only, no Breakdown — trust the scene)

## Scenario Context
A shop on Trichy Road, lunchtime. Karthi has taken Deepa and Ravi out to buy Paati's seventy-fifth-birthday present — five hundred rupees that is really Mama's, and Karthi already owes Mama money from before, which is exactly why he cannot let this trip look like what it is. His tic: every time the shopkeeper or Deepa gets close to the money's real owner, Karthi oversells — louder, more generous, "இது ஒன்னும் இல்ல" — which is the opposite of reassuring and the comedy engine of the scene. Deepa prices everything in sight (எவ்ளோ ஆகும்?), Ravi wants all of it and says so, and both kids are working against Paati's own public line that she wants nothing (ஒன்னும் வேண்டாம்) while quietly knowing — because they've heard her say it at home — what she'd actually love. Nothing resolves; they leave with one small thing, Karthi a little poorer in dignity, the secret intact for lunch.

## Word Payload

**NEW (4 words):**
- **நாலு** (*naalu*) — four
- **ஆறு** (*aaru*) — six
- **ஏழு** (*ezhu*) — seven
- **எட்டு** (*ettu*) — eight

*(Thin cluster — numbers, known 0/8. A price/counting scene is the natural home for them; they land as quantities and small prices Deepa and Ravi throw around, not as a drilled list.)*

**CALLBACKS (5 words):**
- **எவ்ளோ ஆகும்?** (*evlo aagum?*) — How much will it be? [commission lead — the children's running question, every shop item]
- **வேணும்** (*vendum*) — I want / I need [commission — Ravi's wants, in full sentences under load, not the bare chunk]
- **வேண்டாம்** (*vendaam*) — don't want [commission — Paati's public refusal, quoted and argued over, in full sentences]
- **ஒன்னும்** (*onnum*) — nothing / anything at all [commission, EAR-ONLY frame:spice-dhaan — "ஒன்னும் வேண்டாம்", the exact phrase Paati is known for]
- **சின்ன** (*sinna*) — small/little [due callback, untested — describing the modest gift against Ravi's big wants]
