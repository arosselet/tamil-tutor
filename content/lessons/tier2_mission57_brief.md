# Tier 2, Mission 57 — The Regular

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Request Cluster — வேணும் / குடுங்க / இருக்கா? / எவ்ளோ? / எங்க?
- **Register:** tenderness (from Scene Spec — the spec's gate; warmth, affection for a vivid character)
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** character — a vivid, specific person: the tea stall's old regular, whose wordless ordering tic reveals who he is
- **Scenario Shape:** eavesdrop (hosts observe the old man's transaction; their own order wraps around it)
- **Location class:** street (corner tea stall, morning)
- **Energy:** medium
- **Episode Form:** classic (Intercept + full Breakdown)

Diverging from: M56 public/vignette, M55 mischief/lore, M54 embarrassment/vignette

## Scenario Context

A corner tea stall at mid-morning. Host A and B are waiting for their order. Host A drops the news that a relative called to say they're staying a month (ஒரு மாசம் இருப்போம்), then both settle in to wait. An old regular arrives — someone who has been coming here every morning for decades. He asks "என்ன இருக்கா?" as pure ritual: he already knows what he wants, the stall master already knows, and nobody says it out loud. The hosts watch him with quiet, warm affection. When it's their own turn, Host B accidentally mirrors the old man's move — deflects with "எதுவாவது சரிதான்" — until Host A calls her on it and she finally says what she actually wants.

The Breakdown zeroes in on ONE thing: what does "என்ன இருக்கா?" mean when you already know the answer? Maya reads it as a structural move (question-as-want, softer than வேணும்); Raj reads it as a relational move (decades of mutual recognition — no words needed).
