# Tier 2, Mission 66 — Tea Table Protocol: வேணும் to the Universe

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Request Toggle — குடுங்க (ask-the-person) vs. வேணும் (announce-to-the-room); frame:giveme-noun as the engine
- **Register:** reconciliation (from Scene Spec — diverging from M65 tension/phone_call, M64 tension/story, M63 delight/vignette)
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** character — a vivid, specific person with a defining tic: அத்தை who never once says குடுங்க, only announces வேணும் to the ceiling; one host used to find it grating, now passes the chai before the announcement lands
- **Scenario Shape:** gossip — two hosts riffing about a family member's signature speech habit
- **Location class:** home_social — the family tea table on a quiet afternoon
- **Energy:** low — warm, unhurried, interior
- **Episode Form:** classic (Intercept + full Breakdown) — from Scene Spec

## Scenario Context
Two hosts gossip about a specific relative — அத்தை — whose entire mode of want is the room-address: "Coffee வேணும்!" "Biscuit வேணும்!" announced at the ceiling even when the chai pot sits six inches from her hand. The family runs on குடுங்க — whoever is nearest quietly passes it before the announcement is finished. Host A used to find this habit maddening (பிடிக்காது that move) until a cousin explained: வேணும் is dignity — you put the need into the room and the room decides; குடுங்க is trust — you point at one person and ask them directly. Host A has fully reconciled with the habit and now passes அத்தை's chai before she even announces. The reconciliation is the arc; the character is the dramatic engine.

## Word Payload

**PRIMARY SOAK (1 item — from soak-order, hinted→cold):**
- **குடுங்க** (*kudunga*) — Give me (respectful, directed, hand-to-hand) — the softer ask the family runs on; distinguish from வேணும் (self-announcement)

**NEW (4 items — from verb_present and obligation_ability clusters, 0 known in each):**
- **குடிக்குறேன்** (*kudikkureen*) — I am drinking (verb_present; spoken Coimbatore form of குடிக்கிறேன்) — lands when அத்தை sits there happily குடிக்குறேன் her chai
- **என்ன பண்ற?** (*enna panra?*) — What are (they) doing? / What's going on? (verb_present, spoken) — "அவங்க என்ன பண்ற?" framing what the table does silently
- **நான் பாத்துக்குறேன்** (*naan paathukkureen*) — I'll take care of it / I'm handling it (obligation_ability; spoken form) — Host A's new move: I'm the one who passes the chai
- **அப்புறம் பாக்கலாம்** (*appuram paakalaam*) — Let's see later / Some things take time (proposals cluster) — closing beat of reconciliation arc

**CALLBACKS (3 items — soft target; aim for all 3):**
- **சொன்னாங்க** (*sonnaanga*) — They said [floor-gap, due callback] — the cousin's explanation line
- **பிடிக்காது** (*pidikkaadhu*) — I don't like [floor-gap, due callback] — Host A's former feeling about the habit
- **சீக்கிரம்** (*seekkiram*) — Quickly [floor-gap, due callback] — the table's response speed
