# Tier 2, Mission 77 — The Family Update Call

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The -ல tag as gossip opener (நம்ம {person} இருக்கான்ல / இருக்காங்கல — "our [person], right?")
- **Register:** tenderness (from Scene Spec — commissioned)
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** stakes — something real is on the line: a family gathering, and one key person cannot come
- **Scenario Shape:** gossip
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** low
- **Episode Form:** phone_call (COMMISSIONED via soak order — do NOT re-pick)

## Scenario Context

Two aunties on a phone call, trading family news one piece at a time. Each fresh bit of gossip opens the same way — நம்ம {person} இருக்கான்ல… — a different relative named each time. The stakes are real and warm: a family gathering is coming; one much-loved relative (அக்கா) cannot attend because of health, which brings genuine concern; another (மாமா) is making a special trip from out of town, which brings surprise and joy; the paternal aunt (அத்தை) is also coming; and the next-door neighbours are rallying to help. The tenderness is in how these two women hold every person in the family with care. Direction: catch — this frame is EAR ONLY, and it must never be set up as production.
