# Tier 2, Mission 99 — The Call From Chennai

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The person-tail rhyme (நான்-ஏன், நீ-ஆ/இயா, அவன்-ஆன், அவங்க-ஆங்க, நீங்க-ஈங்க) plus the odd one, -ஓம் on நாங்க/நாம; and நாம (inclusive "we, you too") against நாங்க (exclusive "we, not you"); போலாம் (let's go) against போனோம் (we went); இருக்கு (a thing) against இருக்கேன் (me) — all riding on two stems, சாப்பிடு and போ.
- **Register:** mischief
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** stakes — real money on the line, not a chore
- **Scenario Shape:** transaction
- **Location class:** home_social
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** phone_call

## Scenario Context
First night in the upstairs room. The house phone rings — Priya's husband, calling from Chennai to confirm the weekend visit. He never speaks; the episode is the household's side of the call, overheard in the front room with Andrew sitting right there. **Paati** takes it first, delighted, and loops Andrew into the same breath she uses for the uncle — telling both of them whose house this is, asking if he's eaten. On the way to hand it off, **Karthi** intercepts: he owes Mama for the scooter, and this call is his one quiet shot at getting his brother-in-law to cover it before Mama sees the bill — he keeps his voice light for Andrew's benefit while the actual ask is urgent and real. **Deepa** grabs the phone next, reports up the line without missing a beat, and in the same breath hauls Andrew toward the stairs to see the terrace. Mama's voice comes up the stairwell once — *who's on the phone* — just enough to raise the stakes before Paati reclaims the handset, closes the call, and tells Andrew plainly: this is their house too, his, for now.

This directly continues the arc's live question (whose house, how much Tamil he has) and re-stages, in audio, the same three speakers from today's chat session (Paati, Deepa, Karthi) — consolidation in a rich natural context, not first teaching.

## Word Payload

**NEW — commission payload (must recur throughout; carries the episode):**
- **நாம** (*naama*) — we/us, inclusive: "we, and you too"
- **நாங்க** (*naanga*) — we/us, exclusive: "we, not you"
- **போலாம்** (*polaam*) — let's go / shall we
- **போனோம்** (*ponom*) — we went
- **இருக்கேன்** (*irukken*) — I am / I'm here (contrast with the known இருக்கு, for a thing)

*Heard in context, not drilled separately — the tail rhyme on known stems:* அவன் சாப்பிட்டான் (avan saapittaan, he ate), அவங்க சாப்பிட்டாங்க (avanga saapittaanga, they ate), நீங்க போனீங்களா? (neenga poneengala?, did you go?).

**NEW — thin cluster (obligation_ability, 1/11 known — fits the money stakes):**
- **நான் பாத்துக்கிறேன்** — I'll manage / I'll take care of it (Karthi, steadying his voice)
- **இன்னைக்கு வர முடியாது** — Cannot come today (the uncertainty behind the weekend visit)

**CALLBACKS (soft target, 2-3 of these landing is enough):**
- **கொடுத்தேன்** — I gave [untested] — Karthi: "நான் காசு கொடுத்தேன்"
- **frame:idum** ({verb}+ஆகிடும்) [comfortable] — "கவலைப்படாதே, சரியாயிடும்"
- **frame:present-future-toggle** (-உறேன் vs -வேன்) [struggled] — போறேன்/போவேன் as Deepa pulls him toward the stairs
- **சூடு** — Hot [untested] — Paati, feeding him: "காபி சூடா இருக்கு"

## Notes
- Comprehension-led: the person-tail paradigm (நாம/நாங்க, போலாம்/போனோம், இருக்கு/இருக்கேன்) is being *consolidated* in rich natural audio after today's chat session, not cold-quizzed — win condition is recognition in the flow of the call, not a fire.
- Keep Karthi's -ங்க off every first-person verb (standing `respect-nga` gap) — his lines to the uncle stay bare-stem or -om, never -nga turned on himself.
- வா stays out of this episode entirely — reserved, unspent, for the ambush.
- Mama's one offstage line ("யாரு போன்ல?") carries the stakes beat without needing her at the table; Priya's husband stays unheard per canon.
- Light Breakdown only (phone_call form) — English colour on the tail-rhyme pattern and the naama/naanga contrast, no glossary read-through.
- Hard cap: ≤2 unfenced strangers, answered in the same beat. Tamil script only; fourth wall stays up throughout.
