# Tier 2, Mission 62 — இடும் & பாத்துக்கறேன்: The Shrug and the Promise

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Handled-Tag — two competing machines for "it's taken care of": the impersonal fated-future `-இடும்` (it'll happen on its own) vs. the personal catch-all `பாத்துக்கறேன்` (I'll see to it myself), and the boundary between them (clean single verb vs. compound).
- **Register:** grief/nostalgia
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** turn — the scene flips on a reveal partway through
- **Scenario Shape:** debrief
- **Location class:** extended_family
- **Energy:** low
- **Episode Form:** lore

## Scenario Context
A maama at the tea table runs through his worry-list — the wedding, his nerves, the too-spicy food, the general weight of things — and every single fret gets waved off with the same shrug: an -இடும் machine (சரியாயிடும், ஆயிடும், பழகிடும்). Except the very last worry, which gets answered differently: நான் பாத்துக்கறேன். That specimen is what Maya and Raj sit with for the rest of the episode — first as pure grammar (where does -இடும் come from, why does it only bolt onto a clean verb, what does பாத்துக்கறேன் do differently), then as something closer to home: Raj's own grandmother spoke almost exactly like the maama in the vignette, every evening of his childhood, until the last year of her life — when the shrug stopped working and she switched, for the first time, to the active promise. The turn lands there: the same two words carry a full generational handoff, and Raj discovers mid-conversation that he's the one saying it now.

## Word Payload

**NEW (2 words/frames — lore form: one or two told deep, not a survey):**
- **frame:idum** (*-ஆயிடும் / -இடும்*) — {verb}+ஆகிடும்/-இடும் → it'll {verb} itself / it's-handled — the fated, hands-off future-completive: சரியாயிடும், ஆயிடும், பழகிடும், புக் பண்ணிடும், சொல்லிடும், போயிடும். Bolts onto a clean single verb; does NOT bolt onto a compound (கூட்டிட்டு வரோ) — that boundary is a chat-mined lesson from 2026-07-13, worth stating explicitly.
- **பாத்துக்கறேன்** (*paathukkaren*) — I'll take care of it / I'll see to it myself — the catch-all handled-tag for exactly the compound cases -இடும் can't reach. Built on பார் (see/look) + த்துக்க (do-for-oneself, the same shape as the EAR-ONLY `frame:myself-thukka` already in the deck) + கிறேன்/கறேன். Fresh-seeded 2026-07-13, cold-pending — this episode is its second exposure.
