# Tier 2, Mission 14 — The Cost of Bluffing

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The "Repair" vs. The "Bluff" (Social Strategy)
- **Register:** defiance (The protagonist's struggle against a bureaucratic wall)
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** turn (The scene resets/replays to reveal how repair changes the outcome)
- **Scenario Shape:** callback_heavy (Echoing the real-world slip: appadiyaa vs. actual comprehension)
- **Location class:** office
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** narrated_drama

## Scenario Context
You are standing in a crowded, noisy government office. You need to know the specific date to return for your permit. The clerk behind the glass is speaking at lightning speed, using gossip-style openers to talk about his colleagues while burying the one piece of information you need. In the first half, you bluff with "appadiyaa" to look like a pro, but you pay the price. In the second half, you defy the social pressure to "fit in" and force the repair.

## Notes
Andrew's live slip (2026-08-15) shows he uses "appadiyaa" to mask a lack of understanding. This narrated drama explicitly punishes the bluff. The Narrator must emphasize the anxiety of the bluff vs. the clarity of the repair. The "youknow-la" frame is the trigger; when he hears the clerk start a gossip-train with it, he should be prompted to choose: bluff or repair?
