# 🎬 MASTER LESSON PLAN

## 1. THE COMMISSION & SCENE SPEC
**Payload**: `குடுங்க` (Give) - pointing at THEM to pass items.
**Restriction (Twin Law)**: `வேணும்` (Want) is STRICTLY BANNED. No character announces a need.
**Scene Seed**: A crowded table mid-meal. Everything needed is in someone else's reach, forcing the `குடுங்க` ask.
**Form**: Narrated Drama
**Register**: Pride
**Ingredient**: Character with a specific obsession.

## 2. THE CAST & INGREDIENT
**The Character**: *Athai* (Paternal Aunt). She runs the family dining table like a royal court. 
**Her Pride**: Her fiery cooking and legendary hospitality.
**Her Obsession (Tic)**: The number five. She serves everything in exact portions of five and takes it as a personal insult if you reject her heavily spiced portions. 

## 3. VOCABULARY INTEGRATION
**The Payload (Under Load)**:
- கொஞ்சம் தண்ணி குடுங்க
- கொஞ்சம் உப்பு குடுங்க
- அந்த பெரிய பெட்டி குடுங்க

**Focus Set Integrated**:
- அத்தை (Aunt)
- எழுந்தேன் (I woke up)
- காரம் (Spicy)
- பெரிய (Big)
- ஆனா (But)
- கேட்டாங்க (They asked)
- சொல்றேன் (I'm telling)
- அதுக்கு அப்புறம் (After that)
- நாங்க (We - exclusive)
- வெளிய (Outside)

**Callbacks Integrated**:
- உனக்கு (To you)
- அஞ்சு (Five)
- வேண்டாம் (I don't want)
- பிடிக்காது (I don't like)
- நேரா (Straight)

## 4. THE SCRIPT (NARRATED DRAMA)

*(The fourth wall stays up. The Host narrates the story in English, slipping into Tamil dialogue seamlessly.)*

**NARRATOR**
Dinner with my paternal aunt—my **அத்தை**—is less of a meal and more of a royal audience. She runs the dining table with absolute pride and authority. Her bizarre obsession? She always serves exactly five of everything, and she takes personal offense if you don't eat every single bite. 

This morning, I woke up late. 

**PROTAGONIST**
**நான் மெதுவா எழுந்தேன்.** *(I woke up slowly.)*

**NARRATOR**
I walked outside to join the family.

**PROTAGONIST**
**நான் வெளிய போனேன்.** *(I went outside.)*

**NARRATOR**
The table was enormous and chaotic, packed with serving dishes. My **அத்தை** was presiding at the head of the table. 

**AUNT**
**நாங்க நிறைய சமைச்சோம்! சாப்பிடுங்க.** *(We cooked a lot! Eat.)*

**NARRATOR**
Everything I needed was marooned at the far end of the table. I couldn't just reach across, and I definitely couldn't just announce what I wanted—that's rude here. I had to ask them to pass things. First, I needed water, which was near my brother.

**PROTAGONIST**
**கொஞ்சம் தண்ணி குடுங்க.** *(Please give a little water.)*

**NARRATOR**
He slid it over. Next, my food was missing salt. I looked at my sister. 

**PROTAGONIST**
**கொஞ்சம் உப்பு குடுங்க.** *(Please give a little salt.)*

**NARRATOR**
My aunt noticed. Her culinary pride was stung. She leaned in, eyes narrowing. They asked me what was wrong. 

**PROTAGONIST**
**அவங்க கேட்டாங்க.** *(They asked.)*

**NARRATOR**
I had to answer carefully. She loves it when people praise her bold flavors.

**PROTAGONIST**
**காரம் ஜாஸ்தி... ஆனா ரொம்ப நல்லா இருக்கு.** *(Spicy is too much... but very good.)*

**NARRATOR**
She beamed, satisfied. But then she noticed I hadn't taken the main dish. She pointed to a massive serving container at the end of the table.

**AUNT**
**உனக்கு அஞ்சு இருக்கு!** *(For you, there are five!)*

**NARRATOR**
I was already stuffed, and her food was fiery red. I needed to get the container away from her reach so she wouldn't force it onto my plate.

**PROTAGONIST**
**அந்த பெரிய பெட்டி குடுங்க.** *(Give that big box.)*

**NARRATOR**
I took it from my uncle, but served myself none. I had to face her and refuse the food directly. 

**PROTAGONIST**
**வேண்டாம். வயிறு ஃபுல். எனக்கு ரொம்ப காரம் பிடிக்காது.** *(I don't want. Stomach full. I don't like very spicy.)*

**NARRATOR**
A hush fell over the table. To my **அத்தை**, refusing her food is treason. I am telling you, the tension was thick.

**PROTAGONIST**
**நான் சொல்றேன்... கோவம் ஜாஸ்தி!** *(I'm telling you... the anger was too much!)*

**NARRATOR**
I decided to make a quick exit before she could escalate the situation. 

**PROTAGONIST**
**அதுக்கு அப்புறம்... நான் நேரா வெளிய போனேன்.** *(After that... I went straight outside.)*
