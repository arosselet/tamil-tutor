# Tier 2, Mission 14 — The Uncle's Secret

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Present-Future Toggle (-உறேன் vs -வேன்)
- **Register:** mischief
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** subtext — two people want opposite things under polite words
- **Scenario Shape:** gossip
- **Location class:** extended_family
- **Energy:** low
- **Episode Form:** lore

## Scenario Context
Two relatives are quietly discussing family history and an upcoming event involving the maternal uncle (மாமா). Under the polite exchange, they are mischievously trying to extract information from each other without giving their own plans away. The conversation heavily relies on the present-future toggle ("I am telling you now, but what will I say later?") and hearsay markers to gossip about what was said versus what is actually happening. It's a deep dive into family lore laced with polite suspicion and hidden agendas.

## Word Payload
