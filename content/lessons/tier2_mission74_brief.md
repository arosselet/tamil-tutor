# Tier 2, Mission 69 — The Departure Trap

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The Need-to-Go / Hurry frames
- **Register:** mischief
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** character — a vivid, specific person — a tic, an obsession, a lie
- **Scenario Shape:** gossip
- **Location class:** extended_family
- **Energy:** medium-loud
- **Episode Form:** lore

## Scenario Context
Maya and Raj dive into the legendary family lore of the "Indian Auntie Departure Trap." It focuses on a specific, vivid relative whose sole obsession is feeding you just one more bite when you're desperately trying to leave. You are running late for your train, your bags are packed, and she is physically blocking the door with an idli. The tension is mischievous: it's a comedic battle of wills where you must fiercely deploy "I'm in a hurry" and "I need to go" against her relentless, sweet-sounding trap of "eat a little more."

## Word Payload

**NEW (5 words):**
- **frame:needtogo-place** (*{place}-kku poganum*) — (I) need to go to {place}
- **அவசரம் இருக்கு** (*avasaram irukku*) — I'm in a hurry
- **frame:adverb-aa** (*{X}-aa*) — 'X-ly / as X' — one machine turns anything into an adverb
- **இன்னும் கொஞ்சம் சாப்பிடுங்க** (*innum konjam saapidunga*) — Eat a little more (HER line — hear it coming) [EAR ONLY]
- **frame:youknow-la** (*namma {person} irukkaanla...*) — 'you know our {person}, right…' — THE gossip opener [EAR ONLY]

**CALLBACKS (4 words):**
