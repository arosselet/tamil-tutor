# Tier 2, Mission 97 — The Call Paati Makes

## Core Targets
- **Linguistic Pattern:** The -ஆச்சு completive family (the "it's happened / it's-done" marker — enna aachu, evlo aachu, mudinjaachu, late aachu)
- **Register:** Mischief
- **Dramatic Ingredient:** Stakes — something real is on the line, not just a chore
- **Scenario Shape:** Dispute (a quiet power-move over money and truth, not a shouting match)
- **Location class:** Kitchen
- **Energy:** Low — Paati stays pleasant and unhurried throughout; the tension is interior, which is the joke
- **Episode Form:** Phone_call (naturalistic call; light Breakdown)

## Scenario Context
Dinner is winding down at the kitchen table — Paati, Mama, Karthi, and Deepa still sitting around the plates. Paati picks up the phone to call Athai, who is out running birthday errands, and announces breezily that whatever leftover money (மீதி) is sitting around should go toward something a little nicer for her own lunch — she already knows exactly how much there is, and says so without accusing anyone. Karthi, who knows there is no real மீதி left to speak of, has to decide whether to let it ride or step in before Athai hears the wrong number. Deepa keeps grabbing for the phone to say hi, and every interruption raises the stakes a notch. Mama hovers between smoothing it over and not wanting to learn exactly how much his son spent. Paati never once accuses anyone — she doesn't have to. Nothing resolves; the call ends, the money question stays open, and everyone at the table knows a little more than they did five minutes ago.

- **நான் கன்ஃபர்ம் பண்றேன்** (*naan confirm panren*) — I'll confirm

**CALLBACKS (4 words — the commissioned soak-order payload, landed in M96, due for cold resurfacing by ear in a changed scene):**

## Vocabulary Fence (the sea — build from these)

- frame:adverb-aa — {X}+ஆ → 'X-ly / as X' — நல்லா, மெதுவா, தண்ணியா, இன்ஜினியரா இருக்கேன்: one machine turns anything into an adverb
- frame:cant-முடியல — {verb} முடியல → can't {verb} — தூக்க முடியல, போக முடியல
- frame:day-recap — {place} போனோம், ரொம்ப நல்லா இருந்துச்சு → we went to {place}, it was great (the nightly 'inniki enna panninga?' answer)
- frame:done-ittu — {verb}+இட்டு/‑ட்டு → done-and-dusted / off-I-go — வந்துட்டேன், கழுவிட்டு வரேன், விட்டுடு, ஆயிட்டேன்: the completive already inside 7 deck items
- frame:giveme-noun — {noun} குடுங்க → give me {noun} (e.g. 'oru tea kudunga')
- frame:happened-aachu — {V}+ச்சு / ஆச்சு → 'it happened / it's become' — ஆச்சு, போச்சு, முடிஞ்சுச்சு, நேரம் ஆச்சு: the it-verb the pool is already full of
- frame:hearsay-aam — …-ஆம் → 'apparently / they say' — the hearsay tag gossip runs on (varaanga-laam = apparently they're coming) (EAR ONLY)
- frame:howmuch-noun — {noun} எவ்ளோ? → how much is {noun}? (e.g. 'idhu evlo?' — Oracle: always the contracted 'evlo')
- frame:idum — {verb}+ஆகிடும்/-இடும் → it'll {verb} itself / it's-handled — the fated, hands-off future-completive: சரியாயிடும், முடிஞ்சிடும், போயிடும், புக் பண்ணிடும் — bolts onto a clean single verb, not a compound (use பாத்துக்கறேன் there instead)
- frame:if-ndhaa — {verb}+ந்தா / இல்லன்னா → 'if…' — வாய் திறந்தா…, இல்லன்னா என்ன ஆகும்?: the conditional
- frame:in-la — {noun}+ல → in/at/on — கனடாவுல, தமிழ்ல, வீட்ல, போன்ல: the everywhere locative
- frame:isthere-noun — {noun} இருக்கா? → is there {noun}? (e.g. 'coffee இருக்கா?')
- frame:mayi-laama — நான் {verb}-லாமா? → may I {verb}? (permission, e.g. 'ukkaralaama?')
- frame:nearby-noun — பக்கத்துல {noun} இருக்கா? → is there {noun} nearby?
- frame:needtogo-place — {place}-க்கு போகணும் → (I) need to go to {place}
- frame:negative-la — {verb-stem}+ல → didn't / doesn't / can't — புரியல, முடியல, தெரியல, கேட்கல, பண்ணல: the everyday spoken negative (verb + illa, contracted). He already owns puriyala + mudiyala as separate chunks; this is the one machine under them.
- frame:obligation-ணும் — {verb}+ணும் → must {verb} — பண்ணணும், போகணும், சொல்லணும்
- frame:polite-nga — {verb}+ங்க → the polite ask — சொல்லுங்க, குடுங்க, உக்காருங்க, வாங்க: one machine, every verb he owns
- frame:present-future-toggle — -உறேன் (now) vs -வேன் (later) on any verb — போறேன்/போவேன், கேட்குறேன்/கேட்பேன்
- frame:quote-nu — …-னு சொன்னாங்க → 'she said that…' — the quotative -னு; every reported line in gossip hangs on it (EAR ONLY)
- frame:spice-dhaan — the sass clitics: ‑தான் 'just/exactly', ‑ஏ '-self/very', ‑உம் 'even/any', ‑ஓ 'some-or-other' — நேத்து தான், நானே, ஒன்னும், என்னமோ (EAR ONLY)
- frame:want-noun — {English noun} வேணும் → I want {noun} (e.g. 'auto வேணும்')
- frame:we-om — {I-form}+ஓம் → we — வந்தோம், போனோம், இருப்போம், சாப்பிட்டோம்: the trip is a 'we' trip
- frame:where-noun — {noun} எங்க? → where is {noun}? (e.g. 'bus stop எங்க?')
- frame:youknow-la — நம்ம {person} இருக்கான்ல… → 'you know our {person}, right…' — THE gossip opener; the -ல tag means 'right?' (EAR ONLY, Oracle-confirmed)

## Notes

- **Full honorific for Paati, both directions, no exception** — this is her entire voice per the canon; a bare அவ/சொல்லுவா toward her is a defect, not a register choice (M95's own fix log).
- **No -ங்க on any first-person verb** (`nga-has-no-address`, live slip) — Karthi and Deepa keep their own first-person forms untouched (இருக்கேன், தெரியும்) even under pressure.
- **Watch Karthi's noun-exit** (`noun-for-verb`) — if he gets a cornered line, the scene should make the verb do the work, not let a noun absorb it.
- **Tamil script only for names** — பாட்டி, மாமா, தீபா, கார்த்தி, அத்தை never appear as bare English transliteration (M95/M96 dialect-fix precedent).
- **Never target his decade-old core words** — venum/venaam/podhum stay out of this episode's teaching surface entirely, per the commission's own exclusion.
- **Payload fidelity:** மீதி, அப்போ, எல்லாம், ஆச்சு should survive as bare stems carrying only sentence-native suffixes — the same standard M96's Producer pass verified.
- **Athai is the called party, off-scene** — her voice may appear through the phone if the Architect wants a two-way call (her pin is already fixed in the canon), or she can stay implied if a one-sided call reads cleaner; either is legal for `phone_call` form.
- **Light Breakdown only** — this form doesn't need the full machinery; Maya and Raj can land the -ஆச்சு family and the phone_scheduling cluster quickly and get out.
