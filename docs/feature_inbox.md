Output per call is structured text: transcript as spoken, romanized line, meaning in English, deviations heard, confidence. The failure mode the bad takes exist to catch: LAUNDERING — if a deliberately wrong take transcribes identical to the good take, the ear is normalizing him, and its transcripts must never be treated as evidence of what he said.
